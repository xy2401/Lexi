> Reunion: Talk about annual events

## 单元讲解

本单元围绕"谈论年度聚会"展开。核心是聚会表达：It's time for our annual get-together!（到我们年度聚会的时间了！）、We haven't gotten together since graduation.（我们从毕业以来就没聚过。）、Let me introduce a friend of mine.（让我介绍一下我的一个朋友。）、What's up? Long time no see!（怎么了？好久不见！）。

词表里有社交词汇（get together, hang out, introduce, in contact）和外貌词汇（bald, curly, outgoing）。你可以练习：He's given up smoking. / She's a vegetarian now. / We hung out all night. / It's a shame you can't come.

get together 表示"聚会"。现在完成时（have gotten together, have given up）表示从过去到现在的经历。

## Tips

- **get together / gotten together** — 聚会。搭配：get together, have gotten together, a get-together。例句：Let's get together this weekend! / We haven't gotten together in years. 注意 get-together（名词）有连字符。

- **annual / graduation** — 每年的 / 毕业。搭配：an annual event, annual reunion, since graduation。例句：It's our annual reunion. / We haven't met since graduation. 注意 annual = once a year。

- **give up / given up** — 放弃。搭配：give up smoking, given up, give up on。例句：He's given up smoking. / Don't give up! 注意 give up + 动名词（give up smoking）。

- **hang out / hung out** — 出去玩。搭配：hang out with friends, hung out all day。例句：Let's hang out this weekend. / We hung out at the party. 注意 hang out 是口语（非正式）。

- **introduce / of mine** — 介绍 / 我的一个。搭配：introduce a friend, a friend of mine。例句：Let me introduce a friend of mine. / She's a colleague of mine. 注意 a friend of mine = one of my friends。

- **in contact / it's time** — 保持联系 / 该……了。搭配：stay in contact, keep in contact, It's time to...。例句：We're still in contact. / It's time to go! 注意 in contact = in touch。

- **bald / curly / outgoing** — 秃头的 / 卷发的 / 外向的。搭配：go bald, curly hair, very outgoing。例句：He's gone bald! / She has curly hair. / He's very outgoing. 注意 outgoing 形容性格（外向）。

- **vegetarian / cheers / hug** — 素食者 / 干杯 / 拥抱。搭配：a vegetarian, Cheers! / give a hug。例句：I'm a vegetarian now. / Cheers! To our reunion! / She gave me a big hug. 注意 Cheers 用于祝酒。

- **what's up / a shame** — 怎么了 / 遗憾。搭配：What's up? / It's a shame that...。例句：Hey, what's up? / It's a shame you can't come. 注意 What's up? 是打招呼（= How's it going?）。

- **except that / honey / dj** — 除了 / 亲爱的 / DJ。搭配：except that..., honey, the DJ。例句：Everything was great except that it rained. / Come on, honey! / The DJ played great music. 注意 except that + 从句。

- **visit / outgoing / introduce** — 拜访 / 外向 / 介绍。搭配：visit a friend, an outgoing person, introduce yourself。例句：I visited an old friend. / She's very outgoing. / Introduce yourself to the group.

- **get together / hang out / in contact** — 聚会 / 玩 / 联系。搭配：get together often, hang out on weekends, stay in contact。例句：We should get together more often. / We hung out after the party. / Let's stay in contact!
