## 单元讲解

本单元的主题是"解释工会的作用"，场景是回顾百年社会变迁。词表可以分成四组：对比与列举（as opposed to, compared with, in contrast, unlike, likewise, those, one of the）、社会议题（union, strike, equality, gender, prevention, nationwide）、变迁与方式（decade, over, breakthrough, method, lifestyle, long-term）、以及城乡与具体事物（rural, urban, blender, pros and cons）。拼起来就是一段社会观察：Compared with a century ago, life in urban and rural areas has changed in every decade. Unions organized strikes for equality, and likewise, new methods improved prevention and long-term safety.

语法主线有两个。一是对比表达：compared with...（与……相比）、as opposed to...（而不是……）、unlike...（不像……）、in contrast（相比之下）、likewise（同样地），这是议论文与历史叙述的核心工具。二是名词短语的构建：one of the + 复数名词（……之一）、those 指代前面提过的同类事物。

学完这个单元，你能用英语对比今昔、讨论社会进步，还能评价事物的利弊。

## Tips

- **compared with / as opposed to / unlike / in contrast** — 四组对比表达。Compared with 1920, wages are higher. as opposed to = 而不是：by train, as opposed to by car. unlike + 名词：Unlike his father, he works in a city. In contrast 单独使用，引出对比：In contrast, rural life changed slowly.

- **likewise / those / one of the** — likewise = 同样地、也如此：She agreed; likewise, her team did. those 指代同类复数名词：the jobs of those days；one of the + 复数名词：one of the biggest changes。

- **union / strike / equality / gender** — union = 工会、联盟；strike = 罢工（也可指击打）；equality = 平等：gender equality（性别平等）；gender = 性别。

- **nationwide / prevention / breakthrough / method** — nationwide = 全国范围的：a nationwide strike；prevention = 预防：disease prevention；breakthrough = 突破（break + through）；method = 方法：a new teaching method。

- **decade / over / long-term / lifestyle** — decade = 十年；over 表示"超过、贯穿"：over a hundred years；long-term = 长期的（连字符复合形容词）；lifestyle = 生活方式（life + style）。

- **rural / urban** — rural = 乡村的；urban = 城市的，两者常成对出现：urban and rural areas。

- **blender / pros and cons** — blender = 搅拌机（厨房电器），用来说明百年间家用电器的变迁；pros and cons = 利与弊：weigh the pros and cons。

1. **中文**：与一百年前相比，生活变了样。
   **英文**：Compared with a century ago, life has changed.
   > **解析**：Compared with... 是过去分词短语作状语；has changed 是现在完成时。

2. **中文**：人们坐火车出行，而不是坐马车。
   **英文**：People traveled by train as opposed to by horse.
   > **解析**：as opposed to 表示"而不是"，连接两个并列的出行方式。

3. **中文**：不像过去，如今人人都用搅拌机。
   **英文**：Unlike the past, everyone uses a blender now.
   > **解析**：Unlike + 名词作对比状语；uses 是单三形式。

4. **中文**：相比之下，乡村的变化更慢。
   **英文**：In contrast, rural areas changed more slowly.
   > **解析**：In contrast 置于句首引出对比；changed 是过去式。

5. **中文**：同样地，城市也建起了学校。
   **英文**：Likewise, urban cities built new schools.
   > **解析**：Likewise 是副词"同样地"，连接并列论述；built 是过去式。

6. **中文**：工会为全国工人组织了罢工。
   **英文**：The union organized a nationwide strike.
   > **解析**：nationwide 修饰 strike 作定语；organized 是过去式。

7. **中文**：他们为性别平等而战。
   **英文**：They fought for gender equality.
   > **解析**：gender equality 是固定搭配"性别平等"；fought 是 fight 的过去式。

8. **中文**：这是医学上的一次重大突破。
   **英文**：This was a major breakthrough in medicine.
   > **解析**：breakthrough 是复合名词"突破"；a major breakthrough 作表语。

9. **中文**：新方法改善了疾病预防。
   **英文**：The new method improved disease prevention.
   > **解析**：method 是"方法"；improved 是过去式，prevention 作宾语。

10. **中文**：这是近几十年来最大的变化之一。
   **英文**：It is one of the biggest changes in decades.
   > **解析**：one of the + 最高级 + 复数名词；in decades 表时间范围。

11. **中文**：那些日子里，人们过着简单的生活。
   **英文**：In those days, people lived a simple life.
   > **解析**：those 指代过去的日子；lived 是过去式。

12. **中文**：他们的生活方式在一个十年里彻底改变。
   **英文**：Their lifestyle changed completely in one decade.
   > **解析**：lifestyle 是复合名词"生活方式"；in one decade 表时长。

13. **中文**：这是一项长期的承诺。
   **英文**：This is a long-term commitment.
   > **解析**：long-term 是带连字符的复合形容词，修饰 commitment。

14. **中文**：这场运动持续了一百多年。
   **英文**：The movement lasted over one hundred years.
   > **解析**：over 表示"超过"；lasted 是过去式。

15. **中文**：我们来权衡一下利弊。
   **英文**：Let's weigh the pros and cons.
   > **解析**：pros and cons 是固定表达"利与弊"；weigh 表示权衡。

16. **中文**：罢工在全国范围内蔓延。
   **英文**：The strike spread nationwide.
   > **解析**：nationwide 作副词修饰 spread；spread 过去式同形。

17. **中文**：城市工作机会比乡村多。
   **英文**：Urban areas offer more jobs than rural ones.
   > **解析**：than 引导比较；ones 替代 areas 避免重复。

18. **中文**：预防比治疗更重要。
   **英文**：Prevention is more important than treatment.
   > **解析**：Prevention 作主语；more important than 是比较级结构。

19. **中文**：工人们加入了工会。
   **英文**：The workers joined a union.
   > **解析**：join a union 是固定搭配"加入工会"；joined 是过去式。

20. **中文**：这一代人见证了科技的突破。
   **英文**：This generation witnessed technological breakthroughs.
   > **解析**：witnessed 是过去式"见证"；breakthroughs 是复数。

### 1

- [x] Compared with

- [ ] Compare with

> **解析**：句首需要过去分词短语 Compared with 作状语；Compare with 是原形不能作状语。

### 2

- [x] to
- [ ] of
- [ ] with

> **解析**：as opposed to 是固定搭配"而不是"；其余介词不合用法。

### 3

____ his classmates, he finished early.

- [x] Unlike
- [ ] Unliking
- [ ] Unliked

### 4

- [ ] In contrasts
- [ ] In the contrast of

### 5
