# Unit 074: 在家里 3

> 描述家居用品

## 单词

at the back, at the front, available, build, change, couch, downstairs, dresser, for sale, garage, his, inside, made, mirror, one of, ours, paint, picture of, rent, shampoo, shelf, shelves, single, sink, size, soap, theirs, them, they, to, towel, upstairs, which, wooden

## 单元讲解

本单元围绕"描述家居用品"展开。词表里有家具（couch, dresser, mirror, shelf, sink）和位置词（downstairs, upstairs, at the back, at the front, inside）。你可以练习：The couch is downstairs. / The mirror is on the wall. / There are towels on the shelf. / The house is for sale. / Which size do you need?

名词性物主代词继续出现：ours, theirs。shelf 的复数是 shelves（不规则）。for sale 表示"出售"。rent 表示"租"。

wooden 表示"木制的"。made 表示"制作的"。

## Tips

- **downstairs / upstairs** — 楼下 / 楼上。搭配：go downstairs, go upstairs, the bathroom is upstairs。例句：The living room is downstairs. / My bedroom is upstairs. 注意它们既是副词也是形容词。

- **couch** — 沙发（美式）。搭配：sit on the couch, a leather couch。注意美式 couch = 英式 sofa。couch 通常指长沙发（可躺），sofa 更正式。

- **shelf / shelves** — 架子。复数不规则：shelf → shelves（-f 变 -ves）。搭配：on the shelf, a book shelf, put it on the shelf。类似的：knife → knives, wife → wives。

- **for sale** — 出售。搭配：a house for sale, for sale sign, put it up for sale。例句：This house is for sale. / Are these for sale? 注意 for sale（待售）和 on sale（打折）不同。

- **rent** — 租。搭配：rent an apartment, pay the rent, for rent（出租）。例句：We rent this apartment. / How much is the rent? 注意 rent 既是动词也是名词。

- **mirror** — 镜子。搭配：look in the mirror, a bathroom mirror, a full-length mirror。例句：There is a mirror in the bathroom. / She looked in the mirror.

- **sink** — 水槽。搭配：the kitchen sink, the bathroom sink, wash in the sink。例句：The sink is full of dishes. / Put the soap by the sink. 注意 sink 也可以作动词（下沉）。

- **ours / theirs** — 我们的 / 他们的（名词性物主代词）。搭配：This house is ours. / The garage is theirs. 注意代替"our + 名词"：This couch is ours. = This is our couch.

- **wooden / made** — 木制的 / 制作的。搭配：a wooden shelf, made of wood, made in China。例句：The dresser is wooden. / It's made of wood. 注意 made of（看得出材料）vs made from（看不出）。

- **at the back / at the front** — 在后面 / 在前面。搭配：at the back of the house, at the front。例句：The garage is at the back. / The garden is at the front. 注意 back 和 front 前加 the。

- **shampoo / soap / towel** — 洗发水 / 肥皂 / 毛巾。搭配：a bottle of shampoo, a bar of soap, a clean towel。注意 shampoo 不可数（a bottle of shampoo）。soap 通常不可数（a bar of soap）。

- **which / size** — 哪个 / 尺寸。搭配：Which size? / What size? / a single size。例句：Which size do you need? / What size is the mirror? 注意 size 的常见搭配：What size are you?
