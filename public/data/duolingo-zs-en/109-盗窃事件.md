## 单元讲解

本单元围绕"讲述一次盗窃事件"展开。核心是犯罪相关表达：Someone broke in and stole my chain!（有人闯入偷了我的链子！）、The thief was caught and sent to prison.（小偷被抓住送进了监狱。）、The robber grabbed something and ran.（劫匪抓了东西就跑了。）、I found out from the news headline.（我从新闻标题上得知的。）。

词表里有犯罪词汇（rob, thief, robber, stolen, prison, caught）和叙事词汇（immediately, grabbed, ran, headline）。你可以练习：Oh my god—my things are gone! / The robber had a thick chain on. / Call the police as soon as possible! / He was caught around the front of the building.

rob 的对象是地点/人（rob a bank），steal 的对象是物品（steal money）。

## Tips

- **rob / steal / stolen** — 抢劫 / 偷。搭配：rob a bank, rob someone, steal money, stolen goods。例句：They robbed the bank. / Someone stole my wallet. / My bike was stolen. 注意 rob + 地点/人，steal + 物品。

- **thief / robber** — 小偷 / 劫匪。搭配：a thief stole, a robber robbed, catch the thief。例句：A thief stole my phone. / The robber was caught. 注意 thief 偏"偷"，robber 偏"抢"（有暴力）。

- **caught / prison** — 被抓住 / 监狱。搭配：caught the thief, sent to prison, go to prison。例句：The police caught the robber. / He was sent to prison. 注意 catch → caught → caught。

- **broke / break** — 闯入。搭配：break in, broke into the house。例句：Someone broke into my house. / They broke in at night. 注意 break in（不及物），break into + 地点（及物）。

- **grabbed / ran / run** — 抓取 / 跑了。搭配：grabbed the bag, ran away, run for it。例句：The thief grabbed my bag and ran. / He ran as fast as he could. 注意 grab 强调"猛地抓住"。

- **as soon as possible / immediately** — 尽快 / 立刻。搭配：as soon as possible (ASAP), do it immediately。例句：Call the police as soon as possible! / I called immediately. 注意 ASAP 是缩写（口语/短信常用）。

- **headline / news** — 新闻标题 / 新闻。搭配：the headline said, in the news, a news headline。例句：I saw it in the headline. / It was all over the news. 注意 headline 是报纸/新闻的大标题。

- **had on / thick / chain** — 穿着（戴着）/ 粗的 / 链子。搭配：had on a coat, a thick chain, a gold chain。例句：The robber had a thick gold chain on. / He was wearing a thick jacket. 注意 have on = wear（穿着/戴着）。

- **gone / found** — 不见了 / 发现。搭配：My things are gone! / found the stolen items。例句：My wallet is gone! / The police found the stolen goods. 注意 gone 表示"消失了/不见了"。

- **oh my god / reason** — 天哪 / 原因。搭配：Oh my god! / for no reason, for some reason。例句：Oh my god—my bag is gone! / There's no reason for this. 注意 Oh my god 表示惊讶/震惊。

- **as well as / front** — 以及 / 前面。搭配：as well as, in front of, the front door。例句：They stole my phone as well as my wallet. / He was at the front of the building. 注意 as well as = and（以及）。

- **million / movies / fun** — 百万 / 电影 / 有趣。搭配：a million dollars, at the movies, for fun。例句：They stole a million dollars! / We were at the movies. / I did it for fun.

<quiz-sentence-builder>

1. **中文**：他向警察报案称自己的钱包在公交车上被盗了。  
   **英文**：He reported to the police that his wallet was stolen on the bus.
   > **解析**：stolen 为 steal 的过去分词，在被动语态 was stolen 中表示“被盗”。

2. **中文**：小偷抢走了她的手提包，逃进了一条狭窄的小巷。  
   **英文**：The thief snatched her handbag and ran into a narrow alley for around.
   > **解析**：thief 作可数名词，意为“小偷，窃贼”。在句中作主语。

3. **中文**：嫌疑人在商店出口附近被保安抓获。  
   **英文**：The suspect was caught by security guards near the store exit (as soon as possible).
   > **解析**：caught 为 catch 的过去式/过去分词，was caught 表示“被抓获/逮捕”。

4. **中文**：如果你注意到可疑行为，请立即拨打紧急热线。  
   **英文**：Call the emergency hotline immediately if you notice suspicious behavior (as well as).
   > **解析**：immediately 作副词，意为“立即，马上”，在句中作状语修饰动词 call。

5. **中文**：那起惊人的银行劫案成为了早间新闻的头条新闻。  
   **英文**：The dramatic bank robbery became the top headline on morning news for break.
   > **解析**：headline 作名词，意为“头条新闻，大字标题”。top headline 表示“最大头条”。

6. **中文**：如果钥匙丢失，请尽快通知酒店前台。  
   **英文**：Please inform the hotel reception as soon as possible if your key is lost for caught.
   > **解析**：as soon as possible 为固定短语，意为“尽可能快地，尽快”。

7. **中文**：当她回到停车位时，她的自行车不见了。  
   **英文**：When she returned to her parking spot, her bicycle was gone for chain.
   > **解析**：gone 作形容词，意为“离去的，不见了的”，在句中作表语。

8. **中文**：窃贼夜间从后窗破门侵入了这家电子商店。  
   **英文**：Burglars broke into the electronic shop through the back window at night for found.
   > **解析**：break into (过去式 broke into) 意为“强行闯入，破门而入”。

9. **中文**：劫匪从展示柜中抢走了钻石项链。  
   **英文**：The robber grabbed the diamond necklace from the display case for front.
   > **解析**：grab (过去式 grabbed) 作及物动词，意为“一把抓起，抢走”。

10. **中文**：犯罪分子被法官判处五年监禁。  
   **英文**：The criminal was sentenced by the judge to five years in prison for fun.
   > **解析**：prison 作名词，意为“监狱”。in prison 为固定短语，意为“服刑/在狱中”。
