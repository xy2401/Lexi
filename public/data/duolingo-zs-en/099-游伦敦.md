## 单元讲解

本单元围绕"制定度假计划"展开。核心是伦敦旅游表达：Take the Tube to the British Museum.（坐地铁去大英博物馆。）、The walking tour starts at ten.（步行游览十点开始。）、How many pounds is the ticket?（票多少英镑？）、Cross the bridge and turn left.（过桥左转。）。

词表里有交通词汇（tube, tram, lift, take off）和旅游词汇（tour, guide, visitor, walking tour, two-day）。你可以练习：Today's tour includes the British Museum. / The crowd was huge at the square. / I'm a first-time visitor. / The lift is on the ground floor.

tube 是伦敦地铁的俗称。pound 是英国货币单位。

## Tips

- **tube / tram** — 地铁 / 有轨电车。搭配：take the Tube, on the Tube, by tram。例句：Take the Tube to central London. / The tram stops here. 注意 Tube（大写）特指伦敦地铁，tram 是有轨电车。

- **british museum / tour** — 大英博物馆 / 游览。搭配：visit the British Museum, a guided tour, a walking tour。例句：The tour includes the British Museum. / We went on a walking tour. 注意专有名词首字母大写。

- **guide / visitor** — 导游 / 游客。搭配：a tour guide, a visitor, a first-time visitor。例句：The guide showed us around. / I'm a first-time visitor to London. 注意 visitor 比 tourist 更正式。

- **pound** — 英镑。搭配：ten pounds, How many pounds? / five-pound note。例句：It costs twenty pounds. / How many pounds is the ticket? 注意 pound 的符号是 £。

- **bridge / cross** — 桥 / 过（桥）。搭配：cross the bridge, London Bridge, Tower Bridge。例句：Cross the bridge and you'll see the museum. / We walked across the bridge. 注意 cross 是动词，across 是介词。

- **square / crowd** — 广场 / 人群。搭配：Trafalgar Square, in the square, a big crowd。例句：The square was full of people. / There was a huge crowd. 注意 crowd 作名词表示"人群"。

- **two-day / including** — 两天的 / 包括。搭配：a two-day tour, including lunch, including the museum。例句：We booked a two-day tour. / The price includes lunch. 注意 two-day 有连字符（形容词），two days 无连字符（名词）。

- **take off / come back** — 起飞 / 回来。搭配：The plane takes off at..., come back home。例句：Our flight takes off at six. / When do you come back? 注意 take off 的主语通常是飞机/航班。

- **lift / ground** — 电梯 / 地面。搭配：take the lift, on the ground floor, ground level。例句：Take the lift to the third floor. / The shop is on the ground floor. 注意 lift 是英式（美式 elevator），ground floor 是英式一楼。

- **supper / today's** — 晚餐 / 今天的。搭配：have supper, today's tour, today's plan。例句：Let's have supper after the tour. / Today's tour was amazing. 注意 supper 较正式，日常多用 dinner。

- **air / reason** — 空气 / 原因。搭配：fresh air, in the open air, for a reason。例句：Let's get some fresh air. / There's a reason for the crowd. 注意 for a reason = 有原因的。

- **called / so / with** — 叫做 / 所以 / 和。搭配：called..., so..., with a guide。例句：We visited a place called Greenwich. / It was crowded, so we left. / We went with a guide.

<quiz-sentence-builder>

1. **中文**：我们整个上午都在大英博物馆欣赏古代文物。  
   **英文**：We spent the whole morning admiring ancient artifacts at the British Museum.
   > **解析**：British Museum 为专有名词，英国伦敦“大英博物馆”。前接定冠词 the。

2. **中文**：步行穿过这座桥，可以欣赏到令人惊叹的江景。  
   **英文**：Walk across the bridge to get a wonderful view of the river.
   > **解析**：cross 作动词/介词，在 walk across 中 across 为介词，意为“穿过，跨越”。

3. **中文**：一大群人聚集在皇宫附近观看换岗仪式。  
   **英文**：A large crowd gathered near the palace to watch the guard changing ceremony.
   > **解析**：crowd 作可数名词，意为“人群”。a large crowd 指“一大群人”。

4. **中文**：乘坐红色双层巴士是游览伦敦的一种流行方式。  
   **英文**：Riding a red double-decker bus is a popular way to tour London (british museum).
   > **解析**：double-decker 作形容词/名词，意为“双层的（巴士）”。修饰 bus。

5. **中文**：伦敦有许多著名的观光景点，吸引了数百万游客。  
   **英文**：London has many famous sightseeing spots that attract millions of visitors.
   > **解析**：sightseeing 作名词/定语，意为“观光的，游览的”。sightseeing spots 指“观光景点”。

6. **中文**：我们计划在下雨之前返回酒店。  
   **英文**：We plan to come back to the hotel before the rain starts.
   > **解析**：come back 为动词短语，意为“回来，返回”。后接介词 to 表示“回到某地”。

7. **中文**：科文特花园是一个充满街头表演者和咖啡馆的热闹区域。  
   **英文**：Covent Garden is a bustling area filled with street performers and cafes.
   > **解析**：area 作名词，意为“区域，地区”。作表语修饰地名。

8. **中文**：出发前请仔细核对机票和酒店预订的每一个细节。  
   **英文**：Double-check every detail of your flight and hotel bookings before departure (come back).
   > **解析**：detail 作名词，意为“细节，详情”。every detail 表示“每一个细节”。

9. **中文**：咱们打一辆黑色出租车把我们沉重的行李运到火车站吧。  
   **英文**：Let's call a black cab to take our heavy luggage to the train station.
   > **解析**：call 作及物动词，意为“叫（车），打电话”。call a cab 表示“叫出租车”。

10. **中文**：当我们沿着河岸散步时，凉爽的夜空空气让人感觉非常爽快。  
   **英文**：The cool evening air was very refreshing as we strolled along the riverbank.
   > **解析**：air 作不可数名词，意为“空气”。在句中作主语。
