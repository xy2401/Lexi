# Unit 274: Travel 3

> Travel: Share your travel plans

## 单词

accommodations, along the way, alternate, connection, considerably, end up, in order to, intend to, it's time, look into, next, not my idea of, original, plan ahead, planning on, potentially, preference, roughly, seafood, sensible, slight, thinking of, turn around, was going to

## 单元讲解

本单元围绕"分享旅行计划"展开，但重点不是目的地本身，而是计划和决策的过程。词表里有大量表达意图和打算的短语：intend to / planning on / thinking of / was going to——它们从正式到随意排列，都可以用来谈计划。也有描述旅行安排的实用词汇：accommodations（住宿）、connection（转机/转车）、alternate（备选方案）、plan ahead（提前规划）。你可以练习这样的叙述：I'm planning on taking the train, but I was going to fly originally. / We need to look into accommodations and plan ahead—in order to get a sensible price, it's time to book. / If we end up in a coastal town, I'd love some seafood.

词表里有几个表达"变化和调整"的词值得注意：alternate（替代的）、turn around（掉头、转变）、end up（最终到达/变成）。旅行计划很少完全按预期走，这些词正好描述那种"计划赶不上变化"的状态。not my idea of 是一个有趣的口语表达，用来委婉地说"这不是我喜欢的类型"。

## Tips

- **planning on / thinking of / intend to / was going to** — 四个表达计划的短语，语气从随意到正式。I'm planning on staying three days.（我打算待三天。）I'm thinking of taking the train.（我在考虑坐火车。）I intend to book early.（我打算早点订——较正式。）I was going to fly, but...（我本来想飞的——暗示计划变了。）注意 planning on 和 thinking of 后接动名词，intend to 后接动词原形。

- **accommodations** — 住宿（常用复数）。比 hotel 更宽泛，包括酒店、民宿、青旅等一切住处。搭配：book accommodations / find accommodations / accommodations are expensive。美式英语常用复数 accommodations，英式更常说 accommodation（不可数）。

- **connection** — 这里指转机、转车。搭配：miss a connection / a tight connection / a connection in Chicago。例句：We have a connection in Denver—only 40 minutes, which is a bit tight. 注意 connection 也可以指"联系、关系"，看语境。

- **end up** — 最终到达、最终变成。搭配：end up in a place / end up doing something / end up lost。例句：We took a wrong turn and ended up in a small fishing village. 强调结果出乎意料，不是计划中的。

- **look into** — 调查、研究、了解。搭配：look into options / look into prices / look into the schedule。比 research 更口语化：I'll look into accommodations tonight. 注意和 look at（看）区分，look into 有"深入了解"的意味。

- **plan ahead** — 提前规划。搭配：plan ahead for the trip / you should plan ahead / plan ahead to save money。例句：If you plan ahead, you can get considerably cheaper flights. 反义：wing it（即兴、不做计划）。

- **not my idea of** — 不是我心目中的、不是我会选的。委婉表达不喜欢：Camping is not my idea of a vacation. / A 5 a.m. flight is not my idea of fun. 比 I don't like 更含蓄、更有个性。

- **considerably / roughly / slightly / potentially** — 四个副词。considerably = 相当地（程度大）：considerably cheaper。roughly = 大约：roughly three hours。slight 的副词形式 slightly = 略微：a slight delay。potentially = 潜在地：potentially dangerous。注意 slight 是形容词，slightly 是副词。

- **sensible** — 明智的、合理的。搭配：a sensible choice / a sensible plan / be sensible about money。注意不要和 sensitive（敏感的）混淆。sensible 强调理性判断，sensitive 强调感受敏锐。

- **in order to** — 为了。比单纯用 to 更正式、更强调目的：In order to save money, we booked early. 口语中常简化为 to，但写作中 in order to 更清晰。否定形式：in order not to。

- **preference** — 偏好、喜好。动词是 prefer。搭配：a strong preference / personal preference / have a preference for。例句：Do you have a preference for hotels or hostels? 注意拼写：pref-er-ence。

- **turn around** — 掉头、转变方向。搭配：turn around and go back / turn the car around。比喻义：turn things around（扭转局面）。例句：We missed the exit and had to turn around. 注意也可以写成 turnaround（名词，指转变）。

- **seafood** — 海鲜。sea（海）+ food（食物），不可数名词。搭配：fresh seafood / a seafood restaurant / I love seafood。注意 fish 可以指单个鱼或鱼肉，seafood 是各类海产的总称。

- **it's time** — 是时候了。搭配：It's time to book. / It's time we left. / It's about time! 注意 It's time + 过去式（It's time we went）是虚拟语气，暗示"早该做了"。
