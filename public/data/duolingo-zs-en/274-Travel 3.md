## 单元讲解

本单元的主题是"分享你的旅行计划"。词表可以分成四组：行程要素（accommodations, connection, alternate, original, next, along the way, turn around）、计划动词（plan ahead, intend to, planning on, thinking of, was going to, look into, it's time, end up）、程度与评价（considerably, potentially, roughly, slight, sensible, preference, not my idea of）、以及杂项（seafood, in order to）。拼起来就是一段出行规划：I'm planning on taking the next connection. It's time to look into accommodations.

语法主线是"计划表达"的四个层次：intend to（打算，较正式）→ planning on / thinking of（后接动名词，较口语）→ was going to（本来的打算）→ end up（最终结果，常出乎意料）。注意搭配差异：intend to do 但 plan on doing / think of doing，介词后接动名词是本单元的易错点。另一条主线是程度副词 considerably（大大地）、potentially（潜在地）、roughly（大约）、slight（轻微的），用来精确描述行程变动。

学完这单元，你能用英语规划一次旅行：谈住宿与转机、讨论备选方案、表达偏好，还能应对行程变化。

## Tips

- **accommodations / connection** — 行程两大件。accommodations 是住宿（常用复数）：look into accommodations。connection 是转机/转车：miss a connection（错过转机）。

- **plan ahead / look into / it's time** — 筹备三语。plan ahead 是"提前规划"；look into 是"调查、研究"：look into flights。it's time 引出"该做某事了"：It's time to book. ✗ It's time for book → ✓ It's time to book / It's time for booking。

- **intend to / planning on / thinking of** — 计划三式，注意介词。intend to + 动词原形：I intend to fly. planning on / thinking of + 动名词：I'm planning on flying. / I'm thinking of driving. ✗ planning on to fly 是常见错误。

- **was going to / end up** — 计划与现实。was going to 谈原本打算：I was going to take the train. end up 谈最终结果（常非预期）：We ended up staying home. end up + 动名词/形容词。

- **alternate / original** — 方案对照。alternate 是"备选的"：an alternate route。original 是"原来的"：the original plan。行程变动时两词必配：The original flight was canceled, so we took an alternate one.

- **considerably / potentially / roughly / slight** — 程度词。considerably 是"大大地"：considerably cheaper。potentially 是"可能地"：potentially dangerous。roughly 是"大约"：roughly three hours。slight 是"轻微的"：a slight delay。

- **sensible / preference / not my idea of** — 偏好表达。sensible 是"明智的"：a sensible choice。preference 是偏好：What's your preference? not my idea of 表委婉否定：Camping is not my idea of fun.

- **turn around / next / along the way** — 行程动态。turn around 是"掉头、折返"：Let's turn around. next 指"下一个"：the next flight。along the way 是"沿途"：We stopped at a deli along the way.

- **in order to / seafood** — 目的与美食。in order to 表目的，比 to 更正式：in order to save money。seafood 是海鲜：I'd like to try the seafood there.

<quiz-sentence-builder>

1. **中文**：我打算坐下一班转机。
   **英文**：I intend to take the next connection.
   > **解析**：intend to 后接动词原形 take；the next connection 作宾语。

2. **中文**：我在考虑开车去。
   **英文**：I'm thinking of driving there.
   > **解析**：think of 后接动名词 driving；there 是地点副词。

3. **中文**：我正计划提前预订住宿。
   **英文**：I'm planning on booking accommodations early.
   > **解析**：plan on 后接动名词 booking；early 副词修饰 booking。

4. **中文**：该调查一下航班了。
   **英文**：It's time to look into the flights.
   > **解析**：It's time to + 动词原形是固定句型；look into 表示"调查"。

5. **中文**：我们本来打算坐火车的。
   **英文**：We were going to take the train.
   > **解析**：were going to 后接动词原形，表示原本的计划。

6. **中文**：为了省钱，我们改了计划。
   **英文**：We changed the plan in order to save money.
   > **解析**：in order to + 动词原形表示目的；changed 是过去式。

7. **中文**：原航班取消了。
   **英文**：The original flight was canceled.
   > **解析**：was canceled 是被动语态；original 修饰 flight。

8. **中文**：所以我们选了备选路线。
   **英文**：So we took an alternate route.
   > **解析**：took 是 take 的过去式；alternate 修饰 route。

9. **中文**：最终我们待在了家里。
   **英文**：We ended up staying home.
   > **解析**：end up 后接动名词 staying；ended 是过去式。

10. **中文**：这个选择明智得多。
   **英文**：This choice is considerably more sensible.
   > **解析**：considerably 副词修饰比较级 more sensible。

11. **中文**：路上可能很堵。
   **英文**：The traffic could potentially be heavy.
   > **解析**：could be 是情态结构；potentially 副词表示"可能地"。

12. **中文**：飞行时间大约三个小时。
   **英文**：The flight is roughly three hours.
   > **解析**：roughly 副词表示"大约"，修饰 three hours。

13. **中文**：只是轻微的延误。
   **英文**：It's only a slight delay.
   > **解析**：slight 形容词修饰 delay；a slight delay 作表语。

14. **中文**：你的偏好是什么？
   **英文**：What's your preference?
   > **解析**：What 引导特殊疑问句；preference 作表语。

15. **中文**：露营可不是我理解的乐趣。
   **英文**：Camping is not my idea of fun.
   > **解析**：not my idea of 是委婉否定短语；Camping 动名词作主语。

16. **中文**：我们掉头吧。
   **英文**：Let's turn around.
   > **解析**：let's 后接动词原形；turn around 是动副短语"掉头"。

17. **中文**：沿途我们在一家熟食店停了下来。
   **英文**：We stopped at a deli along the way.
   > **解析**：stopped 是过去式；along the way 是地点状语"沿途"。

18. **中文**：我想去那里吃海鲜。
   **英文**：I'd like to try the seafood there.
   > **解析**：would like to 后接动词原形 try；the seafood 作宾语。

19. **中文**：提前规划总是明智的。
   **英文**：It's always sensible to plan ahead.
   > **解析**：It 是形式主语，真正主语是 to plan ahead；sensible 作表语。

20. **中文**：你错过了转机吗？
   **英文**：Did you miss your connection?
   > **解析**：一般疑问句用 Did 开头，动词 miss 用原形；your connection 作宾语。
