## 单元讲解

本单元围绕"讲述大学生活"展开。词表里有学科词（biology, chemistry, history, physics, science, grammar）和大学相关词汇（college, classmate, club, member, textbook, notebook）。你可以练习：I study biology at college. / She joined a science club. / I'm interested in chemistry. / Which class do you have? / This textbook is very useful.

interested in 表示"对……感兴趣"：I'm interested in history. / Are you interested in joining the club? excited to 表示"兴奋于做某事"：I'm excited to start college.

had 是 have 的过去式。which 用于选择：Which subject do you like?

## Tips

- **college / high school** — 大学 / 高中。搭配：go to college, in college, high school student。注意美式 college = 大学（英式用 university）。high school 在美国是9-12年级（约14-18岁）。

- **interested in** — 对……感兴趣。搭配：interested in science, interested in joining, interested in history。例句：I'm interested in biology. / Are you interested in the club? 注意 in 后接名词或动名词。

- **excited / excited to** — 兴奋的。搭配：excited to start, excited about, very excited。例句：I'm excited to go to college! / She's excited about the project. 注意 excited 形容人，exciting 形容事物。

- **join / member** — 加入 / 成员。搭配：join a club, join the team, a club member, a member of。例句：I joined a science club. / She is a member of the biology club. 注意 join 是"加入"（动作）。

- **biology / chemistry / physics** — 生物 / 化学 / 物理。搭配：study biology, a chemistry class, physics exam。注意这些都是不可数名词（学科名）。biology 的缩写是 bio。

- **classmate** — 同学。构词：class + mate。搭配：my classmate, a classmate of mine。注意 classmate 通常指同班同学。schoolmate 是校友（不一定同班）。

- **textbook / notebook** — 教科书 / 笔记本。搭配：a biology textbook, take notes in a notebook。构词：text + book, note + book。注意 textbook 是课本，reference book 是参考书。

- **quiz / project** — 小测验 / 项目。搭配：a pop quiz（突击测验）、a science project, work on a project。注意 quiz 比 test / exam 小（随堂小测）。project 是较大的课题/项目。

- **useful** — 有用的。搭配：very useful, useful information, a useful textbook。反义词 useless（无用的）。注意 useful 的发音：/ˈjuːsfl/（以 /j/ 开头，用 a：a useful book）。

- **which / what** — 哪个 / 什么。搭配：Which subject? / What class? / Which one? 注意 which 用于有限选择中：Which do you prefer, biology or chemistry? what 更开放：What do you study?

- **beginner / level** — 初学者 / 水平。搭配：a beginner, beginner level, intermediate level。例句：I'm a beginner in chemistry. / What level are you? 注意 beginner 和 advanced（高级的）对应。

- **conversation / grammar** — 对话 / 语法。搭配：have a conversation, practice conversation, grammar rules, grammar book。注意 conversation 是可数的：a conversation。grammar 通常不可数。
