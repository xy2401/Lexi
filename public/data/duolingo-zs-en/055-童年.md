> 描述你的童年

## 单元讲解

本单元围绕"描述你的童年"展开。核心句型是 at the age of...（在……岁时）和 there was / there were（过去时的"有"）：I learned to swim at the age of five. / There was a small school near my home. / There were lots of cartoons on TV.

词表里有过去式动词：asked, learned, loved, moved, showed, took。你可以练习：I loved cartoons when I was small. / She learned to ride a bike at the age of six. / We moved to a new city then.

数字 thirteen（13）和 fourteen（14）出现。注意 -teen 后缀表示十几：thirteen, fourteen, fifteen... nineteen。

## Tips

- **at the age of** — 在……岁时。搭配：at the age of five, at the age of ten。例句：I started school at the age of six. 注意也可以简单说 at five / at ten：I started school at six.

- **there was / there were** — 过去时的"有"。there was + 单数/不可数：There was a park near my home. there were + 复数：There were many trees. 否定：There wasn't / There weren't. 疑问：Was there...? / Were there...?

- **learned / learned to** — 学会了。learn → learned。搭配：learned English, learned to swim, learned to drive。注意 learn to do 表示"学会做某事"：I learned to cook at the age of twelve.

- **loved** — 非常喜欢（过去）。love → loved。搭配：I loved cartoons. / She loved sports. 注意 loved 比 liked 程度更强。过去时中 love/liked/enjoyed 都可以描述过去的喜好。

- **moved** — 搬家了、移动了。move → moved。搭配：moved to a new city, moved house。例句：We moved when I was ten. 注意 move 的"搬家"意思很常用：When did you move here?

- **cartoon** — 动画片、漫画。搭配：watch cartoons, a cartoon character, cartoons on TV。注意 cartoon 通常用复数（watch cartoons）。来自意大利语 cartone（大纸）。

- **thirteen / fourteen** — 十三 / 十四。构词：three → thirteen, four → fourteen。注意 thirteen 的重音在第二个音节（thir-TEEN），和 thirty（THIR-ty）不同。-teen 表示"十几"（13-19）。

- **small / poor / short** — 小的 / 贫穷的 / 矮的。搭配：a small village, a poor family, short hair。注意 small 和 little 的区别：small 侧重尺寸小，little 带有感情色彩（小巧可爱的）。

- **showed** — 展示了。show → showed。搭配：showed me a photo, showed us around。例句：My father showed me his old school. 注意 show → showed → shown（过去分词是 shown）。

- **noon** — 中午。搭配：at noon, noon break（午休）。例句：We had lunch at noon. 注意 noon 前用 at：at noon（不是 ✗ in noon）。对应 midnight（午夜）：at midnight。

- **only** — 仅仅、只有。搭配：only one, only a few, I was only five。例句：There was only one school. / I was only thirteen. 注意 only 的位置灵活，但通常放在被修饰词前面。

- **remember** — 记得。搭配：remember doing（记得做过）、remember to do（记得要去做）。例句：I remember watching cartoons. / Remember to finish school early. 注意 remember doing 和 remember to do 意思不同。

<quiz-sentence-builder>

1. **中文**：我的童年充满了欢声笑语和难忘的故事。
   **英文**：My childhood was full of laughter and memorable stories.
   > **解析**：childhood 表示“童年”；full of 表示“充满了”。

2. **中文**：我在一个风景秀丽的小乡村长大。
   **英文**：I grew up in a beautiful small village.
   > **解析**：grew up 表示“长大/成长”。

3. **中文**：我小时候住在祖父母家隔壁。
   **英文**：I lived next door to my grandparents when I was young.
   > **解析**：lived 表示“居住”；when I was young 表示“我小时候”。

4. **中文**：我们小时候经常在公园里玩捉迷藏。
   **英文**：We played hide-and-seek in the park every afternoon.
   > **解析**：played 表示“玩耍”；hide-and-seek 表示“捉迷藏”。

5. **中文**：我过去常常在夏天和朋友们去河里游泳。
   **英文**：I used to go swimming in the river with friends in summer.
   > **解析**：used to do 表示“过去常常做某事”。

6. **中文**：我今天突然记起了小学老师的名字。
   **英文**：I remembered my primary school teacher's name today.
   > **解析**：remembered 是 remember 的过去式，表示“记起/想起”。

7. **中文**：我和我最好的朋友从小就在一起玩耍。
   **英文**：My best friend and I played together since we were kids.
   > **解析**：best friend 表示“最好的朋友”。

8. **中文**：这辆小汽车是我童年最喜欢的玩具。
   **英文**：This toy car was my favorite toy during childhood.
   > **解析**：toy 表示“玩具”；favorite toy 表示“最喜欢的玩具”。

9. **中文**：放学后我们经常在院子里玩户外游戏。
   **英文**：We often played outdoor games after school.
   > **解析**：games 表示“游戏”。

10. **中文**：我们的社区过去非常安静祥和。
   **英文**：Our neighborhood used to be very quiet and peaceful.
   > **解析**：neighborhood 表示“社区/邻里”。

11. **中文**：在老房子里的那段岁月是我最快乐的回忆。
   **英文**：Those days in the old house were my happiest memories.
   > **解析**：happiest 表示“最快乐的”；memories 表示“回忆”。

12. **中文**：我小时候最喜欢的科目是艺术和体育。
   **英文**：My favorite subject when I was young was art.
   > **解析**：favorite 表示“最喜欢的”。

13. **中文**：我们小时候常在小学那片草地上跑步。
   **英文**：We used to run on the field at our primary school.
   > **解析**：school 表示“学校”。

14. **中文**：我家附近有一个带有大秋千的公园。
   **英文**：There was a park with a large swing near my house.
   > **解析**：park 表示“公园”；swing 表示“秋千”。

15. **中文**：在我小的时候，我总是梦想能够飞翔。
   **英文**：When I was young, I always dreamed of flying.
   > **解析**：when I was young 表示“当我小的时候/在童年时”。

16. **中文**：我和同伴们在小镇度过了快乐的时光。
   **英文**：My friends and I had a very happy time in the town.
   > **解析**：happy 表示“快乐的/幸福的”。

17. **中文**：孩子们非常喜欢在秋千上荡来荡去。
   **英文**：Kids loved playing on the swing in the playground with at the age of.
   > **解析**：swing 表示“秋千”。

18. **中文**：捉迷藏是我童年时代最喜爱的传统游戏。
   **英文**：Hide-and-seek was my favorite traditional game with asked.
   > **解析**：hide-and-seek 表示“捉迷藏游戏”。

19. **中文**：我永远不会忘记那些美好的童年时光。
   **英文**：I will never forget those precious childhood days with again.
   > **解析**：forget 表示“忘记”。

20. **中文**：在这片安静的街区成长是一件幸运的事。
   **英文**：Growing up in this quiet neighborhood was lucky.
   > **解析**：growing up 表示“成长/长大”。
