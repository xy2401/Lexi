## 单元讲解

本单元围绕"描述你的童年"展开。核心句型是 at the age of...（在……岁时）和 there was / there were（过去时的"有"）：I learned to swim at the age of five. / There was a small school near my home. / There were lots of cartoons on TV.

词表里有过去式动词：asked, learned, loved, moved, showed, took。你可以练习：I loved cartoons when I was small. / She learned to ride a bike at the age of six. / We moved to a new city then.

数字 thirteen（13）和 fourteen（14）出现。注意 -teen 后缀表示十几：thirteen, fourteen, fifteen... nineteen。

## Tips

- **at the age of** — 在……岁时。搭配：at the age of five, at the age of ten。例句：I started school at the age of six. 注意也可以简单说 at five / at ten：I started school at six.

- **there was / there were** — 过去时的"有"。there was + 单数/不可数：There was a park near my home. there were + 复数：There were many trees. 否定：There wasn't / There weren't. 疑问：Was there...? / Were there...?

- **learned / learned to** — 学会了。learn → learned。搭配：learned English, learned to swim, learned to drive。注意 learn to do 表示"学会做某事"：I learned to cook at the age of twelve.

- **loved** — 非常喜欢（过去）。love → loved。搭配：I loved cartoons. / She loved sports. 注意 loved 比 liked 程度更强。过去时中 love/liked/enjoyed 都可以描述过去的喜好。

- **moved** — 搬家了、移动了。move → moved。搭配：moved to a new city, moved house。例句：We moved when I was ten. 注意 move 的"搬家"意思很常用：When did you move here?

- **cartoon** — 动画片、漫画。搭配：watch cartoons, a cartoon character, cartoons on TV。注意 cartoon 通常用复数（watch cartoons）。来自意大利语 cartone（大纸）。

- **thirteen / fourteen** — 十三 / 十四。构词：three → thirteen, four → fourteen。注意 thirteen 的重音在第二个音节（thir-TEEN），和 thirty（THIR-ty）不同。-teen 表示"十几"（13-19）。

- **small / poor / short** — 小的 / 贫穷的 / 矮的。搭配：a small village, a poor family, short hair。注意 small 和 little 的区别：small 侧重尺寸小，little 带有感情色彩（小巧可爱的）。

- **showed** — 展示了。show → showed。搭配：showed me a photo, showed us around。例句：My father showed me his old school. 注意 show → showed → shown（过去分词是 shown）。

- **noon** — 中午。搭配：at noon, noon break（午休）。例句：We had lunch at noon. 注意 noon 前用 at：at noon（不是 ✗ in noon）。对应 midnight（午夜）：at midnight。

- **only** — 仅仅、只有。搭配：only one, only a few, I was only five。例句：There was only one school. / I was only thirteen. 注意 only 的位置灵活，但通常放在被修饰词前面。

- **remember** — 记得。搭配：remember doing（记得做过）、remember to do（记得要去做）。例句：I remember watching cartoons. / Remember to finish school early. 注意 remember doing 和 remember to do 意思不同。

<quiz-sentence-builder>

1. **中文**：她在六岁时学会了游泳，这让她的父母非常自豪。
   **英文**：She learned to swim at the age of six, which made her parents very proud.
   > **解析**：**at the age of** 是固定介词短语，意为"在……岁时"，后接具体数字；learned to swim 中 learn to do sth 表示"学会做某事"。

2. **中文**：小时候，我非常喜欢看电视上播放的动画片。
   **英文**：When I was little, I loved watching cartoons on television.
   > **解析**：**loved** 是 love 的过去式，love doing sth 表示"热衷于做某事"；**cartoon** 作可数名词意为"动画片"，通常用复数 cartoons。

3. **中文**：我们小时候住过的那个小村子里，只有一所学校。
   **英文**：There was only one school in the small village where we grew up.
   > **解析**：**there was** 是 there is 的过去式，后接单数名词，表示"过去某处存在某物"；**small** 作形容词意为"小的"，修饰名词 village。

4. **中文**：我十岁的时候，我们全家搬到了一个新城市生活。
   **英文**：My whole family moved to a new city when I was ten years old.
   > **解析**：**moved** 是 move 的规则过去式；move to 是固定搭配，意为"搬迁至、移居到"某个新的地方。

5. **中文**：我父亲给我看了一些他十三岁时拍的老照片。
   **英文**：My father showed me some old photos taken when he was thirteen.
   > **解析**：**showed** 是 show 的规则过去式；show sb sth 是双宾语结构，意为"给某人看某物"；**thirteen** 意为"十三"。

6. **中文**：我依然清楚地记得在小学完成学业那天的情景。
   **英文**：I still clearly remember the day I finished school at the primary level.
   > **解析**：**remember** 作及物动词意为"记得"；remember + 名词短语表示"记得某事"；**finish school** 意为"完成学业/毕业"。

7. **中文**：那时候村里非常贫穷，孩子们放学后都在街上玩耍。
   **英文**：The village was very poor back then, and children played in the streets after school.
   > **解析**：**poor** 作形容词意为"贫穷的"，在此修饰村子的整体生活条件；back then 是口语中常用的"在那个年代/当时"。

8. **中文**：我十四岁那年第一次独自乘坐地铁去市中心。
   **英文**：At the age of fourteen, I took the subway to the city center on my own for the first time.
   > **解析**：**took** 是 take 的不规则过去式；take the subway 意为"乘地铁"；**fourteen** 意为"十四"，-teen 结尾表示十几。

9. **中文**：我问妈妈是否可以再看一集动画片，但她说不行。
   **英文**：I asked my mom if I could watch one more cartoon, but she said no.
   > **解析**：**asked** 是 ask 的规则过去式；ask sb if/whether 引导宾语从句，表示"询问某人是否……"；**again** 的含义隐含于 one more 中。

10. **中文**：童年的午休时间，我们通常聚在院子里一起玩耍。
    **英文**：During childhood, we usually gathered in the yard to play together at noon.
    > **解析**：**noon** 作名词意为"正午、中午"，固定搭配是 at noon，注意介词用 at 而非 in；这里描述的是童年的日常生活场景。
