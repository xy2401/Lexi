## 单元讲解

本单元围绕"分享高中学习经历"展开。核心是现在完成时的否定和疑问：Have you passed the exam?（你通过考试了吗？）、I haven't graduated yet.（我还没毕业。）、She has already taken the test.（她已经考完试了。）、Has he prepared for the exam?（他准备好考试了吗？）。

词表里有学校词汇（diploma, grade, semester, report card, principal）和考试词（fail, pass, score, mistake）。你可以练习：I failed geography once, but I passed it twice. / She got a good score on her report card. / I'm worried about my studies. / Have you prepared for the exam yet?

yet 用于否定句和疑问句。already 用于肯定句。

## Tips

- **haven't / hasn't + yet** — 还没。搭配：haven't graduated yet, hasn't passed yet。例句：I haven't graduated yet. / She hasn't taken the exam yet. 注意 yet 放在句末，用于否定句和疑问句。

- **already** — 已经。搭配：have already passed, has already graduated。例句：I've already taken the test. / She has already graduated. 注意 already 放在 have 和过去分词之间，用于肯定句。

- **pass / fail** — 通过 / 不及格。搭配：pass the exam, fail the test, passed geography。例句：I passed math but failed geography. / She passed on her second try. 注意 pass 和 fail 是反义词。

- **graduate / diploma** — 毕业 / 毕业证书。搭配：graduate from high school, get a diploma, graduated from Harvard。例句：I graduated last year. / She got her diploma. 注意 graduate from + 学校。

- **report card / grade / score** — 成绩单 / 年级/成绩 / 分数。搭配：my report card, get a good grade, a high score。例句：I got good grades on my report card. / What's your score? 注意 grade 可以指"年级"也可以指"成绩"。

- **semester** — 学期。搭配：this semester, last semester, first semester。例句：I studied geography last semester. / This semester is hard. 注意美式用 semester（一学年两学期），英式用 term。

- **principal** — 校长。搭配：the school principal, the principal's office。例句：The principal gave a speech. / Go to the principal's office. 注意 principal 和 principle（原则）拼写相同但意思不同。

- **prepared / prepare** — 准备好了。搭配：prepare for the exam, well prepared, prepared for。例句：Have you prepared for the test? / I'm well prepared. 注意 prepare for + 考试/事件。

- **once / twice** — 一次 / 两次。搭配：failed once, passed twice, once a week。例句：I failed once but passed twice. / I've been there twice. 注意"三次"以上用 three times, four times。

- **worried / happy for** — 担心的 / 为……高兴。搭配：worried about, worried about my studies, happy for you。例句：I'm worried about the exam. / I'm happy for you! 注意 worried about + 事物，happy for + 人。

- **mistake** — 错误。搭配：make a mistake, a big mistake, learn from mistakes。例句：I made a mistake on the test. / Don't make the same mistake. 注意 make a mistake 是固定搭配（不说 ✗ do a mistake）。

- **well-known / Harvard** — 著名的 / 哈佛。搭配：a well-known school, well-known university, study at Harvard。例句：Harvard is a well-known university. / She studies at Harvard. 注意 well-known = famous。

<quiz-sentence-builder>

1. **中文**：他在高一学期的期末数学考试中拿到了全A成绩。
   **英文**：He got a straight A grade in math this semester.
   > **解析**：grade 表示“成绩/等级”；semester 表示“学期”。

2. **中文**：她在英语听力测试中获得了九十五分的高分。
   **英文**：She earned a high score of 95 on the test.
   > **解析**：score 表示“分数/得分”。

3. **中文**：经过努力复习，我们班所有同学都顺利通过了考试。
   **英文**：All students passed the graduation exam.
   > **解析**：passed 表示“通过了”。

4. **中文**：他因为平时旷课且没有准备而导致那门科目不及格。
   **英文**：He failed the history course due to laziness.
   > **解析**：failed 表示“不及格/未通过”。

5. **中文**：家长在学期末收到了学校寄来的学期成绩单。
   **英文**：Parents received the end-of-term report card.
   > **解析**：report card 表示“成绩单”。

6. **中文**：高中毕业典礼上，校长亲自颁发了毕业证书。
   **英文**：The principal handed out high school diplomas.
   > **解析**：diploma 表示“毕业证书/文凭”；principal 表示“校长”。

7. **中文**：哥哥将于今年六月份顺利从高中毕业。
   **英文**：My brother will graduate from high school in June.
   > **解析**：graduate 表示“毕业”。

8. **中文**：秋季新学期将于九月初正式开学。
   **英文**：The new fall semester starts in early September.
   > **解析**：semester 表示“学期”。

9. **中文**：高中生应当专注于自己的学业并合理安排时间。
   **英文**：High schoolers should focus on their studies.
   > **解析**：studies 表示“学业/学习”。

10. **中文**：高中校长在全校大会上发表了鼓舞人心的演讲。
   **英文**：The high school principal gave an inspiring speech.
   > **解析**：principal 表示“校长”。

11. **中文**：考上哈佛大学是他自幼以来一直坚持的梦想。
   **英文**：Getting into Harvard has been his dream.
   > **解析**：Harvard 表示“哈佛大学”。

12. **中文**：这是一所学术氛围浓厚且享誉世界的著名高校。
   **英文**：This is a very well-known and top university.
   > **解析**：well-known 表示“著名的/众所周知的”。

13. **中文**：同学们正在图书馆积极复习准备明天的期末考。
   **英文**：Students are in library to prepare for exams.
   > **解析**：prepare 表示“准备/复习”。

14. **中文**：做题时仔细检查可以有效避免不必要的粗心错误。
   **英文**：Careful check avoids unnecessary careless mistakes.
   > **解析**：mistakes 表示“错误”。

15. **中文**：老师对大家在这次竞赛中取得的进步感到非常满意高兴。
   **英文**：The teacher was very pleased with our progress.
   > **解析**：pleased 表示“高兴的/满意的”。

16. **中文**：他在老师提醒前就已经完成了整张试卷。
   **英文**：He had already finished the test before time.
   > **解析**：already 表示“已经”。

17. **中文**：考试成绩结果还没有在公告栏上公布出来。
   **英文**：The test results have not been posted yet.
   > **解析**：yet 用于否定句末，表示“还/尚”。

18. **中文**：高中时代的学习生活充满了挑战与欢笑。
   **英文**：Life in high school is full of learning and fun.
   > **解析**：high school 表示“高中”。

19. **中文**：优秀的学术成绩是申请名牌大学的有利优势。
   **英文**：Great academic performance helps university entry.
   > **解析**：academic 表示“学术的/学业的”。

20. **中文**：明天的期末大考是对本学期所学知识的全面检验。
   **英文**：Tomorrow's exam tests knowledge learned all term.
   > **解析**：exam 表示“考试”。
