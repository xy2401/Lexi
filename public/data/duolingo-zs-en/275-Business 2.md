## 单元讲解

本单元围绕"分享事业里程碑和成就"展开，场景很可能是一个家族企业的传承故事。词表里有商业运营的基本词汇（advertising, goods, run, estimate, in charge），有描述企业生命周期的表达（early days, milestone, next chapter, shut down, take over），也有回顾成就时的情感词汇（rewarding, ups and downs, for the better）。你可以练习这样的叙述：My grandfather started this family-owned business, and my father handed it down to me. / I'll have been running it for ten years by next month. / There have been ups and downs, but overall things have changed for the better.

语法上，本单元最值得注意的是将来完成时：will have been / i'll have been / will you have。这个时态用来站在未来某个时间点回看已经完成的时长或成就：By next year, I'll have been in charge for a decade. / Will you have retired by then? 搭配 from now 和 by，构成"从现在起算到某个未来节点"的时间框架。

## Tips

- **will have been / i'll have been / will you have** — 将来完成时（将来完成进行时）。用来描述到未来某时刻为止已经持续或完成的动作。例句：By December, I'll have been running this shop for 20 years. / Will you have finished the project by Friday? 结构：will + have + 过去分词（或 will + have been + doing）。常和 by + 时间点搭配。

- **hand down / take over** — 一对传承动词。hand down 是"传下去"（长辈传给晚辈）：My grandmother handed down the recipe to me. take over 是"接手、接管"：She took over the family business. 注意 hand down 也可以指"把旧衣服传给弟妹"。

- **family-owned** — 家族拥有的、家族经营的。family（家族）+ owned（拥有的），复合形容词。搭配：a family-owned restaurant / a family-owned business。类似构词：state-owned（国有的）、privately owned（私有的）。

- **milestone** — 里程碑。原指路边标记里程的石碑，引申为人生或事业中的重要节点。搭配：reach a milestone / a major milestone / a milestone in our history。例句：Opening our second location was a huge milestone.

- **ups and downs** — 起起落落、沉浮。固定搭配，总是用复数。搭配：the ups and downs of business / life has its ups and downs。例句：Every business has its ups and downs—the key is to keep going.

- **run** — 这里指"经营、管理"：run a business / run a shop / run a company。不是"跑步"。同义词：manage / operate。例句：She's been running the family store since her father retired. 注意 run 的过去式是 ran，过去分词是 run。

- **shut down** — 关闭、停业。搭配：shut down a business / shut down a factory / the store shut down。比 close 更强调"永久停止运营"。例句：Many small shops shut down during the recession. 注意也可以写成 shutdown（名词）。

- **rewarding** — 有回报的、值得做的。搭配：a rewarding career / a rewarding experience / find it rewarding。强调精神上的满足感，不只是金钱回报。例句：Running a family business is hard but incredibly rewarding.

- **in charge** — 负责、主管。搭配：be in charge of / take charge / who's in charge here? 例句：I've been in charge of the company for five years. 注意 of 后接负责的具体事务。同义：in control of / responsible for。

- **for the better** — 向好的方向。搭配：change for the better / things are looking up for the better。反义：for the worse（向坏的方向）。例句：Since we took over, things have changed for the better.

- **early days** — 早期、创业初期。搭配：in the early days / back in the early days。例句：In the early days, we worked out of a garage. 注意 days 用复数，表示"那段时期"。类似表达：the beginning / the start。

- **next chapter** — 下一篇章。比喻人生或事业的新阶段。搭配：the next chapter of my life / start the next chapter / a new chapter。例句：Retirement is not the end—it's the next chapter. 比 next step 更有叙事感和仪式感。

- **estimate / goods / advertising** — 三个商业基础词。estimate 是"估算"（动词/名词）：estimate the cost / a rough estimate。goods 是"商品、货物"（复数）：deliver goods / goods and services。advertising 是"广告业、广告宣传"：work in advertising / an advertising campaign。

- **do a good job** — 做得好。搭配：do a good job of / do a good job on / you did a good job。例句：My father did a good job building this business. 注意 job 是可数名词，a good job 有冠词。

<quiz-sentence-builder>

1. **中文**：这家家族经营的餐厅已经传承了三代人。
   **英文**：This family-owned restaurant has been operating for three generations.
   > **解析**：family-owned 意为家族拥有的、家族经营的。

2. **中文**：我的祖父把这项传统生意传交给了我的父亲。
   **英文**：My grandfather handed down this traditional business to my father.
   > **解析**：hand down 意为传给（后代）。

3. **中文**：她在五年前接管了这家公司并扩大了业务。
   **英文**：She took over the company five years ago and expanded the business.
   > **解析**：take over 意为接管、接手。

4. **中文**：开设第二家分店是我们发展过程中的一个重要里程碑。
   **英文**：Opening our second location was a major milestone for our company.
   > **解析**：milestone 意为里程碑、重要节点。

5. **中文**：每家企业都会经历起起落落，但我们坚持了下来。
   **英文**：Every business goes through ups and downs, but we kept going.
   > **解析**：ups and downs 意为起起落落、沉浮。

6. **中文**：他经营这家杂货店已经有二十多年的历史了。
   **英文**：He has run this grocery store for more than twenty years.
   > **解析**：run 意为经营、管理。

7. **中文**：许多小店铺在经济衰退期间不幸倒闭停业了。
   **英文**：Many small shops were forced to shut down during the economic decline.
   > **解析**：shut down 意为关闭、停业。

8. **中文**：看着自己的事业从零发展壮大是一件非常有成就感的事。
   **英文**：Seeing your business grow from scratch is extremely rewarding.
   > **解析**：rewarding 意为有回报的、令人满意的。

9. **中文**：经理不在的时候，由她全权负责整个办公室的运转。
   **英文**：She is in charge of the office when the manager is away.
   > **解析**：in charge 意为负责、主管。

10. **中文**：自从引进了新管理层后，情况发生了向好的转变。
   **英文**：Since we brought in new management, things have changed for the better.
   > **解析**：for the better 意为向好的方向。

11. **中文**：在创业初期，我们不得不每天工作很长时间。
   **英文**：In the early days, we had to work very long hours every day.
   > **解析**：early days 意为早期、创业初期。

12. **中文**：退休并不意味着结束，而是人生事业的下一个新篇章。
   **英文**：Retirement is not the end, but the next chapter of his career.
   > **解析**：next chapter 意为下一篇章、新阶段。

13. **中文**：我们加大在网络广告方面的投入以吸引更多客户。
   **英文**：We invested more money in online advertising to attract customers.
   > **解析**：advertising 意为广告、宣传。

14. **中文**：这家工厂每天向全国各地运输高质量的货物。
   **英文**：The factory ships high-quality goods across the country every day.
   > **解析**：goods 意为货物、商品。

15. **中文**：我们需要准确估计修缮老办公楼所需的成本。
   **英文**：We need to accurately estimate the cost of renovating the building.
   > **解析**：estimate 意为估计、预估。

16. **中文**：只要你用心干好工作，你的努力终究会被大家看到。
   **英文**：If you do a good job, your efforts will eventually be recognized.
   > **解析**：do a good job 意为做好工作。

17. **中文**：到下个月，我就将在这家公司工作满十年了。
   **英文**：By next month, I'll have been working at this company for ten years.
   > **解析**：i'll have been 意为“我到时将已经……（将来完成进行）”。

18. **中文**：到今年年底，这家老店就将开业满半个世纪了。
   **英文**：By the end of this year, the shop will have been open for half a century.
   > **解析**：will have been 意为“到时将已经……”。

19. **中文**：到周五下班前，你届时是否已经完成项目的预算评估？
   **英文**：Will you have finished the budget estimate by Friday afternoon?
   > **解析**：will you have 意为“届时你是否已经完成……”。

20. **中文**：从现在算起的五年后，我们希望能再开三家分店。
   **英文**：Five years from now, we hope to open three more locations.
   > **解析**：from now 意为从现在起算。
