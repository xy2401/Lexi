# Unit 110: 梦

> 分享你做的梦

## 单词

about, awake, clown, control, could, could not, couldn't, diary, dream, end, fall asleep, fell asleep, foreign, full of, gem, in, in danger, lion, mouse, president, real, scary, ship, sleep, sound, trial, up all night, wake, war, woke

## 单元讲解

本单元围绕"分享你做的梦"展开。核心是描述梦境的表达：I fell asleep and had a scary dream.（我睡着了，做了个可怕的梦。）、In my dream, I was on a ship full of lions.（在梦里，我在一艘满是狮子的船上。）、I couldn't control what happened.（我控制不了发生的事。）、I was up all night—I couldn't fall asleep.（我整晚没睡——睡不着。）。

词表里有梦境词汇（dream, scary, real, in danger）和睡眠词汇（fall asleep, awake, wake, sleep）。你可以练习：I woke up at the end. / The dream felt so real. / I wrote about it in my diary. / It was like a war movie.

fall asleep 是"入睡"（动作），be asleep 是"睡着"（状态）。

## Tips

- **fall asleep / fell asleep** — 入睡。搭配：fall asleep quickly, fell asleep on the sofa。例句：I fell asleep during the movie. / I can't fall asleep. 注意 fall asleep 是瞬间动作，不能和 for two hours 搭配。

- **wake / woke / awake** — 醒来 / 醒了 / 醒着的。搭配：wake up, woke up, stay awake。例句：I woke up at three. / I was still awake at midnight. 注意 wake → woke → woken，awake 是形容词。

- **dream / scary / real** — 梦 / 可怕的 / 真实的。搭配：have a dream, a scary dream, feel real。例句：I had a scary dream. / The dream felt so real. 注意 dream 既可数（a dream）也可作动词（dream about）。

- **couldn't / could not / control** — 不能 / 控制。搭配：couldn't move, couldn't control, out of control。例句：I couldn't move in the dream. / I couldn't control what happened. 注意 couldn't 是 could not 的缩写。

- **up all night / sleep** — 整晚没睡 / 睡眠。搭配：up all night, get some sleep, lose sleep。例句：I was up all night worrying. / I need some sleep. 注意 up all night = 整夜未眠。

- **in danger / lion / mouse** — 处于危险中 / 狮子 / 老鼠。搭配：in danger, in danger of, a lion, a mouse。例句：In my dream, I was in danger. / A lion was chasing me. / A mouse ran across the ship.

- **ship / war / president** — 船 / 战争 / 总统。搭配：on a ship, a war movie, the president。例句：I was on a ship in my dream. / It was like a war. / I met the president! 注意这些在梦中都可能出现。

- **full of / foreign** — 充满 / 外国的。搭配：full of people, a foreign country。例句：The ship was full of animals. / I was in a foreign country. 注意 full of = filled with。

- **diary / sound** — 日记 / 听起来。搭配：write in my diary, keep a diary, that sounds...。例句：I wrote about the dream in my diary. / That sounds scary! 注意 sound 作动词表示"听起来"。

- **clown / gem / trial** — 小丑 / 宝石 / 审判。搭配：a scary clown, a precious gem, on trial。例句：A clown was in my dream. / I found a gem. / The president was on trial. 注意 trial 可指"审判"或"试验"。

- **end / about / in** — 结尾 / 关于 / 在……里。搭配：at the end, dream about, in the dream。例句：I woke up at the end. / I dreamed about lions. / In the dream, I couldn't run.

- **could / woke / real** — 能够 / 醒了 / 真实的。搭配：I could fly, woke up suddenly, real life。例句：In the dream, I could fly. / I woke up suddenly. / It didn't feel real.
