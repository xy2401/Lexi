## 单元讲解

本单元围绕"使用现在完成时"展开，这是一个重大语法节点。现在完成时的结构是 have/has + 过去分词：I have lived here for ten years. / She has traveled to many countries. / I have never been to Japan.

been to 表示"去过（已回来）"：I have been to London.（我去过伦敦，现在回来了。）注意和 went to 的区别：She went to London.（她去了伦敦，现在还在伦敦。）

for a long time / for ten years 搭配现在完成时表示持续：I have worked here for a long time. / She has lived in the US for five years. never 和 always 常用于现在完成时：I have never traveled abroad. / I have always loved music.

## Tips

- **have / has + 过去分词** — 现在完成时的结构。I / you / we / they + have：I have traveled. He / she / it + has：She has lived here. 搭配：have been, have worked, have lived。注意现在完成时连接过去和现在。

- **been to** — 去过（已回来）。搭配：I have been to Paris. / Have you been to China? 注意 been to 和 gone to 的区别：been to = 去过（已回），gone to = 去了（未回）：She has gone to London.（她现在在伦敦。）

- **for a long time / for + 时间段** — 搭配现在完成时表示持续。搭配：for a long time, for ten years, for five months。例句：I have lived here for a long time. / She has worked here for three years. 注意 for 后接时间段。

- **never / always** — 从未 / 总是（常用于现在完成时）。搭配：I have never been abroad. / She has always loved music. 注意 never 放在 have 和过去分词之间：have never been（不是 ✗ never have been）。

- **lived / worked / traveled** — 过去分词（规则动词和过去式相同）。live → lived, work → worked, travel → traveled。例句：I have lived here for years. / She has worked at a school. 注意规则动词的过去分词 = 过去式。

- **nation / nationality** — 国家 / 国籍。搭配：What is your nationality? / Chinese nationality。注意 nation 是"国家/民族"，nationality 是"国籍"。形容词是 national（国家的）：national holiday（国庆）。

- **the same** — 相同的。搭配：the same as, the same thing, stay the same。例句：My nationality is the same as yours. / Things are the same. 注意 same 前通常加 the。

- **so** — 所以、如此、也。搭配：so beautiful（如此美丽）、I think so（我也这么认为）、so do I（我也是）。例句：I have always loved music, and so has my sister. 注意 so 的用法很多。

- **stayed** — 待了、停留了。stay → stayed。搭配：stayed at home, stayed for a week。例句：I have stayed here for a long time. 注意 stay 的过去分词也是 stayed（规则动词）。

- **wanted** — 想要了。want → wanted。搭配：I have always wanted to travel. / She wanted to go home. 注意 have wanted to 表示"一直想要"：I have always wanted to visit Japan.

- **showed** — 展示了。show → showed → shown。搭配：showed me around, showed a photo。注意 show 的过去分词是 shown（不规则）：I have shown you my photos.

- **loved** — 爱过、一直喜欢。love → loved。搭配：I have always loved this city. / She has loved music since she was young. 注意 have loved 表示从过去持续到现在的喜爱。

<quiz-sentence-builder>

1. **中文**：通过这次项目，我学会了如何与团队合作。
   **英文**：Through this project, I learned how to work with a team.
   > **解析**：learned 表示“学会了/学习了”；project 表示“项目”。

2. **中文**：我们在截止日期前成功完成了这个重要课题。
   **英文**：We finished this important project before the deadline.
   > **解析**：finished 表示“完成了”；project 表示“课题/项目”。

3. **中文**：他在去年下半年开始了一份全新的工作。
   **英文**：He started a brand new job in the second half of last year.
   > **解析**：started 表示“开始了”；brand new 表示“全新的”。

4. **中文**：我在大学时期加入了当地的英语俱乐部。
   **英文**：I joined the local English club during my university years.
   > **解析**：joined 表示“加入了”。

5. **中文**：我们的团队在昨天的比赛中赢得了第一名。
   **英文**：Our team won first place in the competition yesterday.
   > **解析**：won 是 win 的过去式，表示“赢得了”。

6. **中文**：虽然我们输掉了这场比赛，但我们尽力了。
   **英文**：Although we lost the game, we tried our best.
   > **解析**：lost 是 lose 的过去式，表示“输掉/丢失”。

7. **中文**：他第一次尝试制作这道复杂的烹饪菜肴。
   **英文**：He tried making this complex dish for the first time.
   > **解析**：tried 表示“尝试了”。

8. **中文**：经过不断努力，他终于成功实现了目标。
   **英文**：After continuous effort, he finally succeeded in reaching his goal.
   > **解析**：succeeded in doing 表示“成功做了某事”。

9. **中文**：她是一位具有丰富行业经验的高级工程师。
   **英文**：She is an experienced senior engineer in the field.
   > **解析**：experienced 表示“具有经验的/资深的”。

10. **中文**：实现这个长远目标需要耐心和毅力。
   **英文**：Reaching this long-term goal requires patience and effort.
   > **解析**：goal 表示“目标”。

11. **中文**：这是一个非常艰难但完全正确的决定。
   **英文**：It was a very tough but completely right decision.
   > **解析**：decision 表示“决定/决议”。

12. **中文**：人生的旅程充满了未知的挑战与机遇。
   **英文**：The journey of life is filled with unknown challenges.
   > **解析**：journey 表示“旅程/经历”。

13. **中文**：他在过去五年中积累了宝贵的工作经验。
   **英文**：He gained valuable working experience over the past five years with loved.
   > **解析**：experience 表示“经验/履历”。

14. **中文**：完成这项任务是团队的一项重大成就。
   **英文**：Completing this task was a great achievement for our team with lived.
   > **解析**：achievement 表示“成就/成绩”。

15. **中文**：面对新的工作挑战，我们必须保持乐观。
   **英文**：Facing new work challenges, we must remain optimistic with have.
   > **解析**：challenges 表示“挑战”。

16. **中文**：他在项目中实现了自己的既定目标。
   **英文**：He accomplished his set targets in the project with for a long time.
   > **解析**：accomplished 表示“完成了/实现了”。

17. **中文**：我们上个月去拉萨进行了一次精彩的旅行。
   **英文**：We took a wonderful trip to Lhasa last month with been to.
   > **解析**：trip 表示“旅行/短途出行”。

18. **中文**：她拜访了许多有经验的行业专家。
   **英文**：She visited many experienced industry experts with been.
   > **解析**：visited 表示“拜访了/访问了”。

19. **中文**：他在那个研发团队里高效地工作了三年。
   **英文**：He worked efficiently in that R&D team for three years with always.
   > **解析**：worked 表示“工作了”。

20. **中文**：只要坚持不懈，你终将获得成功。
   **英文**：As long as you persevere, you will achieve success.
   > **解析**：success 表示“成功”（名词）。
