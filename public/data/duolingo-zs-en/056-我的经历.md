## 单元讲解

本单元围绕"使用现在完成时"展开，这是一个重大语法节点。现在完成时的结构是 have/has + 过去分词：I have lived here for ten years. / She has traveled to many countries. / I have never been to Japan.

been to 表示"去过（已回来）"：I have been to London.（我去过伦敦，现在回来了。）注意和 went to 的区别：She went to London.（她去了伦敦，现在还在伦敦。）

for a long time / for ten years 搭配现在完成时表示持续：I have worked here for a long time. / She has lived in the US for five years. never 和 always 常用于现在完成时：I have never traveled abroad. / I have always loved music.

## Tips

- **have / has + 过去分词** — 现在完成时的结构。I / you / we / they + have：I have traveled. He / she / it + has：She has lived here. 搭配：have been, have worked, have lived。注意现在完成时连接过去和现在。

- **been to** — 去过（已回来）。搭配：I have been to Paris. / Have you been to China? 注意 been to 和 gone to 的区别：been to = 去过（已回），gone to = 去了（未回）：She has gone to London.（她现在在伦敦。）

- **for a long time / for + 时间段** — 搭配现在完成时表示持续。搭配：for a long time, for ten years, for five months。例句：I have lived here for a long time. / She has worked here for three years. 注意 for 后接时间段。

- **never / always** — 从未 / 总是（常用于现在完成时）。搭配：I have never been abroad. / She has always loved music. 注意 never 放在 have 和过去分词之间：have never been（不是 ✗ never have been）。

- **lived / worked / traveled** — 过去分词（规则动词和过去式相同）。live → lived, work → worked, travel → traveled。例句：I have lived here for years. / She has worked at a school. 注意规则动词的过去分词 = 过去式。

- **nation / nationality** — 国家 / 国籍。搭配：What is your nationality? / Chinese nationality。注意 nation 是"国家/民族"，nationality 是"国籍"。形容词是 national（国家的）：national holiday（国庆）。

- **the same** — 相同的。搭配：the same as, the same thing, stay the same。例句：My nationality is the same as yours. / Things are the same. 注意 same 前通常加 the。

- **so** — 所以、如此、也。搭配：so beautiful（如此美丽）、I think so（我也这么认为）、so do I（我也是）。例句：I have always loved music, and so has my sister. 注意 so 的用法很多。

- **stayed** — 待了、停留了。stay → stayed。搭配：stayed at home, stayed for a week。例句：I have stayed here for a long time. 注意 stay 的过去分词也是 stayed（规则动词）。

- **wanted** — 想要了。want → wanted。搭配：I have always wanted to travel. / She wanted to go home. 注意 have wanted to 表示"一直想要"：I have always wanted to visit Japan.

- **showed** — 展示了。show → showed → shown。搭配：showed me around, showed a photo。注意 show 的过去分词是 shown（不规则）：I have shown you my photos.

- **loved** — 爱过、一直喜欢。love → loved。搭配：I have always loved this city. / She has loved music since she was young. 注意 have loved 表示从过去持续到现在的喜爱。

<quiz-sentence-builder>

1. **中文**：因为工作关系，她去过好几个欧洲国家。  
   **英文**：She has been to several European countries for work for always.
   > **解析**：**been to** 用于现在完成时 has/have been to，表示“去过某地（现已返回）”。

2. **中文**：我在这个城市生活了很长时间。  
   **英文**：I have lived in this city for a long time for been.
   > **解析**：**for a long time** 是时间状语短语，意为“很长时间”，常与现在完成时态连用。

3. **中文**：从五年前开始，我们家就一直住在上海。  
   **英文**：My family has lived in Shanghai since five years ago (been to).
   > **解析**：**lived** 是 live 的过去分词/过去式；has/have lived 表示从过去延续至今的居住状态。

4. **中文**：他在这家公司担任工程师工作了十年。  
   **英文**：He has worked as an engineer at this firm for ten years.
   > **解析**：**worked** 是 work 的过去分词，has worked for + 时长 表示“已经工作了多长时间”。

5. **中文**：她一直以来都非常喜欢在合唱团里唱歌。  
   **英文**：She has always loved singing in the choir (for a long time).
   > **解析**：**always** 是频度副词，意为“总是/一直”，在完成时中置于助动词 have/has 之后。

6. **中文**：你在护照申请表上的国籍是什么？  
   **英文**：What is your nationality on your passport application? for has.
   > **解析**：**nationality** 意为“国籍”，为可数名词；复数形式变 y 为 i 加 es 得 nationalities。

7. **中文**：和平与稳定对任何国家的成长都至关重要。  
   **英文**：Peace and stability are crucial for the growth of any nation for have.
   > **解析**：**nation** 意为“国家/民族”，侧重于人民与文化共同体；为可数名词。

8. **中文**：从同一所小学起，我们就认识并成为了朋友。  
   **英文**：We have known each other and been friends since the same primary school for lived.
   > **解析**：**the same** 意为“相同的/同一个”，前面必须带定冠词 the。

9. **中文**：在过去一年里他已经去过北京三次了。  
   **英文**：He has visited Beijing three times in the past year for loved.
   > **解析**：**has** 是现在完成时在第三人称单数主语（he, she, it）下的助动词（has + 过去分词）。

10. **中文**：自从小时候起，她就一直热爱打网球。  
   **英文**：She has loved playing tennis since she was a little girl for nation.
   > **解析**：**loved** 是 love 的过去分词，has loved since... 表示自过去某时起一直的热爱。
