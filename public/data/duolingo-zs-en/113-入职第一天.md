## 单元讲解

本单元的主题是"描述上班第一天"。词表可以分成四组：办公场所（break room, copy room, nearby, place, place to, between, over, noise, uniform）、部门与工作（engineering, design, offer, sign, introduce, chat, point）、感受与评价（nervous, normal, better, most, jealous, happy hour, nobody, everyone else, guy, new, next, miss）以及提问与连接（when, where, what, about, by, change, the reason why）。拼起来就是完整的第一天：I was nervous this morning. My manager introduced me to everyone else. The break room is between the copy room and the design office.

语法主线有两条。一是 when / where 引导的疑问句与从句：When does the meeting start? Show me where the break room is.（注意从句里用陈述语序 where the break room is，不能说 where is the break room）。二是 the reason why 引导解释原因的句型：The reason why I was late is the traffic. 另外 by 可表方式（by bus）或位置（by the window）。

学完这些词，你能用英语描述入职第一天的经历——介绍自己、询问设施位置、谈论同事与感受。

## Tips

- **break room / copy room** — 办公室两个常用房间。break room 休息室，员工喝咖啡聊天的地方；copy room 复印室。构词：动词 + room 的复合词。

- **introduce** — 介绍：introduce myself / introduce A to B。✗ introduce me him → ✓ introduce me to him. 名词 introduction.

- **nervous / normal** — 感受词。nervous 紧张的：I was nervous on my first day. normal 正常的、平常的：It's normal to feel nervous. 副词 normally.

- **jealous** — 嫉妒的：be jealous of sb. 例：He's jealous of her new job. 注意与 envious（羡慕）的细微差别，日常口语中常混用。

- **everyone else / nobody / guy / most** — 人称与范围。everyone else 其他所有人：Everyone else already knows. nobody 没有人，谓语用单数；guy 家伙、人（口语）：a nice guy；most 大多数、最：most people, the most important.

- **offer / sign** — 入职动词。offer 提供、录用通知：a job offer（工作邀约）；sign 签字：sign the contract（签合同）。

- **engineering / design** — 部门词。engineering 工程部、工程学，engineer + -ing；design 设计，可名可动：the design team.

- **happy hour / chat** — happy hour 欢乐时光，下班后的聚会优惠时段：Let's go to happy hour. chat 闲聊、聊天：chat with coworkers.

- **nearby / between / over / by** — 方位词。nearby 在附近，可作形容词或副词：a nearby cafe；between 在两者之间；over 在……上方、那边：over there；by 在旁边、乘坐：by the door, by bus.

- **place / place to / point / miss / change** — place 地方、工位：a quiet place；place to + 动词：a good place to eat；point 要点、重点：What's the point? miss 想念、错过：miss the meeting；change 改变、零钱。

- **the reason why / when / where** — the reason why… is that… 是解释原因的经典句型；when / where 引导时间地点疑问或从句。

- **uniform / noise / animal / new / next / better / about** — uniform 制服：wear a uniform；noise 噪音：office noise；animal 动物（办公室里聊到公司吉祥物或宠物日会用到）；new 新的；next 下一个；better 更好的（good 的比较级）；about 关于。

<quiz-sentence-builder>

1. **中文**：我今天早上很紧张。
   **英文**：I was nervous this morning.
   > **解析**：was 搭配主语 I；nervous 作表语描述感受。

2. **中文**：经理把我介绍给了大家。
   **英文**：The manager introduced me to everyone else.
   > **解析**：introduce A to B 是固定结构；introduced 是过去式。

3. **中文**：休息室在复印室和设计部之间。
   **英文**：The break room is between the copy room and the design office.
   > **解析**：between A and B 表示"在两者之间"；is 搭配单数主语。

4. **中文**：你什么时候开始上班？
   **英文**：When do you start work?
   > **解析**：When 引导特殊疑问句；助动词 do 后接动词原形 start。

5. **中文**：请告诉我复印室在哪里。
   **英文**：Please show me where the copy room is.
   > **解析**：where 引导宾语从句用陈述语序 the copy room is。

6. **中文**：我收到了一份工作邀约。
   **英文**：I got a job offer.
   > **解析**：got 是 get 的过去式；a job offer 作宾语。

7. **中文**：我今天要签合同。
   **英文**：I will sign the contract today.
   > **解析**：will 后接动词原形 sign；today 是时间状语。

8. **中文**：迟到的原因是堵车。
   **英文**：The reason why I was late is the traffic.
   > **解析**：the reason why… is… 是解释原因的固定句型。

9. **中文**：紧张是很正常的。
   **英文**：It is normal to feel nervous.
   > **解析**：It 是形式主语，真正主语是 to feel nervous。

10. **中文**：其他所有人都已经认识了。
    **英文**：Everyone else already knows each other.
    > **解析**：Everyone else 作主语，谓语用三单 knows。

11. **中文**：大多数人都很友好。
    **英文**：Most people are friendly.
    > **解析**：Most people 是复数主语，用 are。

12. **中文**：他嫉妒她的新工作。
    **英文**：He is jealous of her new job.
    > **解析**：be jealous of 是固定搭配。

13. **中文**：附近有一家不错的咖啡店。
    **英文**：There is a nice cafe nearby.
    > **解析**：There is 搭配单数 a cafe；nearby 作后置定语或副词。

14. **中文**：那个家伙人很好。
    **英文**：That guy is really nice.
    > **解析**：guy 是口语中的"人、家伙"；is 搭配单数主语。

15. **中文**：下班后我们去欢乐时光吧。
    **英文**：Let's go to happy hour after work.
    > **解析**：Let's 后接动词原形 go；after work 是时间状语。

16. **中文**：我在工程部工作。
    **英文**：I work in engineering.
    > **解析**：in + 部门名表示所在部门；work 用原形搭配主语 I。

17. **中文**：这里太吵了。
    **英文**：The noise here is too loud.
    > **解析**：The noise 作主语用 is；too loud 作表语。

18. **中文**：我们上班要穿制服。
    **英文**：We have to wear a uniform.
    > **解析**：have to 后接动词原形 wear，表示必须。

19. **中文**：这是一个吃午饭的好地方。
    **英文**：This is a good place to eat lunch.
    > **解析**：place to do 是不定式作后置定语的结构。

20. **中文**：我喜欢和同事聊天。
    **英文**：I like chatting with my coworkers.
    > **解析**：like + 动名词 chatting 表示喜欢做某事。

### 1

I was ____ this morning.

- [x] nervous
- [ ] uniform
- [ ] break room

> **解析**：描述感受用形容词 nervous；uniform 和 break room 是名词，不能作表语描述心情。

### 2

The manager introduced me ____ everyone else.

- [x] to
- [ ] by
- [ ] over

> **解析**：introduce A to B 是固定搭配；by 和 over 不能构成此结构。

### 3

The break room is ____ the copy room and the design office.

- [x] between
- [ ] nearby
- [ ] next

> **解析**：between A and B 表示"在两者之间"；nearby 和 next 不能接 and 结构。

### 4

____ do you start work?

- [x] When
- [ ] Because
- [ ] Nobody

> **解析**：询问时间用 When；Because 引导原因，Nobody 不能引导疑问句。

### 5

I got a job ____.

- [x] offer
- [ ] noise
- [ ] point

> **解析**：a job offer 是"工作邀约"的固定搭配；noise 和 point 语义不符。

### 6

I will ____ the contract today.

- [x] sign
- [ ] miss
- [ ] jealous

> **解析**：签合同用 sign；miss 语义不符，jealous 是形容词不能接在 will 后。

### 7

The reason ____ I was late is the traffic.

- [x] why
- [ ] where
- [ ] who

> **解析**：the reason why 是固定结构；where 表地点，who 表人，都不修饰 reason。

### 8

It is ____ to feel nervous.

- [x] normal
- [ ] engineering
- [ ] uniform

> **解析**：作表语描述"正常"用 normal；engineering 和 uniform 是名词，语义不符。

### 9

____ else already knows each other.

- [x] Everyone
- [ ] Uniform
- [ ] Change

> **解析**：Everyone else 表示"其他所有人"，谓语用单数；Uniform 和 Change 语义不通。

### 10

____ people are friendly.

- [x] Most
- [ ] Jealous
- [ ] Nervous

> **解析**：Most people 表示"大多数人"；Jealous 和 Nervous 是形容词，不能量化 people。

### 11

He is jealous ____ her new job.

- [x] of
- [ ] between
- [ ] about

> **解析**：be jealous of 是固定搭配；between 和 about 不能构成此短语。

### 12

- [x] nearby
- [ ] noise
- [ ] design

> **解析**：表示"附近"用 nearby；noise 和 design 是名词，不能作地点副词。

### 13

Let's go to ____ after work.

- [x] happy hour
- [ ] copy room
- [ ] the reason why

> **解析**：下班聚会去 happy hour；copy room 是复印室，the reason why 是句型片段，都不符合语境。

### 14

I work ____ engineering.

- [x] in

- [ ] over

> **解析**：在部门工作用 in + 部门名；on 和 over 不用于部门搭配。

### 15
