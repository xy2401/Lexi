## 单元讲解

本单元围绕"形容人物细节"展开。词表里有职业（actor, police officer, runner, singer）和家庭关系（cousin, granddaughter, grandson）。你可以练习：My cousin is an actor. / She is a popular singer. / He is a strong runner. / My grandson likes video games.

形容词描述外貌和性格：blond（金发的）, bright（明亮的）, dark（深色的）, kind（善良的）, strong（强壮的）, popular（受欢迎的）。副词 quickly / slowly 描述动作方式。

how many times 问次数：How many times have you been to the gym? free time 是"空闲时间"。

## Tips

- **cousin** — 堂/表兄弟姐妹。搭配：my cousin, a cousin of mine。注意英语中 cousin 不分堂/表、不分性别，一个词涵盖所有。中文需要说"表哥/堂妹"等，英文只需 cousin。

- **granddaughter / grandson** — 孙女 / 孙子。构词：grand- + daughter / son。搭配：my granddaughter, my grandson。注意 grand- 前缀表示"隔一代"：grandfather（祖父）, grandchild（孙辈）。

- **actor / singer / runner** — 演员 / 歌手 / 跑步者。搭配：a famous actor, a pop singer, a fast runner。注意 actor 现在男女通用（actress 逐渐少用）。-er / -or 后缀表示"做……的人"。

- **police officer** — 警察。搭配：a police officer, talk to a police officer。注意 police officer 比 policeman / policewoman 更中性、更现代。police 本身是集合名词：The police are here.

- **blond / dark** — 金发的 / 深色的。搭配：blond hair, dark hair, blond and dark。注意 blond 形容头发颜色。blond 也可以拼作 blonde（通常指女性）。dark hair = 深色头发（黑/棕）。

- **kind / popular** — 善良的 / 受欢迎的。搭配：a kind person, very popular, popular with。例句：She is kind and popular. / He is popular with students. 注意 popular with / among 表示"受……欢迎"。

- **quickly / slowly** — 快速地 / 慢慢地。搭配：run quickly, walk slowly, speak slowly。注意 -ly 后缀构成副词：quick → quickly, slow → slowly。副词修饰动词：She runs quickly.

- **how many times** — 多少次（频率）。搭配：How many times did you go? / How many times a week? 回答：Three times. / Twice a week. 注意 how many times 问具体次数，how often 问频率。

- **free time** — 空闲时间。搭配：in my free time, have free time, free time activities。例句：What do you do in your free time? 注意 free time = spare time = leisure time。

- **exercise / gym** — 锻炼 / 健身房。搭配：do exercise, go to the gym, exercise every day。注意 exercise 可数也可不可数：do exercise（泛指）/ do exercises（具体动作）。gym 是 gymnasium 的缩写。

- **board game / video game** — 桌游 / 电子游戏。搭配：play board games, play video games。注意 board game 是棋盘游戏/桌面游戏（如大富翁），video game 是电子/视频游戏。

- **awesome** — 太棒了（口语）。搭配：That's awesome! / an awesome singer。注意 awesome 是非常口语化的赞美词，比 great 更强烈。正式写作中建议用 impressive / excellent。
