## 单元讲解

本单元围绕"描述童话中的情节"展开。词表里有童话元素（castle, king, queen, monster, woods）和叙事词（one day, in the end, found, wrote, decide）。你可以练习：One day, a monster came to the castle. / The king wrote a letter. / In the end, the queen found the bracelet. / No one could kill the monster. / Stay away from the woods!

数量表达 hundreds of / thousands of / millions of 表示"数百/数千/数百万"。someone else 表示"别人"。somewhere else 表示"别处"。

probably 表示"大概"。except for 表示"除了"。

## Tips

- **one day / in the end** — 有一天 / 最终。搭配：One day, ...（故事开头）、In the end, ...（故事结尾）。例句：One day, a monster appeared. / In the end, they lived happily. 注意这是讲故事的经典框架词。

- **castle / king / queen** — 城堡 / 国王 / 王后。搭配：a big castle, the king and queen, in the castle。例句：The king lived in a castle. / The queen was missing. 注意 king 和 queen 前通常加 the。

- **monster / woods** — 怪物 / 树林。搭配：a scary monster, in the woods, stay away from the woods。例句：A monster lived in the woods. / Don't go into the woods! 注意 woods 通常用复数。

- **hundreds of / thousands of / millions of** — 数百 / 数千 / 数百万。搭配：hundreds of people, thousands of stars, millions of years。注意具体数字后不加 s：two hundred（不是 ✗ two hundreds）。但 hundreds of 要加 s。

- **someone else / somewhere else** — 别人 / 别处。搭配：ask someone else, go somewhere else。例句：Ask someone else. / Let's go somewhere else. 注意 else 放在 someone / somewhere 后面。

- **stay away** — 远离。搭配：stay away from, Stay away! 例句：Stay away from the monster! / Stay away from the woods. 注意 stay away from + 名词：Stay away from the castle.

- **found / find** — 找到了。find → found → found。搭配：found the bracelet, found a way, found in the woods。例句：The queen found the missing bracelet. / He found a castle.

- **wrote / write** — 写了。write → wrote → written。搭配：wrote a letter, wrote a story, wrote down。例句：The king wrote a letter to the queen. / She wrote a story about a monster.

- **decide** — 决定。搭配：decide to do, decide that, decided to go。例句：The king decided to kill the monster. / She decided to leave. 注意 decide to do（决定做某事）。

- **probably** — 大概、可能。搭配：probably will, probably not, It's probably...。例句：The monster is probably in the woods. / He'll probably come. 注意 probably 表示较高的可能性（比 maybe 更确定）。

- **except for** — 除了。搭配：except for one thing, everyone except for him。例句：Everyone was there except for the king. / The castle was quiet except for one sound.

- **missing / dead** — 失踪的 / 死的。搭配：the missing queen, the missing bracelet, the dead monster。例句：The queen was missing. / The monster was dead. 注意 missing 是形容词（失踪的），不是动词进行时。

<quiz-sentence-builder>

1. **中文**：智慧的国王和善良的王后治着这个和平的国家。
   **英文**：The wise king and good queen ruled the peaceful land.
   > **解析**：king 表示“国王”；queen 表示“王后/女王”。

2. **中文**：美丽的王后在城堡的花园里种植了许多玫瑰花。
   **英文**：The beautiful queen planted roses in the garden.
   > **解析**：queen 表示“王后/女王”。

3. **中文**：古老的皇家城堡矗立在幽暗的森林深处。
   **英文**：The ancient royal castle stands deep in the forest.
   > **解析**：castle 表示“城堡”。

4. **中文**：传说山洞里居住着一只守护宝藏的恐怖怪物。
   **英文**：Legend says a scary monster lived inside the cave.
   > **解析**：monster 表示“怪物/魔王”。

5. **中文**：孩子们不敢在漆黑的夜晚独自走进这片树林。
   **英文**：Kids were afraid to walk into the dark woods at night.
   > **解析**：woods 表示“树林/森林”。

6. **中文**：那棵干枯的老树在寒冷的冬天里已经彻底枯死了。
   **英文**：That old dry tree was completely dead in winter.
   > **解析**：dead 表示“死亡的/枯死的”。

7. **中文**：那位慈祥的老国王蓄着一把雪白的长胡须。
   **英文**：The kind old king has a long white beard.
   > **解析**：beard 表示“胡须/下巴胡子”。

8. **中文**：公主戴着一条用珍珠和金子打造的精致手链。
   **英文**：The princess wore a delicate pearl bracelet.
   > **解析**：bracelet 表示“手链/手镯”。

9. **中文**：每年有成百上千的游人来到这里参观古城堡。
   **英文**：Hundreds of tourists visit this old castle yearly.
   > **解析**：hundreds of 表示“成百上千的”。

10. **中文**：数以千计的鸟儿在秋天成群结队地飞往南方。
   **英文**：Thousands of birds fly south together in autumn.
   > **解析**：thousands of 表示“数以千计的”。

11. **中文**：这个古老的童话故事传唱给了数以百万计的人。
   **英文**：Millions of people have heard this famous fairy tale.
   > **解析**：millions of 表示“数以百万计的”。

12. **中文**：在这片神秘的森林深处，没有人敢轻易靠近。
   **英文**：No one dared to go deep into the mysterious woods.
   > **解析**：no one 表示“没有人”。

13. **中文**：似乎有人在迷雾笼罩的木屋窗前静静伫立。
   **英文**：Someone stood by the window of the wooden cabin.
   > **解析**：someone 表示“某人/有人”。

14. **中文**：经过艰难的考验，骑士最终成功拯救了公主。
   **英文**：In the end, the brave knight saved the princess.
   > **解析**：in the end 表示“最终/最后”。

15. **中文**：老师警示孩子们在野外要远离有毒的蛇类。
   **英文**：The teacher warned kids to stay away from snakes.
   > **解析**：stay away 表示“远离/避开”。

16. **中文**：勇敢的年轻王子决定亲自前往黑森林探险。
   **英文**：The young prince decided to explore the dark woods.
   > **解析**：decided 是 decide 的过去式，表示“决定”。

17. **中文**：著名作家在多年前写下了这本经典的童话故事。
   **英文**：The famous writer wrote this classic fairy tale.
   > **解析**：wrote 是 write 的过去式，表示“写下了”。

18. **中文**：国王在宫殿里收到了来自邻国的友好和平信件。
   **英文**：The king received a peace letter from neighbors.
   > **解析**：received 表示“收到了”。

19. **中文**：绿油油的广阔田野里盛开着五颜六色的野花。
   **英文**：Colorful wild flowers bloom in the green field.
   > **解析**：field 表示“田野/牧场”。

20. **中文**：这本充满奇幻色彩的童话给童年带来了无尽乐趣。
   **英文**：This fairy tale brought endless fun to childhood.
   > **解析**：fairy tale 表示“童话故事”。
