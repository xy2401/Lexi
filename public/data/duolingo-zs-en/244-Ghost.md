## 单元讲解

本单元的主题是"解释为什么感到不安"，场景是鬼屋探险。词表可以分成四组：恐怖意象（haunted, spirit, skeleton, peculiar, sensation, off）、动作与逃离（abandon, crawl, fall apart, get away, make for, think through）、过去时骨架（had, had been, it was no use, know better）、以及环境描写（chimney, flashlight, footstep, tight, winding, by, panic）。拼起来就是一段鬼屋叙事：The house had been abandoned for years. I heard footsteps and my heart beat tight. It was no use — I had to get away.

语法主线是过去完成时 had been / had done，表示"过去的过去"：The house had been haunted long before we arrived. 另一条主线是氛围描写词：peculiar（古怪的）、winding（蜿蜒的）、tight（发紧的）配合 off（失灵、不对劲）营造不安感。

学完这个单元，你能用英语讲述恐怖经历、描述令人不安的细节，并解释当时为什么害怕。

## Tips

- **had / had been** — 过去完成时的两个骨架。had + 过去分词表示"过去的过去"：I realized I had left my flashlight behind. had been 可接形容词或分词：The house had been empty for years. 讲鬼故事回忆"更早之前"时必用。

- **haunted / spirit / skeleton** — 鬼屋三词。haunted = 闹鬼的：a haunted house；spirit = 灵魂、鬼魂；skeleton = 骷髅。haunt 本是动词"萦绕、出没"，-ed 形式作形容词。

- **peculiar / sensation / off** — 不安感受词。peculiar = 古怪的，比 strange 更带悬疑感；sensation = 感觉：a strange sensation；off 表示"不对劲、失灵"：The lights went off. Something feels off.

- **abandon / fall apart** — abandon = 遗弃：an abandoned house（被遗弃的房子）；fall apart = 散架、崩塌：The old stairs fell apart.

- **crawl / get away / make for** — 逃离动作。crawl = 爬行：crawl through a hole；get away = 逃脱：We need to get away. make for = 朝……走去：make for the door。

- **it was no use / know better** — it was no use = 没用、徒劳，后常接 -ing：It was no use calling for help. know better = 本应更明白：I know better than to enter alone.（我不该独自进去。）

- **think through** — "仔细想清楚"：Think through the plan before entering. through 表示从头到尾想一遍。

- **flashlight / footstep / chimney** — 探险道具词。flashlight = 手电筒；footstep = 脚步声，常用复数 footsteps；chimney = 烟囱。

- **tight / winding / by** — tight 形容身体感受"发紧的"：My chest felt tight. winding = 蜿蜒的：a winding path。by 可表"在旁边"：something stood by the door，也可表"被"：frightened by the noise.

- **panic** — "惊慌"，名词动词同形：Don't panic! / She felt panic rising.

<quiz-sentence-builder>

1. **中文**：这栋房子被遗弃多年了。
   **英文**：The house had been abandoned for years.
   > **解析**：had been abandoned 是过去完成时的被动语态；for years 表示持续的时间。

2. **中文**：我们到达之前，这里早就闹鬼了。
   **英文**：The house had been haunted before we arrived.
   > **解析**：had been haunted 表示"过去的过去"；before we arrived 是过去的时间参照。

3. **中文**：我听见了脚步声。
   **英文**：I heard footsteps.
   > **解析**：heard 是 hear 的过去式；footsteps 用复数表示一连串脚步声。

4. **中文**：灯突然灭了。
   **英文**：The lights went off suddenly.
   > **解析**：go off 表示"熄灭、失灵"；went 是过去式，suddenly 是副词。

5. **中文**：我突然有一种古怪的感觉。
   **英文**：I had a peculiar sensation.
   > **解析**：peculiar 修饰 sensation 作宾语；had 是过去式。

6. **中文**：没用了，门打不开。
   **英文**：It was no use; the door wouldn't open.
   > **解析**：It was no use 表示"徒劳"；wouldn't 表示"怎么也不肯"。

7. **中文**：我们只好朝大门跑去。
   **英文**：We had to make for the main door.
   > **解析**：had to 表示过去不得不；make for 是固定短语"朝……走去"。

8. **中文**：我们得赶紧离开。
   **英文**：We needed to get away quickly.
   > **解析**：needed to + 动词原形 get away；quickly 修饰动作。

9. **中文**：我本该更清楚，不该一个人进去。
   **英文**：I knew better than to enter alone.
   > **解析**：know better than to do 是固定表达"本应更明智而不做某事"。

10. **中文**：别慌，深呼吸。
   **英文**：Don't panic; take a deep breath.
   > **解析**：Don't 后接动词原形构成否定祈使句；两个祈使句并列。

11. **中文**：楼梯在我们脚下散架了。
   **英文**：The stairs fell apart under our feet.
   > **解析**：fall apart = 散架，fell 是过去式；under our feet 是地点状语。

12. **中文**：他从窗户爬了出来。
   **英文**：He crawled out of the window.
   > **解析**：crawled 是过去式；out of the window 表示从窗户出来。

13. **中文**：手电筒的光照出了墙上的骷髅。
   **英文**：The flashlight showed a skeleton on the wall.
   > **解析**：showed 是 show 的过去式；a skeleton 作宾语。

14. **中文**：那条蜿蜒的小路通向烟囱废墟。
   **英文**：The winding path led to the old chimney.
   > **解析**：winding 作形容词"蜿蜒的"修饰 path；led 是 lead 的过去式。

15. **中文**：我的胸口发紧。
   **英文**：My chest felt tight.
   > **解析**：felt 是系动词 feel 的过去式；tight 作表语描述身体感受。

16. **中文**：鬼魂据说会在午夜出现。
   **英文**：The spirit was said to appear at midnight.
   > **解析**：was said to 是被动结构"据说……"；to appear 是不定式。

17. **中文**：进门之前先想清楚。
   **英文**：Think through the plan before entering.
   > **解析**：think through 是可拆分短语，宾语放在中间；before entering 用动名词。

18. **中文**：门边站着什么东西。
   **英文**：Something stood by the door.
   > **解析**：stood 是 stand 的过去式；by the door 表示"在门旁"。

19. **中文**：我被那声音吓到了。
   **英文**：I was frightened by the noise.
   > **解析**：was frightened 是被动语态；by the noise 引出施动者。

20. **中文**：逃跑是毫无意义的。
   **英文**：It was no use running away.
   > **解析**：It was no use + 动名词 running，表示"做……是徒劳的"。

### 1

The house had ____ abandoned for years.

- [x] been
- [ ] being
- [ ] be

> **解析**：过去完成时被动用 had been + 过去分词；being 和 be 不能与 had 构成该时态。

### 2

The lights suddenly went ____ in the hallway.

- [x] off
- [ ] of
- [ ] away

> **解析**：go off 表示"熄灭"；go away 是"走开"，主语不能是灯，of 不合结构。

### 3

I heard ____ upstairs at midnight.

- [x] footsteps
- [ ] footstep's
- [ ] footstepping

> **解析**：指一串脚步声用复数 footsteps；所有格和动名词形式都不合语义。

### 4

There was a ____ smell in the old room.

- [x] peculiar
- [ ] peculiarly
- [ ] peculiarity

> **解析**：修饰名词 smell 需要形容词 peculiar；peculiarly 是副词，peculiarity 是名词。

### 5

It was no ____ calling for help.

- [x] use
- [ ] useful
- [ ] used

> **解析**：It was no use + 动名词是固定结构；useful 是形容词，不能进入该结构。

### 6

We made ____ the exit as fast as we could.

- [x] for
- [ ] to
- [ ] at

> **解析**：make for 是固定短语"朝……走去"；to 和 at 不能构成该短语。

### 7

They ran fast and finally got ____.

- [x] away
- [ ] out to
- [ ] off of

> **解析**：get away 是固定短语"逃脱"；out to 和 off of 结构错误。

### 8

You should know ____ than to open that door.

- [x] better
- [ ] good
- [ ] best

> **解析**：know better than to do 是固定表达；good 和 best 不能构成该结构。

### 9

Don't ____; we can still find a way out.

- [x] panic
- [ ] panicked
- [ ] panicking

> **解析**：Don't 后接动词原形 panic；其余两项不符合祈使句要求。

### 10

The old bridge fell ____ when we crossed it.

- [x] apart
- [ ] away
- [ ] aside

> **解析**：fall apart 是固定短语"散架"；fall away 和 fall aside 无此含义。

### 11

He had to ____ through the narrow hole.

- [x] crawl
- [ ] crawled
- [ ] crawling

> **解析**：had to 后接动词原形 crawl；其余两项不符合不定式要求。

### 12

The ____ lit up a white skeleton.

- [x] flashlight
- [ ] flashlights
- [ ] flashing

> **解析**：The 后接单数名词 flashlight；flashing 是动名词，复数与 lit up 语义不搭。

### 13

A ____ staircase led down into the dark.

- [x] winding
- [ ] wind
- [ ] winded

> **解析**：修饰 staircase 需要形容词 winding（蜿蜒的）；wind 是名词或动词，winded 意为"喘不过气"。

### 14

My chest felt ____ when I saw the shadow.

- [x] tight
- [ ] tightly
- [ ] tighten

> **解析**：felt 后需要形容词 tight 作表语；tightly 是副词，tighten 是动词。

### 15

The ____ of the house appeared at night.

- [x] spirit
- [ ] spiritual
- [ ] spirited

> **解析**：The 后需要名词 spirit（鬼魂）；spiritual 是形容词，spirited 是形容词"有生气的"。

### 16

Please think ____ the plan before we go in.

- [x] through
- [ ] though
- [ ] thorough

> **解析**：think through 是固定短语"想清楚"；though 是连词，thorough 是形容词。

### 17

A cold wind came in through the broken ____.

- [x] chimney
- [ ] chimneys
- [ ] chimning

> **解析**：the broken 后接单数名词 chimney；chimning 不是正确构词。

### 18

The family ____ the house in a hurry.

- [x] abandoned
- [ ] abandon
- [ ] abandoning

> **解析**：叙述过去事件用过去式 abandoned；其余两项不能单独作谓语。

### 19

Something stood silently ____ the door.

- [x] by
- [ ] in
- [ ] on

> **解析**：by the door 表示"在门旁"；in 和 on 不表示"在旁边"。

### 20

We realized the noise had ____ coming from the attic.

- [x] been
- [ ] being
- [ ] be

> **解析**：过去完成进行时 had been coming；being 和 be 不能与 had 构成该时态。
