# Unit 284: Olympics

> Olympics: Describe athlete achievements

## 单词

anticipated, australian, brazilian, break, bronze, close, determination, expected to, following, gather, in a few short, inspiring, looking ahead to, medalist, national anthem, now that, olympic, on the horizon, opening ceremony, place, podium, qualify, set to, track and field, ultimate, upcoming

## 单元讲解

本单元围绕"描述运动员的成就"展开，场景是奥运会。词表里有奥运特有的词汇（olympic, medalist, podium, bronze, national anthem, opening ceremony, track and field），有描述赛事进程的表达（qualify, anticipated, expected to, set to, upcoming, on the horizon），也有赞美运动员品质的词（determination, inspiring, ultimate）。你可以练习这样的叙述：The opening ceremony is just around the corner—athletes from around the world are gathering, and the most anticipated event is track and field. / Now that she's qualified, she's set to compete for a bronze medal. / Looking ahead to the next Games, there are inspiring young athletes on the horizon.

词表里有几组表达"即将到来"的词值得注意：upcoming（即将到来的）、on the horizon（在地平线上，即即将出现）、anticipated（备受期待的）、set to（准备好要……）。这些表达在体育新闻中极其常见。break 在这里不是"打破"，而是"打破纪录"（break a record）的省略。

## Tips

- **podium** — 领奖台。搭配：stand on the podium / reach the podium / a podium finish。奥运会上前三名站的高低不同的台子，金牌站最高处。例句：She stood on the podium and listened to the national anthem. 注意 podium 原义是"讲台"，在体育语境中专指领奖台。

- **medalist** — 奖牌获得者。搭配：a gold medalist / a bronze medalist / an Olympic medalist。注意拼写：medal + -ist。美式拼写是 medalist，英式有时写 medallist。例句：She became the youngest medalist in track and field.

- **national anthem** — 国歌。搭配：play the national anthem / sing the national anthem / stand for the national anthem。例句：When the gold medalist's national anthem played, she cried. anthem 原义是"圣歌"，national anthem 特指国歌。

- **opening ceremony** — 开幕式。搭配：the opening ceremony / attend the opening ceremony / the Olympic opening ceremony。例句：The opening ceremony gathered athletes from every country. 反义：closing ceremony（闭幕式）。注意 ceremony 的拼写：cer-e-mo-ny。

- **track and field** — 田径运动。track（跑道）指跑步项目，field（场地）指跳跃和投掷项目。搭配：a track and field event / compete in track and field / a track and field star。这是美式说法，英式更常说 athletics。

- **qualify** — 获得参赛资格、达标。搭配：qualify for the Olympics / qualify for the finals / fail to qualify。例句：She qualified for the Games in a few short months. 名词是 qualification。注意 qualify 不是"合格"那么简单，在体育语境中特指"达到参赛标准"。

- **anticipated / expected to / set to** — 三个表达"预期"的词。anticipated = 备受期待的：the most anticipated event。expected to = 预计会：She's expected to win gold. set to = 准备好要、即将：The Games are set to begin. 语气从期待到确定递增。

- **on the horizon / upcoming** — on the horizon = 即将出现、初露端倪：New talent is on the horizon. upcoming = 即将到来的：the upcoming Games / an upcoming event。例句：Looking ahead to the upcoming season, there are exciting changes on the horizon. on the horizon 更有画面感（地平线上出现）。

- **determination / inspiring** — determination = 决心、毅力：show great determination / her determination paid off。inspiring = 鼓舞人心的：an inspiring story / an inspiring athlete。例句：Her determination to qualify was truly inspiring. 注意 determine（动词）→ determination（名词）→ determined（形容词）的词族。

- **break** — 这里指"打破（纪录）"：break a record / break the world record。例句：She broke the Olympic record in track and field. 注意 break 的过去式是 broke，过去分词是 broken。break a record 是体育新闻中的固定搭配。

- **gather / following / close** — gather = 聚集：Athletes gathered for the opening ceremony. following = 接下来的、随后的：the following day / in the following weeks。close = 接近的、势均力敌的：a close race / a close finish。例句：It was a close race, and the following day, everyone gathered to celebrate.

- **ultimate / bronze / place** — ultimate = 最终的、最高的：the ultimate goal / the ultimate achievement。bronze = 铜牌（第三名）：a bronze medal / win bronze。place = 名次：finish in first place / take third place。例句：Her ultimate goal was a bronze medal, but she finished in first place!

- **now that / looking ahead to** — now that = 既然、现在：Now that she's qualified, she can focus on training. looking ahead to = 展望未来：Looking ahead to the next Olympics...。例句：Now that the Games are over, we're already looking ahead to the next ones. now that 引出已成事实，looking ahead to 引出未来期待。

- **in a few short** — 在短短……之内。搭配：in a few short weeks / in a few short months / in a few short years。例句：In a few short months, she went from unknown to Olympic medalist. short 在这里强调"时间短得惊人"，带有感叹语气。

- **australian / brazilian** — 澳大利亚的/巴西的。注意国籍形容词首字母大写：an Australian swimmer / a Brazilian runner。做名词时指人：an Australian / the Brazilians。在奥运语境中常用于介绍运动员国籍。
