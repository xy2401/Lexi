# Unit 061: 个人物品 2

> 描述家居用品

## 单词

anywhere, between, blanket, bookshelf, closet, comfortable, difference, doll, drawer, everywhere, glove, hers, in, large, larger, largest, mine, on, on the left, on the right, pillow, plastic, pocket, purple, share, sock, some of, somewhere, sweater, toy, wallet, yours

## 单元讲解

本单元围绕"描述家居用品"展开。词表里有家居物品（blanket, bookshelf, closet, drawer, pillow）和衣物（glove, sock, sweater）。你可以练习：The blanket is on the bed. / My socks are in the drawer. / The bookshelf is between the closet and the door. / This pillow is very comfortable.

名词性物主代词出现：mine, yours, hers。这些代替"形容词性物主代词 + 名词"：This wallet is mine.（= my wallet）/ The sweater is hers.（= her sweater）

large → larger → largest 是比较级和最高级。on the left / on the right 描述位置。

## Tips

- **mine / yours / hers** — 名词性物主代词。搭配：This is mine. / Is this yours? / The bag is hers. 注意它们代替"my + 名词"：This wallet is mine. = This is my wallet. 完整的：mine, yours, his, hers, ours, theirs。

- **blanket / pillow** — 毯子 / 枕头。搭配：a warm blanket, under the blanket, a soft pillow。注意 blanket 和 quilt（被子）的区别：blanket 是毯子（较薄），quilt 是被子（有填充物）。

- **closet / drawer** — 壁橱 / 抽屉。搭配：in the closet, open the drawer, put it in the drawer。注意 closet 是嵌入墙内的储物空间（美式），wardrobe 是独立的衣柜（英式）。

- **bookshelf** — 书架。搭配：on the bookshelf, a wooden bookshelf。构词：book + shelf。注意 shelf 的复数是 shelves（不规则）：two shelves。类似的：wife → wives。

- **comfortable** — 舒适的。搭配：a comfortable chair, very comfortable, comfortable shoes。缩写 comfy（口语）。反义词 uncomfortable。注意发音：/ˈkʌmftəbl/（常省略中间音节）。

- **larger / largest** — 更大 / 最大。large → larger → largest。搭配：a larger room, the largest closet。注意 large 的比较级直接加 -r / -st（以 e 结尾）。large 和 big 同义，但 large 稍正式。

- **on the left / on the right** — 在左边 / 在右边。搭配：on the left of, on the right side。例句：The bookshelf is on the left. / The closet is on the right. 注意 left 和 right 前加 the。

- **between** — 在……之间。搭配：between A and B, between the closet and the drawer。例句：The pillow is between the blankets. 注意 between 用于两者之间，among 用于三者及以上。

- **share** — 分享、共用。搭配：share a room, share with, share the closet。例句：I share the closet with my sister. 注意 share 后接人或物：share something with someone。

- **plastic** — 塑料的、塑料。搭配：a plastic bag, plastic toy, made of plastic。注意 plastic 既是形容词也是名词。plastic 作名词时通常不可数：made of plastic。

- **anywhere / everywhere / somewhere** — 任何地方 / 到处 / 某处。搭配：put it anywhere, look everywhere, somewhere nice。注意 some- 用于肯定句，any- 用于疑问/否定句：Is it anywhere? / It's somewhere in the drawer.

- **glove / sock / sweater** — 手套 / 袜子 / 毛衣。搭配：a pair of gloves, a pair of socks, wear a sweater。注意 glove 和 sock 通常成对出现：a pair of gloves / socks。sweater 美式，英式用 jumper。
