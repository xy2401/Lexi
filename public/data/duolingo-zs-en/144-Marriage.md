## 单元讲解

本单元围绕"询问婚礼细节"展开。核心是婚姻和关系表达：They've been in a relationship for three years, and now they're planning their honeymoon.（他们在一起三年了，现在正在计划蜜月。）、Have you set a date for the wedding?（你们定好婚礼日期了吗？）、It's a tradition to wave at the guests as you leave.（离开时向宾客挥手是传统。）、Their anniversary is more peaceful than a typical wedding event.（他们的纪念日比典型的婚礼活动更宁静。）。

词表里有关系词汇（in a relationship, honeymoon, anniversary）和频率副词（rarely, more often, lately）。你可以练习：They haven't set the date yet. / She has an incredible story. / It's nice and quiet. / He rarely talks about it.

have/has + 过去分词构成现在完成时。than 用于比较级。

## Tips

- **honeymoon / anniversary / in a relationship** — 蜜月 / 纪念日 / 在恋爱中。搭配：go on a honeymoon, celebrate an anniversary, be in a relationship。例句：They went to Paris for their honeymoon. / Happy anniversary! / They've been in a relationship for two years. 注意 honeymoon 是婚后旅行。

- **have / has / haven't / hasn't** — 有 / 有（三单）/ 没有。搭配：have you set...? / has she...? / haven't done, hasn't arrived。例句：Have you planned the wedding? / Has she chosen a dress? / We haven't decided yet. / He hasn't called. 注意这些构成现在完成时。

- **tradition / typical / event** — 传统 / 典型的 / 活动。搭配：a tradition, a typical wedding, a special event。例句：It's a Chinese tradition. / A typical wedding has 100 guests. / What an incredible event! 注意 tradition 是名词，traditional 是形容词。

- **rarely / more often / lately** — 很少 / 更经常 / 最近。搭配：rarely see, more often than not, lately。例句：They rarely go out. / I see them more often now. / Have you been busy lately? 注意 rarely = almost never。

- **set / wave / van** — 设定 / 挥手 / 面包车。搭配：set a date, wave goodbye, a van。例句：Have you set the date? / She waved at the guests. / They rented a van. 注意 set 的过去式还是 set（不规则）。

- **incredible / peaceful / nice and** — 难以置信的 / 宁静的 / 又…又…。搭配：incredible! / a peaceful place, nice and warm。例句：The view was incredible! / The garden is peaceful. / It's nice and quiet here. 注意 nice and + 形容词 = 令人愉快地…。

- **than / typical / tradition** — 比 / 典型 / 传统。搭配：more than, a typical day, by tradition。例句：It's bigger than I expected. / A typical ceremony lasts an hour. / By tradition, the bride wears white. 注意 than 用于比较级之后。

- **did / event / anniversary** — 做了 / 活动 / 周年。搭配：Did you go? / a big event, a wedding anniversary。例句：Did you enjoy the wedding? / It was a wonderful event. / It's their 10th anniversary. 注意 anniversary 指结婚周年。

- **peaceful / incredible / honeymoon** — 宁静 / 不可思议 / 蜜月。搭配：a peaceful evening, an incredible story, a honeymoon trip。例句：The ceremony was peaceful. / The decorations were incredible. / Where did you go for your honeymoon? 注意这些描述婚礼相关场景。

- **more often / rarely / lately** — 更常 / 很少 / 最近。搭配：see more often, rarely happen, until lately。例句：We meet more often now. / Problems rarely happen. / I haven't seen them lately. 注意 lately 常用于完成时。

- **wave / set / nice and** — 挥手 / 设定 / 舒适地。搭配：wave at, set up, nice and clean。例句：Wave at the photographer! / They set the date for June. / The venue is nice and spacious. 注意 wave at sb = 向某人挥手。

- **haven't / hasn't / have / has** — 没有 / 没有（三单）/ 有。搭配：haven't planned, hasn't decided, have you...? / has she...? 例句：We haven't booked the venue. / She hasn't chosen flowers. / Have you invited everyone? 注意 haven't = have not。

<quiz-sentence-builder>

1. **中文**：这对夫妇举办家庭晚宴庆祝他们的银婚纪念日。  
   **英文**：The couple celebrated their silver wedding anniversary with a family dinner party.
   > **解析**：anniversary 为名词，意为“周年纪念日”。wedding anniversary 意为“结婚纪念日”。silver wedding anniversary 指“银婚（25周年）纪念日”。

2. **中文**：婚礼结束后你们计划去哪里度蜜月？  
   **英文**：Where do you plan to go for your honeymoon after the wedding ceremony.
   > **解析**：honeymoon 为名词，意为“蜜月”。go for a honeymoon 表示“去度蜜月”。

3. **中文**：你目前是在恋爱中，还是在享受单身生活？  
   **英文**：Are you currently in a relationship, or are you enjoying your single life.
   > **解析**：in a relationship 为固定介词短语，作表语，意为“处于恋爱关系中”。

4. **中文**：一起切婚礼蛋糕是许多文化中共同拥有的甜蜜传统。  
   **英文**：Cutting the wedding cake together is a sweet tradition shared by many cultures.
   > **解析**：tradition 为名词，意为“传统”。shared by... 为过去分词短语作后置定语。形容词为 traditional。

5. **中文**：他们选择了一个宁静的湖畔度假村来举办他们亲密的婚礼仪式。  
   **英文**：They chose a peaceful lakeside resort to host their intimate marriage ceremony.
   > **解析**：peaceful 为形容词，意为“宁静的、和平的”。修饰 resort（度假村）。intimate 意为“亲密的/小范围的”。

6. **中文**：当新娘步入装饰一新的宴会厅时，她看起来美极了/令人难以置信地棒。  
   **英文**：The bride looked incredible as she stepped into the decorated banquet hall.
   > **解析**：incredible 为形容词，意为“极好的、难以置信的”。在句中作系动词 looked 的表语。

7. **中文**：你最近听到了关于他们即将来临的婚礼安排的任何消息吗？  
   **英文**：Have you heard any news about their upcoming wedding arrangements lately.
   > **解析**：lately 为副词，意为“近来、最近”。常用于完成时态的疑问句或否定句中（Have you heard... lately?）。

8. **中文**：那种安静的新郎很少在公开场合发言，但他今晚做了一场感人的演讲。  
   **英文**：The quiet groom rarely speaks in public, but he made a moving speech tonight.
   > **解析**：rarely 为频度副词，意为“很少、罕见地”。在句中修饰动词 speaks。含有否定意味。

9. **中文**：在你老家文化中，典型的婚礼菜单是什么样的？  
   **英文**：What is a typical wedding menu like in your hometown culture?
   > **解析**：typical 为形容词，意为“典型的、特有的”。what is ... like? 用于询问某种情况的特征。

10. **中文**：组织一场盛大的婚礼是一项需要周密规划的重大活动。  
   **英文**：Organizing a grand wedding is a major event that requires careful planning.
   > **解析**：event 为名词，意为“活动、盛事”。major event 表示“重大活动”。require careful planning 意为“需要周密规划”。
