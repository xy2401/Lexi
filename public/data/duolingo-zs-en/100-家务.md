## 单元讲解

本单元围绕"描述家务活"展开。核心是家务表达：Mop the floor!（拖地！）、Fill the bucket with water.（把桶装满水。）、Clean the cabinet.（擦柜子。）、I have got to do the things before lunchtime.（午饭前我得把东西做完。）。

词表里有家居物品（bucket, cabinet, carpet, ceiling, curtain, lamp, washing machine）和家务动词（mop, fill, clean, look after）。你可以练习：You have got to mop the carpet. / Let me look after the things. / Get dressed before breakfast! / Why are you shouting?

have got to 表示"必须"（口语中常缩写为 have gotta）。ourselves / themselves / yourselves 是反身代词。

## Tips

- **have got to / has got to** — 必须。搭配：have got to do, has got to go。例句：I've got to clean the house. / She's got to mop the floor. 注意 have got to = have to（更口语），缩写 've got to / 's got to。

- **mop / bucket** — 拖把（拖地）/ 桶。搭配：mop the floor, a mop and bucket, fill the bucket。例句：Mop the kitchen floor! / Fill the bucket with water. 注意 mop 既是名词（拖把）也是动词（拖地）。

- **carpet / ceiling** — 地毯 / 天花板。搭配：clean the carpet, vacuum the carpet, the ceiling is high。例句：The carpet is dirty—vacuum it! / Look at the ceiling! 注意 carpet 是铺在地上的地毯（wall-to-wall），rug 是小块地毯。

- **cabinet / curtain** — 柜子 / 窗帘。搭配：open the cabinet, in the cabinet, close the curtain, draw the curtain。例句：The cleaner is in the cabinet. / Close the curtains—it's dark. 注意 draw the curtain = 拉上/拉开窗帘。

- **washing machine / cleaner** — 洗衣机 / 清洁剂。搭配：put it in the washing machine, use the cleaner。例句：Put the shorts in the washing machine. / Use the cleaner on the carpet. 注意 cleaner 可指清洁剂，也可指清洁工。

- **fill / look after** — 装满 / 照顾。搭配：fill the bucket, fill with water, look after the kids。例句：Fill the bucket with water. / Can you look after the cat? 注意 look after = take care of。

- **ourselves / themselves / yourselves** — 我们自己 / 他们自己 / 你们自己。搭配：by ourselves, by themselves, by yourselves。例句：We cleaned the house ourselves. / They did it themselves. / Did you do it yourselves? 注意反身代词用于强调"亲自"。

- **get dressed / before** — 穿好衣服 / 在……之前。搭配：get dressed quickly, before breakfast, before lunchtime。例句：Get dressed before breakfast! / Finish this before lunchtime. 注意 get dressed 表示"穿好衣服"的动作。

- **let / lie** — 让 / 躺。搭配：let me help, let go, lie down, lie on the bed。例句：Let me mop the floor. / Don't lie on the carpet! 注意 lie（躺）的过去式是 lay，别和 lie（说谎）混淆。

- **lamp / things** — 灯 / 东西。搭配：turn on the lamp, a table lamp, do things, put things away。例句：Turn on the lamp—it's dark. / Put your things away! 注意 things 常指"物品/东西"。

- **at least / a bit** — 至少 / 一点。搭配：at least once, at least do this, a bit dirty。例句：At least mop the kitchen! / The carpet is a bit dirty. 注意 at least 表示"起码、至少"。

- **shout / why / what** — 喊叫 / 为什么 / 什么。搭配：stop shouting, Why are you shouting? / What are you doing? 例句：Don't shout at me! / Why are you shouting? / What do you want? 注意 shout at sb = 冲某人喊。
