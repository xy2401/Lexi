## 单元讲解

本单元围绕"建议户外活动"展开，场景是探险和野外运动。词表里有具体的活动（rafting, trekking, safari, attempt to reach the summit）和自然环境词汇（canyon, trail, wild, daylight），也有描述条件和安全的表达（provided, so long as, assuming that, conditions, poisonous, dehydrated, overdo it）。你可以练习这样的提议：How about we go rafting, provided the conditions are safe? / We could attempt to reach the summit, so long as we have enough gear and don't overdo it. / Assuming that we start at daylight, we should be able to finish the trail before dark.

词表里有几组表达"条件"的连词值得注意：provided 和 so long as 都表示"只要"，比 if 更强调前提条件；assuming that 表示"假设、假定"，用于提出一个前提。这些表达在提议户外活动时特别实用，因为户外计划总是依赖于天气、体能等条件。

## Tips

- **provided / so long as / assuming that** — 三个条件表达。provided = 只要、前提是：We can go rafting provided the water level is safe. so long as = 只要（语气稍口语化）：So long as we have enough water, we'll be fine. assuming that = 假设、假定：Assuming that the weather holds, we'll reach the summit by noon. provided 和 so long as 后接真实条件，assuming that 更侧重"姑且认为"。

- **rafting** — 漂流、筏运。搭配：go rafting / white-water rafting / a rafting trip。例句：How about we go rafting this weekend? 注意 white-water rafting（激流漂流）是最常见的搭配。raft 是名词"筏"，rafting 是活动。

- **trekking** — 徒步旅行、长途跋涉。搭配：go trekking / a trekking trail / trekking gear。比 hiking 更强调长距离、多日、有难度的步行。例句：We spent three days trekking through the canyon. 注意 trekking 双写 k。

- **safari** — 野生动物观赏之旅、游猎。来自阿拉伯语（意为"旅行"），在英语中专指在非洲等地观赏野生动物的旅行。搭配：go on a safari / a safari park / a photo safari。例句：On the safari, we saw a leopard in the wild.

- **summit** — 山顶、顶峰。搭配：reach the summit / climb to the summit / the summit of the mountain。例句：We attempted to reach the summit before daylight. 比喻义：a summit meeting（峰会，国家领导人之间的最高级别会议）。

- **canyon** — 峡谷。搭配：a deep canyon / the Grand Canyon / hike through a canyon。例句：The trail follows the canyon for miles. 注意和 valley（山谷）区分：canyon 更深、更窄、两侧是陡峭的岩壁；valley 更宽、更平缓。

- **dehydrated** — 脱水的。de-（去除）+ hydrated（含水的）。搭配：get dehydrated / feel dehydrated / severely dehydrated。例句：You'll get dehydrated if you don't drink enough water on the trail. 名词是 dehydration。户外运动中最重要的安全提醒之一。

- **overdo it** — 做过头、太拼命。搭配：don't overdo it / overdo it on the first day / tend to overdo it。例句：You're out of shape—don't overdo it on your first trek. 口语中极常用，提醒别人（或自己）不要过度。

- **poisonous** — 有毒的。搭配：poisonous snakes / poisonous plants / a poisonous spider。例句：Be careful—some plants on this trail are poisonous. 注意和 venomous 区分：poisonous 指"吃了/碰了会中毒"，venomous 指"会主动注射毒液"（如毒蛇咬人）。口语中两者常混用。

- **gear** — 装备、器材。搭配：camping gear / trekking gear / pack your gear / gear up。例句：Make sure you have the right gear before we attempt the summit. 不可数名词。比 equipment 更口语化，更常用于户外运动语境。

- **picturesque** — 风景如画的。搭配：a picturesque village / a picturesque view / picturesque scenery。例句：The canyon is absolutely picturesque at sunset. 来自 picture（画）+ -esque（……风格的），字面意思是"像画一样的"。比 beautiful 更强调视觉上的构图美。

- **daylight** — 日光、白天。搭配：at daylight / before daylight / in daylight / daylight hours。例句：We need to start at daylight to finish the trail in time. 注意和 daytime（白天）区分：daylight 更强调"有光线的时间段"，常用于户外语境。

- **attempt / attempt to / try to** — attempt 比 try 更正式，暗示"有难度的尝试"。attempt to reach the summit = 尝试登顶（有挑战性）。try to 更日常：Try to drink more water. attempt 做名词：an attempt to / make an attempt。例句：Our attempt to reach the summit failed due to conditions.

- **be able to / enough to / too** — be able to = 能够（比 can 更正式，可用于更多时态）：We'll be able to see the leopard. enough to = 足够……以至于：strong enough to climb / enough water to last。too = 太……（常含负面）：too tired to continue / too dangerous。三者常搭配使用：We need to be strong enough to attempt the climb, but not too tired to enjoy the view.

- **wild / conditions / trail** — wild = 野生的、野外的：wild animals / in the wild / wild leopard。conditions = 条件、状况（常用复数）：weather conditions / trail conditions / safe conditions。trail = 小径、步道：a hiking trail / follow the trail / a narrow trail。例句：The trail conditions are good, and we might see wild animals.

<quiz-sentence-builder>

1. **中文**：只要天气保持晴朗，我们明天就可以开始徒步。
   **英文**：Provided the weather stays clear, we can start our hike tomorrow.
   > **解析**：Provided 表示“只要、前提是”。

2. **中文**：只要我们紧跟在一起，就不会迷路。
   **英文**：So long as we stick together, we won't lose our way.
   > **解析**：So long as 表达前提条件，意为“只要”。

3. **中文**：假设步道是开放的，我们将在中午前到达。
   **英文**：Assuming that the trail is open, we will arrive by noon.
   > **解析**：Assuming that 表示“假设、假定”。

4. **中文**：我们这周末去河上漂流怎么样？
   **英文**：How about we go rafting on the river this weekend?
   > **解析**：rafting 表示漂流活动。

5. **中文**：他们决定去穿越整条山脉徒步旅行。
   **英文**：They decided to go trekking across the mountain range.
   > **解析**：trekking 表示徒步旅行、长途跋涉。

6. **中文**：我们在非洲游猎观赏野生动物时看见了一头野生花豹。
   **英文**：We saw a wild leopard while on safari in Africa.
   > **解析**：safari 专指野外观赏野生动物的旅行。

7. **中文**：登山者们尝试登顶山峰。
   **英文**：The climbers made an attempt to reach the summit.
   > **解析**：reach the summit 表示达到顶峰、登顶。

8. **中文**：这条河流穿过一条又深又窄的峡谷。
   **英文**：The river flows through a deep and narrow canyon.
   > **解析**：canyon 指两侧峭壁陡峭的峡谷。

9. **中文**：一定要喝水，这样你才不会脱水。
   **英文**：Make sure to drink water so you don't get dehydrated.
   > **解析**：dehydrated 表示脱水的。

10. **中文**：经常休息，这样你就不会运动过头。
   **英文**：Take breaks frequently so that you don't overdo it.
   > **解析**：overdo it 表示做事过头、太拼命。

11. **中文**：小心点，因为这个灌木丛上的某些浆果是有毒的。
   **英文**：Be careful because some berries on this bush are poisonous.
   > **解析**：poisonous 表示有毒的。

12. **中文**：出发前仔细检查你的露营装备。
   **英文**：Double-check your camping gear before setting off.
   > **解析**：gear 不可数名词，指装备、器材。

13. **中文**：这座山村展现出了山谷风光如画的景观。
   **英文**：The mountain village offers a picturesque view of the valley.
   > **解析**：picturesque 形容风景像画一样美丽。

14. **中文**：我们需要在仍有日光的时候尽早出发。
   **英文**：We need to set off early while there is still daylight.
   > **解析**：daylight 指日光、白天。

15. **中文**：你必须足够强壮才能背动自己的背包。
   **英文**：You must be strong enough to carry your own backpack.
   > **解析**：enough to 表示“足够……以至于可以做某事”。

16. **中文**：我们应当能够在日落前到达营地。
   **英文**：We should be able to reach the camp before sunset.
   > **解析**：be able to 表示“能够”。

17. **中文**：阳光太刺眼了，不戴太阳镜根本无法行走。
   **英文**：The sun is too bright to walk without sunglasses.
   > **解析**：too... to... 表示“太……以至于不能……”。

18. **中文**：在步道上你绝不应该喂食野生动物。
   **英文**：You should never feed wild animals on the trail.
   > **解析**：wild 形容词，表示野生的。

19. **中文**：雨后步道的状况出人意料地良好。
   **英文**：The trail conditions were surprisingly good after the rain.
   > **解析**：conditions 常用复数，表示路况、环境状况。

20. **中文**：紧跟标记好的步道，这样你在森林里才能保持安全。
   **英文**：Follow the marked trail so you stay safe in the forest.
   > **解析**：trail 表示山路、步道。
