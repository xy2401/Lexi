## 单元讲解

本单元围绕"买快餐"展开。核心句型包括：I'd like to order a burger.（我想点一个汉堡。）、What does it come with?（配什么？）、Here's your fried rice.（这是您的炒饭。）、Would you like anything else?（还要别的吗？）、Maybe some hot chocolate.（也许来杯热巧克力。）。

词表里有快餐食物（burger, fries, fried rice, dim sum, hot chocolate, ice water）和点餐用语（order, come with, here's, i'd like to）。你可以练习：I'd like a kids' meal. / Most of the dishes are fried. / Let's order during our lunch break.

most of / many of / all of 表示数量。

## Tips

- **i'd like to / order** — 我想要 / 点餐。搭配：I'd like to order, I'd like a burger, order some fries。例句：I'd like to order a burger and fries. / Can I order now? 注意 I'd like 比 I want 更礼貌。

- **come with** — 附带、配。搭配：What does it come with? / comes with fries, comes with a drink。例句：The burger comes with fries. / What does the kids' meal come with? 注意 come with 表示"包含/附带"。

- **here's** — 这是（here is 的缩写）。搭配：Here's your order. / Here's your change. 例句：Here's your fried rice. / Here's your hot chocolate. 注意 here's 后接单数，here are 后接复数。

- **fried / fries** — 油炸的 / 薯条。搭配：fried rice, fried chicken, french fries。例句：I'd like fried rice. / A large fries, please. 注意 fries 是 french fries 的缩写（美式）。英式用 chips。

- **burger** — 汉堡。搭配：a cheeseburger, a beef burger, order a burger。注意 burger 是 hamburger 的缩写。搭配：burger and fries 是经典组合。

- **dim sum** — 点心（粤式）。搭配：have dim sum, a dim sum restaurant。注意 dim sum 来自粤语"点心"的音译，在英文中特指粤式早茶点心。通常用复数概念但形式不变。

- **kids' meal** — 儿童套餐。搭配：a kids' meal, order a kids' meal。例句：I'd like a kids' meal for my son. / The kids' meal comes with a toy. 注意 kids' 是复数所有格。

- **lunch break** — 午休。搭配：on my lunch break, during lunch break, take a lunch break。例句：Let's get fast food on our lunch break. / I only have a short lunch break.

- **most of / many of / all of** — 大部分 / 许多 / 全部。搭配：most of the dishes, many of them, all of the food。例句：Most of the menu is fried. / I tried many of the dishes. 注意 of 后接 the / these / my 等限定词。

- **maybe / or / else** — 也许 / 或者 / 其他。搭配：maybe some fries, burger or fries, anything else。例句：Maybe some hot chocolate. / Do you want anything else? / Burger or fried rice?

- **hot chocolate / ice water** — 热巧克力 / 冰水。搭配：a hot chocolate, a glass of ice water。例句：I'd like a hot chocolate. / Can I get some ice water? 注意 ice water 是美式（英式说 iced water）。

- **see you soon** — 回头见。搭配：See you soon! / See you later. 例句：Thanks for lunch! See you soon! 注意 see you soon 比 goodbye 更随意、更友好。soon 表示"很快"。

<quiz-sentence-builder>

1. **中文**：我们所有的汉堡包都是每天用新鲜牛肉现做的。  
   **英文**：All of our burgers are made with fresh organic beef daily.
   > **解析**：**all of** 是固定数量代词短语，意为“所有的、全部的”，后接代词或带限定词的复数名词（our burgers），作主语。

2. **中文**：这款套餐里面随附薯条和饮料吗？  
   **英文**：Does this meal combo come with French fries and a drink.
   > **解析**：**come with** 是固定动词短语，意为“附带、配有”，在快餐点餐询问套餐包含内容时高频使用。

3. **中文**：过于频繁地吃快餐对你的身体健康有害。  
   **英文**：Eating fast food too frequently is bad for your health.
   > **解析**：**fast food** 作不可数名词意为“快餐、速食”。eat fast food（吃快餐）在句中作动名词短语中的宾语。

4. **中文**：我在取餐柜台点了一份鸡肉炒饭。  
   **英文**：I ordered a plate of chicken fried rice at the counter.
   > **解析**：**fried rice** 作不可数名词短语意为“炒饭”。a plate of fried rice 表示“一盘炒饭”，为快餐经典主食搭配。

5. **中文**：在寒冷的冬日下午，喝一杯热乎乎的热巧克力很暖身。  
   **英文**：A cup of warm hot chocolate is comforting on a winter day (come with).
   > **解析**：**hot chocolate** 作不可数名词短语意为“热巧克力”。a cup of hot chocolate 表达热饮消费需求。

6. **中文**：趁着四十分钟的午休时间，我们去快速吃一口吧。  
   **英文**：Let us grab a quick bite during our lunch break (dim sum).
   > **解析**：**lunch break** 作可数名词短语意为“午休时间”。during one's lunch break 表示“在午休期间”，解释吃快餐的原因。

7. **中文**：大部分顾客更倾向于在数字自助点餐机上下单。  
   **英文**：Most of the customers prefer ordering through the kiosk.
   > **解析**：**most of** 是限定短语，意为“大多数的、大部分的”，后接带有定冠词 the 的复数名词 the customers 作主语。

8. **中文**：你的鸡块需要搭配一些额外的番茄酱吗？  
   **英文**：Would you like some extra ketchup with your chicken nuggets.
   > **解析**：**some** 作限定词意为“一些”。在疑问句中表示期望得到肯定回答或客气征询对方意愿（Would you like some...）。

9. **中文**：我们非常喜欢在周末早午餐时点上一蒸笼点心。  
   **英文**：We love ordering steam baskets of dim sum.
   > **解析**：**dim sum** 作不可数名词（源自粤语拼音），意为“广式点心/中式点心”，在快餐美食用词中广泛流行。

10. **中文**：这家快餐店的汽车穿梭取餐通道是二十四小时营业的。  
   **英文**：The drive-thru at this fast food restaurant is open 24 hours.
   > **解析**：**fast food restaurant** 作复合名词意为“快餐店、速食餐厅”，作介词 at 的宾语，说明用餐营业场所类型。
