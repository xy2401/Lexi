## 单元讲解

本单元围绕"讨论一场时装秀"展开。核心是时装相关表达：The show was held on a big stage.（秀在一个大舞台上举办。）、The model was dressed in gold.（模特穿着金色的衣服。）、The latest design is made of cotton.（最新的设计是棉质的。）、The designer is very famous.（这位设计师很有名。）。

词表里有服装词汇（bathing suit, heels, fashion）和描述词汇（good-looking, thin, soft, light）。你可以练习：The show was canceled—it was delayed. / She was dressed in a soft, light dress. / I hate those heels! / Welcome to the fashion show!

held 是 hold 的过去式（举办）。made of 表示"由……制成"。

## Tips

- **fashion / in fashion** — 时尚 / 流行。搭配：in fashion, out of fashion, the latest fashion。例句：This style is in fashion now. / She loves fashion. 注意 in fashion = trendy / fashionable。

- **designer / design / designed** — 设计师 / 设计。搭配：a famous designer, design clothes, designed by。例句：The dress was designed by a famous designer. / I love this design. 注意 designer 也可作形容词（designer clothes = 名牌服装）。

- **held / hold** — 举办。hold → held → held。搭配：hold a show, the show was held, hold an event。例句：The fashion show was held last night. / They hold a show every year. 注意 held 是过去式和过去分词。

- **model / stage** — 模特 / 舞台。搭配：a fashion model, on the stage, walk the stage。例句：The model walked on the stage. / She's a professional model. 注意 model 也有"模型"的意思。

- **dressed in** — 穿着。搭配：dressed in gold, dressed in black, well-dressed。例句：She was dressed in a gold gown. / The models were dressed in cotton. 注意 dressed in 表示状态（穿着什么），dress 表示动作（穿衣服）。

- **made of / made of cotton** — 由……制成。搭配：made of cotton, made of gold, made of glass。例句：This dress is made of cotton. / The necklace is made of gold. 注意 made of 能看出原材料，made from 看不出。

- **heels / bathing suit** — 高跟鞋 / 泳衣。搭配：wear heels, high heels, a bathing suit。例句：I can't walk in these heels! / She wore a bathing suit on stage. 注意 heels 通常用复数。

- **latest / soft / light** — 最新的 / 柔软的 / 轻的。搭配：the latest design, soft fabric, light material。例句：This is the latest collection. / The fabric is soft and light. 注意 latest = 最新的（不是"最晚的"）。

- **cancel / delayed** — 取消 / 延迟。搭配：The show was canceled, delayed by an hour。例句：The show was canceled. / It was delayed by thirty minutes. 注意 cancel 和 delay 的区别：cancel = 不办了，delay = 推迟。

- **good-looking / thin** — 好看的 / 瘦的。搭配：a good-looking model, very thin。例句：The model is good-looking and thin. / She's very thin. 注意 good-looking 男女都可用。

- **gold / glass / sight** — 金色 / 玻璃 / 景象。搭配：made of gold, a glass of, a beautiful sight。例句：The dress was made of gold fabric. / What a beautiful sight! 注意 sight 表示"景象/风景"。

- **welcome / hate** — 欢迎 / 讨厌。搭配：Welcome to..., I hate...。例句：Welcome to the fashion show! / I hate those shoes! 注意 Welcome to + 名词（Welcome to the show）。

<quiz-sentence-builder>

1. **中文**：时装秀在一个大舞台上举行。  
   **英文**：The fashion show was held on a big stage.
   > **解析**：hold a show 表示“举办秀”，held 为 hold 的过去分词，与 was 构成被动语态；on a big stage 表示“在大舞台上”。

2. **中文**：顶级模特身穿金色的礼服。  
   **英文**：The top model was dressed in gold.
   > **解析**：be dressed in 表示“穿着某种颜色的衣服/某种材质的服装”；gold 指金色的服饰。

3. **中文**：这款最新的设计是由棉花制成的。  
   **英文**：This latest design is made of cotton.
   > **解析**：latest 表示“最新的”；be made of 表示“由……制成”（能看出原料）；cotton 意为“棉花”。

4. **中文**：这位设计师在会场受到了大家的热烈欢迎。  
   **英文**：The designer was welcomed by everyone at the show.
   > **解析**：designer 表示“设计师”；welcomed 是 welcome 的过去分词，与 was 构成被动语态。

5. **中文**：她身穿一套时尚的泳衣走下 T 台。  
   **英文**：She walked on the stage wearing a stylish bathing suit.
   > **解析**：bathing suit 表示“泳衣”；wear 表示“穿着”；on the stage 表示“在舞台上”。

6. **中文**：这场户外时装秀被推迟了一个小时。  
   **英文**：The outdoor fashion show was delayed by an hour.
   > **解析**：delayed 表示“被推迟的/延误的”；delay by + 时间 表示“推迟了多长时间”。

7. **中文**：不幸的是，晚间秀被取消了。  
   **英文**：Unfortunately, the evening show was canceled.
   > **解析**：canceled 是 cancel 的过去分词，表示“被取消”；unfortunately 意为“不幸地”。

8. **中文**：我讨厌穿高跟鞋，因为它们弄痛了我的脚。  
   **英文**：I hate wearing high heels because they hurt my feet.
   > **解析**：hate doing sth 表示“讨厌做某事”；heels / high heels 表示“高跟鞋”。

9. **中文**：鲜艳的颜色在这一季非常流行。  
   **英文**：Bright colors are very much in fashion this season.
   > **解析**：in fashion 表示“流行/时尚”；bright colors 表示“鲜艳的颜色”。

10. **中文**：这件连衣裙的面料既柔软又轻盈。  
   **英文**：The fabric of this dress is soft and light.
   > **解析**：soft 表示“柔软的”；light 表示“轻盈的/重量轻的”；fabric 意为“面料/布料”。

11. **中文**：留着红头发的英俊模特吸引了所有人的注意。  
   **英文**：The good-looking model with red hair attracted everyone.
   > **解析**：good-looking 表示“好看的/帅气的”；with red hair 表示“留着红头发”。

12. **中文**：这款配饰是由透明玻璃制成的。  
   **英文**：This accessory is made of clear glass.
   > **解析**：made of glass 表示“由玻璃制成”；accessory 意为“配饰”；clear 表示“透明的”。

13. **中文**：他们计划下个月举办另一场时尚活动。  
   **英文**：They plan to hold another fashion event next month.
   > **解析**：hold an event 表示“举办活动”；plan to do sth 表示“计划做某事”。

14. **中文**：在登台前，她需要换上她的红裙子。  
   **英文**：She needs to change into her red dress before going on stage.
   > **解析**：change into 表示“换上（衣服）”；before going on stage 表示“登台之前”。

15. **中文**：看到所有这些漂亮的衣服，真是奇妙的景象！  
   **英文**：What a beautiful sight to see all these fine clothes!
   > **解析**：sight 表示“景象/风景”；what a + 名词短语 表示感叹句“多么……的景象”。

16. **中文**：那位模特穿上那件裙子显得非常瘦高。  
   **英文**：The model looked very thin and tall in that dress.
   > **解析**：thin 表示“瘦的/纤细的”；look + adj. 表示“看起来……”。

17. **中文**：欢迎来到我们一年一度的春季时装秀！  
   **英文**：Welcome to our annual spring fashion show!
   > **解析**：Welcome to... 表示“欢迎来到……”；annual 表示“一年一度的”。

18. **中文**：这座熠熠生辉的皇冠是由纯金打造的。  
   **英文**：This shining crown is made of solid gold.
   > **解析**：made of gold 表示“由黄金制成”；solid gold 意为“纯金/实金”。

19. **中文**：这件大衣是由一位著名的设计师设计的。  
   **英文**：The coat was designed by a famous designer.
   > **解析**：designed 是 design 的过去分词，表示“被设计”；by + 人 表示“由……完成”。

20. **中文**：她在时装秀上逗留了两个小时。  
   **英文**：She stayed at the fashion show for two hours.
   > **解析**：for + 时间段 表示“持续……时间”；stay at 表示“逗留在/停留在”。
