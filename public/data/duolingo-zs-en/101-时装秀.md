## 单元讲解

本单元围绕"讨论一场时装秀"展开。核心是时装相关表达：The show was held on a big stage.（秀在一个大舞台上举办。）、The model was dressed in gold.（模特穿着金色的衣服。）、The latest design is made of cotton.（最新的设计是棉质的。）、The designer is very famous.（这位设计师很有名。）。

词表里有服装词汇（bathing suit, heels, fashion）和描述词汇（good-looking, thin, soft, light）。你可以练习：The show was canceled—it was delayed. / She was dressed in a soft, light dress. / I hate those heels! / Welcome to the fashion show!

held 是 hold 的过去式（举办）。made of 表示"由……制成"。

## Tips

- **fashion / in fashion** — 时尚 / 流行。搭配：in fashion, out of fashion, the latest fashion。例句：This style is in fashion now. / She loves fashion. 注意 in fashion = trendy / fashionable。

- **designer / design / designed** — 设计师 / 设计。搭配：a famous designer, design clothes, designed by。例句：The dress was designed by a famous designer. / I love this design. 注意 designer 也可作形容词（designer clothes = 名牌服装）。

- **held / hold** — 举办。hold → held → held。搭配：hold a show, the show was held, hold an event。例句：The fashion show was held last night. / They hold a show every year. 注意 held 是过去式和过去分词。

- **model / stage** — 模特 / 舞台。搭配：a fashion model, on the stage, walk the stage。例句：The model walked on the stage. / She's a professional model. 注意 model 也有"模型"的意思。

- **dressed in** — 穿着。搭配：dressed in gold, dressed in black, well-dressed。例句：She was dressed in a gold gown. / The models were dressed in cotton. 注意 dressed in 表示状态（穿着什么），dress 表示动作（穿衣服）。

- **made of / made of cotton** — 由……制成。搭配：made of cotton, made of gold, made of glass。例句：This dress is made of cotton. / The necklace is made of gold. 注意 made of 能看出原材料，made from 看不出。

- **heels / bathing suit** — 高跟鞋 / 泳衣。搭配：wear heels, high heels, a bathing suit。例句：I can't walk in these heels! / She wore a bathing suit on stage. 注意 heels 通常用复数。

- **latest / soft / light** — 最新的 / 柔软的 / 轻的。搭配：the latest design, soft fabric, light material。例句：This is the latest collection. / The fabric is soft and light. 注意 latest = 最新的（不是"最晚的"）。

- **cancel / delayed** — 取消 / 延迟。搭配：The show was canceled, delayed by an hour。例句：The show was canceled. / It was delayed by thirty minutes. 注意 cancel 和 delay 的区别：cancel = 不办了，delay = 推迟。

- **good-looking / thin** — 好看的 / 瘦的。搭配：a good-looking model, very thin。例句：The model is good-looking and thin. / She's very thin. 注意 good-looking 男女都可用。

- **gold / glass / sight** — 金色 / 玻璃 / 景象。搭配：made of gold, a glass of, a beautiful sight。例句：The dress was made of gold fabric. / What a beautiful sight! 注意 sight 表示"景象/风景"。

- **welcome / hate** — 欢迎 / 讨厌。搭配：Welcome to..., I hate...。例句：Welcome to the fashion show! / I hate those shoes! 注意 Welcome to + 名词（Welcome to the show）。

<quiz-sentence-builder>

1. **中文**：一年一度的时装秀展示了下一个季节的华丽新款式。  
   **英文**：The annual fashion show presented magnificent new styles for the upcoming season (bathing suit).
   > **解析**：fashion 作名词，意为“时尚，时装”。fashion show 指“时装秀，时装展”。

2. **中文**：时装秀结束时，这位著名的意大利设计师走出来向观众挥手。  
   **英文**：The famous Italian designer came out to wave to the audience at the end of the show.
   > **解析**：designer 作可数名词，意为“设计师”。Italian designer 表示“意大利设计师”。

3. **中文**：专业模特身着优雅的晚礼服在走秀台上优雅行走。  
   **英文**：Professional models walked gracefully down the runway wearing elegant evening gowns.
   > **解析**：model 作名词，意为“模特”。句中使用复数形式 models 作主语。

4. **中文**：随着时装秀的开始，璀璨的聚光灯照亮了主舞台。  
   **英文**：Bright spotlights lit up the main stage as the show began.
   > **解析**：stage 作名词，意为“舞台，台子”。main stage 指“主舞台”。

5. **中文**：主模特身穿一身令人惊艳的红色丝绸连衣裙。  
   **英文**：The lead model was dressed in a stunning red silk dress.
   > **解析**：dressed in 为固定形容词短语，意为“身穿……衣服”，后接服装颜色或款式。

6. **中文**：这款轻便的夏款衬衫由纯棉制成，非常舒适。  
   **英文**：This light summer shirt is made of pure cotton, making it very comfortable.
   > **解析**：be made of cotton 意为“由棉花制成”，be made of 表原材料未经过化学变质。

7. **中文**：这家时尚杂志重点介绍了来自米兰的最新服装趋势。  
   **英文**：The fashion magazine highlighted the latest clothing trends from Milan.
   > **解析**：latest 作形容词最高级，意为“最新的”。the latest trends 意为“最新趋势”。

8. **中文**：她踩着高跟鞋自信地走过舞台。  
   **英文**：She walked confidently across the stage wearing high heels.
   > **解析**：heels (high heels) 作名词，意为“高跟鞋”。短语 wear high heels 表示“穿高跟鞋”。

9. **中文**：九十年代的皮夹克今年又重新流行起来了。  
   **英文**：Leather jackets from the nineties are back in fashion this year (dressed in).
   > **解析**：in fashion 为固定短语，作表语意为“时兴的，流行的”。

10. **中文**：由于恶劣天气，主办方不得不取消户外走秀。  
   **英文**：The organizer had to cancel the outdoor runway show because of bad weather.
   > **解析**：cancel 作及物动词，意为“取消”。have to cancel 表达“不得不取消”。
