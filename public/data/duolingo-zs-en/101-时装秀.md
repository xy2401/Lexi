## 单元讲解

本单元的主题是"讨论一场时装秀"。词表可以分成四组：秀场元素（fashion, show 相关：stage, model, designer, sight, welcome）、服饰与材质（bathing suit, heels, red hair, gold, glass, made of, made of cotton, soft, light, thin）、状态与变化（cancel/canceled, change, delayed, held/hold）以及流行表达（in fashion, latest, dressed in, good-looking, hate, for, in）。拼在一起就是一场秀的完整报道：Welcome to the show! The models are dressed in the latest designs. One dress is made of cotton, and the heels are gold.

语法主线是被动语态和材料表达。秀的安排常用被动：The show was held on the stage. The event was delayed/canceled. 材质用 be made of：The dress is made of cotton. 另一条线索是动词的 -ed 变化：design → designed、cancel → canceled，注意美式拼写 canceled 只有一个 l。

学完这些词，你能用英语谈论时装秀的流程、模特与设计师，描述服装的材质与风格，并说明活动的推迟或取消。

## Tips

- **fashion / in fashion / latest** — fashion 时尚；in fashion 正流行：Bathing suits are in fashion. latest 最新的，是 late 的特殊比较形式：the latest design（最新款）。注意 latest（最新）与 last（最后）的区别。

- **design / designed / designer** — 一词家族。design 名词"设计"或动词"设计"；designed 是过去式与过去分词；designer 设计师，-er 表"做……的人"。搭配：a famous designer（著名设计师）。

- **hold / held** — hold 举办、握住，不规则变化 hold → held → held：They hold the show every year. The show was held on the stage. ✗ holded → ✓ held.

- **cancel / canceled / delayed** — 活动变动三词。cancel 取消，canceled 是过去式（美式单 l，英式常写 cancelled）；delayed 推迟。被动句常用：The show was canceled/delayed.

- **model / designer / good-looking** — 人物描述。model 模特；designer 设计师；good-looking 好看的，由 good + looking 合成，可形容男女。类似的还有 well-dressed（穿着入时的）。

- **dressed in** — 穿着……：The models are dressed in gold. be dressed in 强调状态，wear 强调动作习惯。✗ She dressed in a red dress. → ✓ She is dressed in a red dress. / She wears a red dress.

- **made / made of / made of cotton** — 材质表达。be made of 表示"看得出原材料"：The ring is made of gold. 若材料发生化学变化则用 be made from：Paper is made from wood. made of cotton 棉制的，cotton 棉花。

- **bathing suit / heels / red hair** — 造型词。bathing suit 泳装；heels 高跟鞋，常说 a pair of heels；red hair 红头发，注意英语说 hair 通常不可数：She has red hair.（✗ red hairs 除非指几根头发）

- **gold / glass / light / soft / thin** — 材质与质感。gold 金（色）的；glass 玻璃；light 轻的（也有"光、浅色"义）；soft 柔软的；thin 瘦的、薄的。反义词串记：light ↔ heavy、soft ↔ hard、thin ↔ thick.

- **hate / welcome / sight / for** — 态度与杂项。hate 讨厌，比 dislike 语气重：I hate these heels. welcome 欢迎：Welcome to the show. sight 景象：What a beautiful sight! for 表目的或对象：ready for the show.

- **change / stage / in** — change 更换、改变：She changed her dress. stage 舞台、阶段；in 在……里，也用于穿着：dressed in gold.

<quiz-sentence-builder>

1. **中文**：欢迎来到时装秀。
   **英文**：Welcome to the fashion show.
   > **解析**：Welcome to… 是固定欢迎语；to 引出地点或活动。

2. **中文**：秀在大舞台上举行。
   **英文**：The show was held on the main stage.
   > **解析**：was held 是 hold 的被动语态；on the stage 是地点状语。

3. **中文**：模特们穿着金色的衣服。
   **英文**：The models are dressed in gold.
   > **解析**：be dressed in 表示穿着状态；gold 指金色的服装。

4. **中文**：那位红头发的模特很高。
   **英文**：The model with red hair is very tall.
   > **解析**：with red hair 作后置定语修饰 model；hair 是不可数名词。

5. **中文**：最新的设计非常漂亮。
   **英文**：The latest design is beautiful.
   > **解析**：latest 作定语修饰 design；beautiful 作表语。

6. **中文**：这条裙子是棉制的。
   **英文**：This dress is made of cotton.
   > **解析**：be made of 表示材质可见；made of cotton 是固定表达。

7. **中文**：演出被推迟了。
   **英文**：The show was delayed.
   > **解析**：was delayed 是被动语态，表示"被推迟"。

8. **中文**：他们不得不取消活动。
   **英文**：They had to cancel the event.
   > **解析**：had to 表"不得不"，后接动词原形 cancel。

9. **中文**：我讨厌这双高跟鞋。
   **英文**：I hate these heels.
   > **解析**：hate 后接宾语 these heels；heels 常用复数。

10. **中文**：那位设计师很帅。
    **英文**：The designer is good-looking.
    > **解析**：good-looking 是复合形容词作表语。

11. **中文**：泳装现在正流行。
    **英文**：Bathing suits are in fashion now.
    > **解析**：in fashion 是"流行"的固定短语；主语是复数，用 are。

12. **中文**：这个玻璃杯很轻。
    **英文**：This glass cup is very light.
    > **解析**：glass 作定语修饰 cup；light 表"轻的"。

13. **中文**：布料摸起来很软。
    **英文**：The cloth feels soft.
    > **解析**：feel 是系动词，后接形容词 soft 作表语。

14. **中文**：她又高又瘦。
    **英文**：She is tall and thin.
    > **解析**：and 连接两个并列形容词作表语。

15. **中文**：多么美的景象啊！
    **英文**：What a beautiful sight!
    > **解析**：What + a + 形容词 + 名词构成感叹句。

16. **中文**：他们每年举办这场秀。
    **英文**：They hold the show every year.
    > **解析**：hold 表"举办"；every year 是一般现在时的时间标志。

17. **中文**：这件外套是巴黎设计的。
    **英文**：This coat was designed in Paris.
    > **解析**：was designed 是被动语态；in Paris 是地点状语。

18. **中文**：这枚戒指是金的。
    **英文**：This ring is made of gold.
    > **解析**：be made of gold 表示"由金制成"；材质可见用 of。

19. **中文**：她换了两次衣服。
    **英文**：She changed her clothes twice.
    > **解析**：changed 是 change 的过去式；twice 是频率副词放句末。

20. **中文**：一切都为演出准备好了。
    **英文**：Everything is ready for the show.
    > **解析**：be ready for 是"为……准备好"的固定搭配。

### 1

Welcome ____ the fashion show.

- [x] to
- [ ] in
- [ ] on

> **解析**：Welcome to… 是固定欢迎语；in 和 on 不能与 welcome 构成这一搭配。

### 2

The show was ____ on a big stage.

- [x] held
- [ ] hated
- [ ] dressed

> **解析**：举办用 hold 的过去分词 held；hated 和 dressed 语义不通。

### 3

The models are dressed ____ gold.

- [x] in
- [ ] of
- [ ] for

> **解析**：be dressed in 是"穿着"的固定搭配；of 和 for 不能构成这一短语。

### 4

The model with ____ is very tall.

- [x] red hair
- [ ] good-looking
- [ ] in fashion

> **解析**：with 后接名词 red hair；good-looking 是形容词，in fashion 是短语，都不作 with 的宾语。

### 5

The ____ designs are beautiful.

- [x] latest
- [ ] soft
- [ ] thin

> **解析**：修饰"最新款"用 latest；soft 和 thin 描述质感与厚度，语义不符。

### 6

- [ ] in
