## 单元讲解

本单元围绕"谈论传统节日"展开。核心句型包括：I can't wait for Thanksgiving!（我等不及感恩节了！）、This is how we celebrate.（我们就是这样庆祝的。）、What will you do for Halloween?（万圣节你打算做什么？）、We're having roast duck and pumpkin pie.（我们要吃烤鸭和南瓜派。）。

词表里有节日名（Halloween, Thanksgiving, Mid-Autumn Festival, National Day）和节日食物（turkey, ham, mooncake, pumpkin pie, roast duck, mashed potatoes）。你可以练习：Trick or treat! / We serve turkey on Thanksgiving. / The children are coming for candy. / I'm thinking of granny.

can't wait 表示"迫不及待"。days off 表示"休息日"。

## Tips

- **can't wait / can't wait for / can't wait to** — 迫不及待。搭配：I can't wait! / can't wait for Christmas / can't wait to see you。例句：I can't wait for Thanksgiving! / I can't wait to eat the turkey! 注意 can't wait to + 动词，can't wait for + 名词。

- **thanksgiving / halloween** — 感恩节 / 万圣节。搭配：on Thanksgiving, for Halloween, Thanksgiving dinner。注意 Thanksgiving 是美国重要节日（11月第四个周四）。Halloween 是10月31日。

- **mid-autumn festival / national day** — 中秋节 / 国庆节。搭配：Mid-Autumn Festival, National Day holiday。注意这些是中国节日的英文翻译。Mid-Autumn Festival 吃 mooncake（月饼）。

- **trick or treat** — 不给糖就捣蛋。搭配：Trick or treat! / go trick-or-treating。注意这是万圣节儿童挨家挨户要糖时说的话。trick = 恶作剧，treat = 款待（糖果）。

- **turkey / ham / roast duck** — 火鸡 / 火腿 / 烤鸭。搭配：roast turkey, a slice of ham, Peking roast duck。例句：We serve turkey on Thanksgiving. / I love roast duck. 注意 turkey 是感恩节传统食物。

- **pumpkin pie / mashed potatoes** — 南瓜派 / 土豆泥。搭配：a slice of pumpkin pie, mashed potatoes with gravy。注意这些都是感恩节传统配菜。mashed = 捣碎的。

- **mooncake** — 月饼。搭配：eat mooncakes, a mooncake, Mid-Autumn mooncakes。注意 mooncake 是中国特色食品，英文直译。通常用复数：eat mooncakes。

- **days off** — 休息日、假期。搭配：days off, have days off, three days off。例句：We have three days off for National Day. / I need some days off. 注意 day off = 不上班的日子。

- **serve / prepare / grilled** — 上菜 / 准备 / 烤的。搭配：serve the turkey, prepare dinner, grilled ham。例句：We serve roast duck at dinner. / I'm preparing the meal. / The ham is grilled.

- **guest / children / granny** — 客人 / 孩子们 / 奶奶。搭配：invite guests, the children are coming, visit granny。例句：We have guests coming for Thanksgiving. / The children want candy. / I'm thinking of granny.

- **this is how** — 这就是……的方式。搭配：This is how we celebrate. / This is how I cook it. 例句：This is how we celebrate Mid-Autumn Festival. / This is how you make pumpkin pie.

- **think of** — 想起、想到。搭配：think of someone, think of a plan, thinking of。例句：I'm thinking of granny. / What do you think of the food? 注意 think of 和 think about 的区别较细微。

<quiz-sentence-builder>

1. **中文**：我等不及感恩节去吃烤火鸡了。  
   **英文**：I can't wait for Thanksgiving to eat roasted turkey.
   > **解析**：can't wait for 后接名词，表示“迫不及待地想要……”；Thanksgiving 意为“感恩节”。

2. **中文**：孩子们喜欢在万圣节穿戏服并喊“不给糖就捣蛋”。  
   **英文**：Children love to wear costumes and say trick or treat on Halloween.
   > **解析**：trick or treat 是万圣节讨糖口的号；Halloween 意为“万圣节”。

3. **中文**：我们在中秋节期间吃美味的月饼。  
   **英文**：We eat delicious mooncakes during the Mid-Autumn Festival.
   > **解析**：mooncakes 表示“月饼”；Mid-Autumn Festival 意为“中秋节”。

4. **中文**：国庆节假期我们有七天假。  
   **英文**：We have seven days off for the National Day holiday.
   > **解析**：days off 表示“休息日/假期”；National Day 意为“国庆节”。

5. **中文**：我的家人将为我们的晚餐客人提供烤鸭。  
   **英文**：My family will serve roast duck for our dinner guests.
   > **解析**：roast duck 表示“烤鸭”；serve 意为“提供/上菜”。

6. **中文**：每年秋天她都会做甜南瓜派和奶油土豆泥。  
   **英文**：She makes sweet pumpkin pie and creamy mashed potatoes every autumn.
   > **解析**：pumpkin pie 表示“南瓜派”；mashed potatoes 意为“土豆泥”。

7. **中文**：这就是我们与家人一起庆祝传统节日的方式。  
   **英文**：This is how we celebrate traditional holidays with our family.
   > **解析**：This is how... 表示“这就是……的方式/这就是如何……”。

8. **中文**：在节日期间，我总是会想起我亲爱的奶奶。  
   **英文**：I always think of my dear granny during the holiday season.
   > **解析**：think of 表示“想起/想到”；granny 意为“奶奶/外婆”。

9. **中文**：孩子们很兴奋，因为万圣节快到了。  
   **英文**：The children are excited because halloween is coming soon.
   > **解析**：is coming 表示“即将到来”；children 意为“孩子们”。

10. **中文**：我们买了一些便宜的糖果给邻居的孩子们。  
   **英文**：We bought some cheap candy to give to the neighbors' kids.
   > **解析**：candy 表示“糖果”；cheap 意为“便宜的”。

11. **中文**：他们计划为二十位客人准备一场丰盛的节日盛宴。  
   **英文**：They plan to prepare a big holiday feast for twenty guests.
   > **解析**：guest 表示“客人/来宾”；prepare 意为“准备”。

12. **中文**：我迫不及待想在春假期间去泰国旅游。  
   **英文**：I can't wait to visit Thailand during the spring break.
   > **解析**：can't wait to 后接动词原形，表示“迫不及待要做某事”。

13. **中文**：他给最好的朋友寄了一张漂亮的节日明信片。  
   **英文**：He sent a nice holiday postcard to his best friend.
   > **解析**：postcard 表示“明信片”；sent 是 send 的过去式。 

14. **中文**：切一些烤火腿放在桌子上。  
   **英文**：Slice some grilled ham and put it on the table.
   > **解析**：ham 表示“火腿”；grilled 意为“烤的/炙烤的”。

15. **中文**：即将到来的假期你打算做些什么？  
   **英文**：What will you do for the upcoming holiday?
   > **解析**：What will... 表示“你将要做什么？”（询问将来计划）。

16. **中文**：晚餐几乎准备好了，所以请坐下吧。  
   **英文**：The dinner is almost ready, so please sit down.
   > **解析**：almost 是副词，表示“几乎/差不多”。

17. **中文**：他们在感恩节聚会上供应了一只大火鸡。  
   **英文**：They served a huge turkey at the Thanksgiving party.
   > **解析**：turkey 表示“火鸡”；served 意为“端上/提供”。

18. **中文**：奶油土豆泥是我最喜欢的节日副食。  
   **英文**：Creamy mashed potatoes are my favorite holiday side dish.
   > **解析**：mashed potatoes 表示“土豆泥”。

19. **中文**：我们在明亮的满月下度过了月饼节。  
   **英文**：We enjoyed the Mid-Autumn Festival under the bright full moon.
   > **解析**：Mid-Autumn Festival 表示“中秋节”。

20. **中文**：请在餐桌旁给我留一个位置。  
   **英文**：Please save a place for me at the dinner table.
   > **解析**：place 表示“位置/座位”。
