## 单元讲解

本单元围绕"在城市中游玩"展开。核心句型包括：What do you like about New York?（你喜欢纽约什么？）、I'm glad you're here!（你来了我真高兴！）、I'm sure you'll love it.（你肯定会喜欢的。）、Let me show you around.（我带你转转。）。

词表里有纽约地标（Chinatown, NYU）和活动（cycling, ice skating, jazz, opera）。你可以练习：My roommate is from NYU. / Let's go ice skating! / The jazz club is across from the opera house. / I'll pick you up at your street.

pick up 表示"接（人）"。序数词 twenty-first / twenty-second / twenty-third 表示日期。

## Tips

- **like about** — 喜欢……的什么。搭配：What do you like about...? / I like everything about...。例句：What do you like about New York? / I like the jazz and the food. 注意 about 后接地点/事物。

- **i'm glad / i'm sure** — 我很高兴 / 我确定。搭配：I'm glad you're here. / I'm sure you'll love it. 例句：I'm glad to meet you! / I'm sure it'll be fun. 注意 glad 后接 to do 或 that 从句。

- **pick up** — 接（人）、捡起。搭配：pick you up, pick up the luggage, pick up at...。例句：I'll pick you up at the airport. / Can you pick up my luggage? 注意 pick up 是 separable：pick you up / pick up the bag。

- **show around** — 带……转转。搭配：show you around, show someone around the city。例句：Let me show you around New York. / She showed me around Chinatown. 注意 show around 是固定搭配。

- **Chinatown / NYU** — 唐人街 / 纽约大学。搭配：in Chinatown, at NYU, go to Chinatown。注意 Chinatown 是专有名词（大写）。NYU = New York University。

- **jazz / opera** — 爵士乐 / 歌剧。搭配：a jazz club, listen to jazz, an opera house, go to the opera。例句：Let's go to a jazz club! / The opera was amazing. 注意 jazz 不可数。

- **ice skating / cycling** — 溜冰 / 骑行。搭配：go ice skating, go cycling, ice skating rink。例句：Let's go ice skating! / I went cycling around the city. 注意 go + 动名词（活动）。

- **roommate** — 室友。构词：room + mate。搭配：my roommate, a college roommate。例句：My roommate is a student at NYU. / I have two roommates. 注意 roommate 不分性别。

- **across from** — 在……对面。搭配：across from the park, across from my street。例句：The jazz club is across from the opera house. / My apartment is across from NYU. 注意 = opposite。

- **twenty-first / twenty-second / twenty-third** — 第21 / 22 / 23。构词：twenty-one → twenty-first。搭配：on the twenty-first（21号）。注意序数词拼写：twenty-first（不是 ✗ twenty-oneth）。

- **luggage** — 行李。搭配：my luggage, carry luggage, luggage rack。例句：I'll help you with your luggage. / Where is my luggage? 注意 luggage 不可数（不说 ✗ a luggage / ✗ luggages）。

- **various / zero** — 各种各样的 / 零。搭配：various things, various activities, zero degrees。例句：There are various things to do in New York. / It's zero degrees outside! 注意 various = many different。
