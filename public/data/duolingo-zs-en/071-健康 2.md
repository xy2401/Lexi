# Unit 071: 健康 2

> 描述健康问题

## 单词

appointment, available, back, body, break, broke, brush, cold, dentist, down, fall, fell, for, go to, have, how often should, keep, made, make, meals, move, office, remember to, rest, should, should not, shouldn't, toothache, wake up, well, went to, when should, with, woke up

## 单元讲解

本单元围绕"描述健康问题"展开。核心是 should / shouldn't（应该/不应该）：You should rest. / You shouldn't eat too much. / You should brush your teeth. / How often should I go to the dentist? / When should I wake up?

词表里有健康相关词汇（dentist, toothache, cold, body, meals）和动作（brush, rest, move, wake up）。你可以练习：I have a toothache. / You should go to the dentist. / I fell and broke my arm. / Remember to brush your teeth.

fell（fall 的过去式）和 broke（break 的过去式）描述受伤经过。woke up（wake up 的过去式）。

## Tips

- **should / shouldn't** — 应该 / 不应该。搭配：You should rest. / You shouldn't eat junk food. / Should I go to the doctor? 注意 should 后接动词原形：should go（不是 ✗ should to go）。否定：should not = shouldn't。

- **toothache** — 牙痛。构词：tooth + ache。搭配：have a toothache, a bad toothache。类似的：headache（头痛）, stomachache（胃痛）, backache（背痛）。注意 -ache 表示"疼痛"。

- **dentist** — 牙医。搭配：go to the dentist, see a dentist, dentist's office。例句：You should see a dentist. / I have a dentist appointment. 注意 dentist 和 doctor 的区别：dentist 专看牙齿。

- **fell / fall / broke / break** — 摔了 / 断了。fall → fell → fallen, break → broke → broken。搭配：fell down, broke my arm, fell and broke。例句：I fell and broke my leg. / She fell down the stairs.

- **brush** — 刷。搭配：brush your teeth, brush my hair。例句：You should brush your teeth twice a day. / Remember to brush before bed. 注意 brush 既是动词也是名词（a toothbrush）。

- **wake up / woke up** — 醒来。wake → woke → woken。搭配：wake up early, woke up at six, wake up feeling sick。例句：I woke up with a headache. 注意 wake up 和 get up 的区别：wake up 是醒来，get up 是起床。

- **remember to** — 记得要去做。搭配：remember to brush, remember to take medicine。注意 remember to do（记得要做）vs remember doing（记得做过）：Remember to rest! / I remember resting.

- **appointment** — 预约。搭配：make an appointment, have an appointment, dentist appointment。例句：I have a dentist appointment at three. / You should make an appointment.

- **rest** — 休息。搭配：get some rest, rest for a day, You should rest。例句：You need to rest. / Get some rest! 注意 rest 既是动词也是名词：have a rest（英式）/ take a rest（美式）。

- **how often should / when should** — 应该多久……/ 应该什么时候……。搭配：How often should I brush? / When should I take the medicine? 注意 should 在疑问词后面：How often should I...?

- **available** — 有空的、可用的。搭配：Are you available? / available appointments, Is the dentist available? 例句：The dentist is available tomorrow. / Is this time available?

- **meals** — 餐（复数）。搭配：three meals a day, regular meals, eat meals on time。例句：You should eat three meals a day. / Don't skip meals. 注意 meal 的复数是 meals。
