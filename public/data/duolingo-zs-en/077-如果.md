## 单元讲解

本单元围绕"谈论计划"展开，核心是 if 条件句（第一类条件句）：If it rains, I'll stay home.（如果下雨，我就待在家。）、If the sun comes out, we'll go surfing.（如果出太阳，我们就去冲浪。）、If you earn enough money, you can travel around the world.（如果你赚够了钱，就可以环游世界。）。

词表里有活动词（fly a kite, play cards, skiing, surfing, volleyball）和数量词（thousand, ten thousand, more, less, enough, nothing）。你可以练习：If I have nothing to do, I'll play cards. / She wants to travel around the world. / That's enough.

will 用于条件句的主句（if 从句用一般现在时）。

## Tips

- **if + 一般现在时, will + 动词原形** — 第一类条件句。搭配：If it rains, I'll stay home. / If you study, you'll pass. 注意 if 从句不用 will：✗ If it will rain → ✓ If it rains。主句用 will。

- **around the world** — 环游世界。搭配：travel around the world, around the world trip。例句：I want to travel around the world. / She's been around the world. 注意 around the world = all over the world。

- **enough** — 足够的。搭配：enough money, enough time, good enough。例句：Do you have enough money? / That's enough. 注意 enough 放在形容词后面：good enough（不是 ✗ enough good）。

- **thousand / ten thousand** — 千 / 万。搭配：a thousand, ten thousand, thousands of。例句：It costs ten thousand yuan. / Thousands of people came. 注意具体数字后不加 s：two thousand（不是 ✗ two thousands）。

- **more / less / nothing** — 更多 / 更少 / 没有东西。搭配：more money, less time, nothing to do。例句：I want to earn more. / I have nothing to do. 注意 less 是 little 的比较级，more 是 much/many 的比较级。

- **skiing / surfing / volleyball** — 滑雪 / 冲浪 / 排球。搭配：go skiing, go surfing, play volleyball。注意 skiing 和 surfing 用 go，volleyball 用 play。go + 动名词（运动），play + 球类。

- **fly a kite / play cards** — 放风筝 / 打牌。搭配：fly a kite, go fly a kite, play cards with friends。例句：If the wind is strong, we'll fly a kite. / Let's play cards. 注意 fly a kite 中 kite 前加 a。

- **raincoat / umbrella** — 雨衣 / 雨伞。搭配：wear a raincoat, take an umbrella, bring an umbrella。例句：If it rains, take an umbrella. / Wear a raincoat. 注意 raincoat 构词：rain + coat。

- **online / chat** — 在线 / 聊天。搭配：chat online, online shopping, chat with friends。例句：If I'm bored, I'll chat online. / She chats with friends online. 注意 online 既是形容词也是副词。

- **own** — 自己的、拥有。搭配：my own house, on my own, own a business。例句：I want my own island! / She owns a magazine company. 注意 own 作形容词时放在物主代词后：my own。

- **simple / rough** — 简单的 / 粗糙的。搭配：a simple life, a simple plan, rough sea, rough weather。例句：Let's keep it simple. / The sea is rough today. 注意 rough 可以形容天气/海面"汹涌的"。

- **will** — 将会（将来时）。搭配：I'll go, she'll come, we'll see。例句：If it's sunny, we'll go surfing. / I'll earn more money. 注意 will 后接动词原形：will go（不是 ✗ will to go）。
