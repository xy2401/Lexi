## 单元讲解

本单元围绕"与老友叙旧"展开。核心句型包括：Hey! How's it going?（嘿！最近怎么样？）、We grew up together.（我们一起长大的。）、Let's keep in touch!（我们保持联系吧！）、I heard you're a photographer now.（我听说你现在是摄影师了。）、Take care!（保重！）。

词表里有社交用语（hey, how's, take care, keep in touch, text message）和叙旧词（grow up, old friend, each other, news）。你可以练习：We used to be so close. / I sent her a text message. / What about meeting up? / I hope to see you again. / She's my ex-colleague.

ex- 前缀表示"前任的"。anymore 用于否定句。

## Tips

- **keep in touch** — 保持联系。搭配：keep in touch with, stay in touch, Let's keep in touch! 例句：Let's keep in touch! / I keep in touch with my old friends. 反义词 lose touch（失去联系）。

- **grow up** — 长大。搭配：grow up together, grew up in, growing up。例句：We grew up together. / I grew up in this city. 注意 grow up 的过去式是 grew up。

- **each other** — 互相。搭配：help each other, know each other, close to each other。例句：We know each other well. / They help each other. 注意 each other 用于两者之间，one another 用于三者及以上（但现代英语中常互换）。

- **text / text message** — 发短信 / 短信。搭配：send a text, text me, a text message。例句：I sent her a text message. / Text me when you arrive. 注意 text 既是名词也是动词（发短信）。

- **ex-** — 前任的。搭配：ex-boyfriend, ex-wife, ex-colleague。例句：She's my ex-colleague. / He's my ex-boyfriend. 注意 ex- 是前缀，表示"前任的/以前的"。口语中常说 my ex（我的前任）。

- **actually / anyway** — 其实 / 不管怎样。搭配：Actually, ... / Anyway, ... 例句：Actually, I'm a photographer now. / Anyway, let's keep in touch! 注意 actually 用于纠正或补充信息，anyway 用于转换话题。

- **how's** — ……怎么样？（how is 的缩写）。搭配：How's it going? / How's your family? / How's work? 例句：Hey! How's it going? / How's your new job? 注意 How's it going? 是非常常见的问候语。

- **take care** — 保重。搭配：Take care! / Take care of yourself. 例句：It was great seeing you! Take care! / Take care of yourself. 注意 take care 是道别用语，比 goodbye 更温暖。

- **surprised / heard** — 惊讶的 / 听说了。搭配：I'm surprised! / I heard that... / surprised to see。例句：I'm surprised! You're a photographer now? / I heard you moved. 注意 surprised 形容人。

- **hobby / violin / photographer** — 爱好 / 小提琴 / 摄影师。搭配：my hobby, play the violin, a professional photographer。例句：My hobby is playing the violin. / She's a photographer now. 注意 play the violin（乐器前加 the）。

- **anymore** —  anymore（用于否定句）。搭配：not... anymore, don't... anymore。例句：We don't see each other anymore. / She doesn't live here anymore. 注意 anymore 用于否定句末尾，表示"不再"。

- **hope / perhaps / what about** — 希望 / 也许 / ……怎么样。搭配：I hope so, perhaps we can meet, What about dinner? 例句：I hope to see you soon. / Perhaps we can meet next week. / What about getting coffee?

<quiz-sentence-builder>

1. **中文**：我们在同一个社区长大，读同一所学校。  
   **英文**：We grew up in the same neighborhood and went to the same school about actually.
   > **解析**：grow up 为动词短语，意为“长大，成长”。在句中使用过去式 grew up 描述童年经历。

2. **中文**：自大学毕业以来，我们一直没见过面。  
   **英文**：We haven't seen each other since we graduated from college.
   > **解析**：each other 为相互代词，意为“彼此，互相”。作及物动词 seen 的宾语。

3. **中文**：尽管他搬到了国外，我们依然通过短信保持联系。  
   **英文**：Although he moved abroad, we still keep in touch via text messages.
   > **解析**：keep in touch 为固定短语，意为“保持联系”。介词 via 意为“通过……方式”。

4. **中文**：昨天下午我居然在超市碰到了我们以前的老师。  
   **英文**：I actually met our former teacher at the supermarket yesterday afternoon about anymore.
   > **解析**：actually 作副词，意为“居然，实际上”。在句中作状语，表达意想不到的巧合。

5. **中文**：十年后能与我童年最好的朋友重新取得联系真是太棒了。  
   **英文**：It was wonderful to reconnect with my childhood best friend after ten years about card.
   > **解析**：reconnect 作动词，意为“重新联系”。reconnect with someone 表示“与某人重新建立联系”。

6. **中文**：我们坐在舒适的咖啡馆里回忆我们的校园岁月。  
   **英文**：We sat in the cozy cafe to reminisce about our school days about close.
   > **解析**：reminisce 作不及物动词，意为“追忆，回忆”。常与介词 about 搭配使用。

7. **中文**：在伦敦找到工作后，他不再住在我们的家乡了。  
   **英文**：He doesn't live in our hometown anymore after finding a job in London (each other) about anyway.
   > **解析**：anymore 作副词，用于否定句末，意为“再也不，不再”。与 not 呼应使用。

8. **中文**：我的前室友发消息邀请我参加他的生日聚会。  
   **英文**：My ex-roommate sent me a message inviting me to his birthday party.
   > **解析**：ex- 作前缀，意为“前……”。ex-roommate 指“前室友”。

9. **中文**：无论如何，咱们去喝杯咖啡，多聊聊你的新计划吧。  
   **英文**：Anyway, let's grab a coffee and talk more about your new plan about call.
   > **解析**：anyway 作副词，意为“无论如何，总之”，常置于句首用于转换话题。

10. **中文**：我们在上初中时曾是非常亲密的朋友。  
   **英文**：We used to be very close friends when we were in middle school going.
   > **解析**：close 作形容词，意为“亲密的，紧密的”。close friends 表示“密友，亲密朋友”。
