## 单元讲解

本单元围绕"给指令"展开，场景是学校。核心句型是祈使句：Open the door. / Close the window. / Look at the board. / Don't forget your homework. / Please don't talk. 祈使句直接用动词原形开头，没有主语——这是英语中表达命令、请求、指示的方式。

否定祈使用 don't：Don't forget! / Don't open the door. 加 please 变礼貌：Please close the window. / Please don't forget.

词表里有学校相关词（bookstore, classes, homework, pen, studies, words）和日常动词（close, come, forget, go, look, open, write）。during the week 和 weekend 提供了时间框架。see you tomorrow 是道别用语。

## Tips

- **祈使句** — 动词原形开头，省略主语 you：Open the door. / Write your name. / Come here. 否定：Don't open the door. / Don't forget. 加 please 更礼貌：Please sit down. / Open the window, please.

- **don't / please don't** — 否定祈使：Don't forget your homework! / Please don't close the window. don't = do not。注意 don't want to 表示"不想"：I don't want to go.（这不是祈使句，是陈述句。）

- **open / close** — 开 / 关。搭配：open the door / close the window / open your book。注意 open 和 close 既是动词也是形容词：The door is open.（开着的）/ The store is closed.（关着的）。

- **forget** — 忘记。三态：forget - forgot - forgotten。搭配：forget your homework, forget to do（忘记要做）、forget doing（忘记做过）。反义 remember。注意 Don't forget to... 是非常常用的提醒句式。

- **homework** — 家庭作业，不可数：do homework, a lot of homework（不说 ✗ many homeworks）。搭配：finish homework, hand in homework。注意 home + work 的构词：在家做的工作。

- **write** — 写。三态：write - wrote - written。搭配：write your name, write a letter, write down（记下来）。注意 w 不发音（/raɪt/），类似的还有 wrong, wrist。

- **during the week** — 在工作日期间（周一到周五）。搭配：I study during the week. 对比 on the weekend / at the weekend（在周末）。during = 在……期间，后面接一段时间。

- **weekend** — 周末（Saturday + Sunday）。搭配：on the weekend（美式）/ at the weekend（英式）、every weekend, have a good weekend! 注意介词差异。

- **studies / works / goes** — 三单动词形式。study → studies（变 y 为 i 加 es），work → works（直接加 s），go → goes（加 es）。注意 study 的三单规则：辅音 + y 结尾，变 y 为 i 再加 es。

- **bookstore** — 书店（美式）。英式用 bookshop。搭配：go to the bookstore, at the bookstore。类似的复合词：shoe store, clothes store。

- **see you tomorrow** — 明天见。道别用语。类似的：See you later! / See you next week! / See you soon! 口语中常简化为 See ya!

- **look** — 看。搭配：Look at the board! / Look here! 注意 look at 是固定搭配，不能省略 at：✗ Look the board → ✓ Look at the board。
