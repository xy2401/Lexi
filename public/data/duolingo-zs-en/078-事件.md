## 单元讲解

本单元围绕"描述过去进行的事件"展开，核心语法是过去进行时（was/were + doing）：I was drinking coffee at that time.（那时我正在喝咖啡。）、What were you doing all day?（你一整天在干什么？）、She was following me on the way home.（她在回家的路上跟着我。）。

词表里有地点词（bar, gas station, police station）和时间词（all afternoon, all day, all night, at that time）。你可以练习：I was at the bar all night. / He lost his keys on the way home. / I tried to explain, but she didn't listen. / What was wrong?

过去进行时描述过去某时刻正在进行的动作。

## Tips

- **was / were + doing** — 过去进行时。搭配：I was sleeping. / They were drinking. / What were you doing? 注意 was 用于 I / he / she / it，were 用于 you / we / they。描述过去某时刻正在做的事。

- **at that time** — 在那时。搭配：at that time, at that moment。例句：At that time, I was sleeping. / What were you doing at that time? 注意 at that time 指过去某个具体时刻。

- **all day / all night / all afternoon** — 一整天 / 整晚 / 整个下午。搭配：rained all day, worked all night, waited all afternoon。例句：I was studying all day. / She was working all night.

- **way home** — 回家的路上。搭配：on the way home, on my way home。例句：I lost my keys on the way home. / I met her on my way home. 注意 way home 前不加 to：on the way home（不是 ✗ on the way to home）。

- **lost / lose** — 丢了、迷路了。lose → lost → lost。搭配：lost my keys, lost my phone, get lost。例句：I lost my wallet on the way home. / She lost her way. 注意 lose 和 loose（松的）拼写不同。

- **tried / try** — 尝试了。try → tried（变 y 为 i 加 -ed）。搭配：tried to explain, tried to call, tried my best。例句：I tried to explain, but he didn't listen. 注意 try to do（努力做）vs try doing（试着做）。

- **gas station / police station** — 加油站 / 警察局。搭配：at the gas station, go to the police station。例句：I stopped at the gas station. / He went to the police station. 注意 station 是"站"。

- **headache** — 头痛。构词：head + ache。搭配：have a headache, a bad headache, get a headache。例句：I had a headache all day. / I've got a terrible headache. 注意 have a headache 是固定搭配。

- **stopped / leave / left** — 停了 / 离开。stop → stopped, leave → left → left。搭配：stopped the car, left the bar, leave at fifteen。例句：He stopped at the gas station. / She left at fifteen.

- **follow** — 跟随。搭配：follow me, follow the road, following。例句：She was following me. / Follow this road. 注意 follow 也可以表示"关注"（社交媒体）：follow someone online。

- **at all** — 根本（用于否定句）。搭配：not... at all, don't... at all。例句：I didn't sleep at all. / It doesn't hurt at all. 注意 at all 加强否定语气：not at all = 一点也不。

- **plain / wrong** — 清楚的 / 错误的。搭配：plain water（白水）、What's wrong? / something wrong。例句：I'll just have plain water. / What was wrong with the car? 注意 plain 也可以表示"朴素的"。

<quiz-sentence-builder>

1. **中文**：我们在公路自驾途中停在加油站加满了油。
   **英文**：We stopped at a gas station to refuel the car.
   > **解析**：gas station 表示“加油站”。

2. **中文**：他在丢失钱包后立刻去警察局报案登记。
   **英文**：He went to the police station to report his lost wallet.
   > **解析**：police station 表示“警察局”。

3. **中文**：他因为昨天工作过度劳累而得了严重的头痛。
   **英文**：He got a terrible headache after working late yesterday.
   > **解析**：headache 表示“头痛”。

4. **中文**：我们在图书馆里专心致志地学习了整个下午。
   **英文**：We studied hard in the library all afternoon.
   > **解析**：all afternoon 表示“整个下午”。

5. **中文**：前天天空阴云密布，一整天都在持续下雨。
   **英文**：It rained continuously all day the day before yesterday.
   > **解析**：all day 表示“一整天”。

6. **中文**：为了完成紧急的项目方案，他加班忙碌了一整夜。
   **英文**：He worked hard on the urgent project all night.
   > **解析**：all night 表示“一整夜”。

7. **中文**：在那时，我们还没有意识到这个决定的重要性。
   **英文**：At that time, we did not realize the importance.
   > **解析**：at that time 表示“在那时/当时”。

8. **中文**：他在回家路上偶遇了高中时代的同班同学。
   **英文**：He ran into his high school classmate on his way home.
   > **解析**：way home 表示“回家路上”。

9. **中文**：下班后，同事们聚在街角酒吧喝啤酒放松。
   **英文**：Colleagues gathered at a local bar for drinks after work.
   > **解析**：bar 表示“酒吧”。

10. **中文**：在炎热的夏天喝一杯冰镇饮料让人感觉非常爽快。
   **英文**：Having a cold drink feels refreshing in hot summer.
   > **解析**：drink 表示“饮料/喝”。

11. **中文**：请向大家清楚地解释一下事情发生的经过。
   **英文**：Please explain clearly how the incident happened.
   > **解析**：explain 表示“解释/说明”。

12. **中文**：他在半小时前收拾好行李离开了公司。
   **英文**：He packed his bags and left the office half an hour ago.
   > **解析**：left 是 leave 的过去式，表示“离开了”。

13. **中文**：他不小心在嘈杂的人群中丢掉了自己的身份证。
   **英文**：He accidentally lost his ID card in the crowd.
   > **解析**：lost 表示“丢失了”。

14. **中文**：汽车在红灯前平稳地停了下来。
   **英文**：The car stopped smoothly in front of the red light.
   > **解析**：stopped 表示“停下了/停止了”。

15. **中文**：虽然多次尝试失败，但他依然没有放弃。
   **英文**：He tried many times and never gave up.
   > **解析**：tried 表示“尝试了”。

16. **中文**：如果你遇到困难，可以向身边的朋友寻求帮助。
   **英文**：If you have trouble, ask your friends for help.
   > **解析**：ask 表示“询问/请求”。

17. **中文**：我对这件突发事情的来龙去脉一点都不了解。
   **英文**：I don't know the truth of this incident at all.
   > **解析**：at all 用于否定句表示“根本/一点也”。

18. **中文**：从这里步行前往火车站只需要十五分钟。
   **英文**：Walking to the train station takes only fifteen minutes.
   > **解析**：fifteen 表示“十五”。

19. **中文**：对不起，是我记错了时间，我给出了错误的答复。
   **英文**：Sorry, I gave the wrong answer by mistake.
   > **解析**：wrong 表示“错误的/不对的”。

20. **中文**：警方正在严密调查昨天在市中心发生的这起事件。
   **英文**：Police are investigating yesterday's incident downtown.
   > **解析**：incident 表示“事件/事故”。
