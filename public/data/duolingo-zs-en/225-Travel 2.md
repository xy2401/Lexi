## 单元讲解

本单元围绕"描述理想的度假地点"展开。词表里有目的地与场景词汇（tropical, volcano, off the coast of, outskirts, in the middle of nowhere, diner, deck），也有旅行规划与意外（itinerary, backup plan, just in case, sold out, spontaneous, unpredictable, stormy）。你可以用这些词讨论旅行计划：I want to get away to a tropical island off the coast of somewhere. / Not only is it beautiful, but there's a volcano you can hike. / The weather is unpredictable and stormy, so we need a backup plan just in case. / The hotel is sold out—let's be spontaneous and find a diner on the outskirts.

这里值得注意 not only...but (also) 的递进结构，以及 in the middle of nowhere 这个生动的习语——它形容一个极其偏僻、前不着村后不着店的地方。spontaneous 和 itinerary 形成有趣的对比：一个随性，一个按计划。

学完本单元，你可以用英语描绘理想度假地、讨论旅行中的变数，并在计划与随性之间表达偏好。

## Tips

- **in the middle of nowhere** — 前不着村后不着店、极其偏僻的地方。搭配：in the middle of nowhere, out in the middle of nowhere。The diner was in the middle of nowhere. 这是一个常用口语表达，带点夸张，不一定真的荒无人烟。

- **itinerary / backup plan / just in case** — itinerary 是行程表，backup plan 是备用方案，just in case 是"以防万一"。搭配：plan an itinerary, have a backup plan, take an umbrella just in case。I made an itinerary, but I also have a backup plan just in case things go wrong.

- **spontaneous** — 自发的、随性的、说走就走的。搭配：a spontaneous trip, be spontaneous。Let's be spontaneous—no itinerary! 反义词是 planned。注意发音：/spɑːnˈteɪniəs/。

- **off the coast of** — 在……海岸外（的海上）。搭配：an island off the coast of Italy, off the coast of Africa。There's a tropical island off the coast. 注意 off 表示"离开海岸一段距离"，不是 on the coast（在海岸上）。

- **sold out** — 售罄、满员。搭配：be sold out, the tickets are sold out。The hotel is sold out for the weekend. 注意 sold out 是形容词性短语：It's sold out.（× It's sold-out. 作表语时不加连字符）

- **get away** — 逃离、去度假。搭配：get away from it all, a weekend get-away。I need to get away for a few days. 名词形式 getaway（连写）= 短途度假。注意 get away 强调"离开日常"。

- **unpredictable / stormy** — unpredictable 是不可预测的，stormy 是暴风雨的。搭配：unpredictable weather, a stormy night。The weather here is unpredictable—one minute sun, the next stormy. 构词：un- + predict + -able。

- **volcano / hike** — volcano 是火山，hike 是徒步。搭配：hike up a volcano, an active volcano。You can hike to the top of the volcano. 注意 volcano 的复数是 volcanoes（也可写 volcanos）。

- **tropical** — 热带的。搭配：a tropical island, tropical weather, tropical storm。构词：tropics（热带地区）→ tropical（形容词）。I dream of a tropical getaway.

- **not only...than / more...than** — not only 表"不仅"，常与 but also 搭配：Not only is it cheap, but it's also beautiful. more...than 表比较：It's more spontaneous than planned. 注意 not only 放句首时主句要倒装。

- **diner / deck** — diner 是美式小餐馆（通常路边、复古风格），deck 是甲板或露台。搭配：eat at a diner, sit on the deck。We stopped at a diner on the outskirts. / Let's sit on the deck and watch the sunset.

- **outskirts** — 郊区、市郊。搭配：on the outskirts of town, the outskirts。The hotel is on the outskirts. 注意 outskirts 通常用复数，搭配介词 on（不是 in）。

- **sunburn** — 晒伤。搭配：get a sunburn, sunburn cream。I got a bad sunburn on the tropical beach. 注意 sunburn 可作名词也可作动词：I got sunburned. / I have a sunburn.
