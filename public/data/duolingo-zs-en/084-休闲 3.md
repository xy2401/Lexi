## 单元讲解

本单元围绕"谈论休闲娱乐活动"展开。词表里有音乐/艺术类型（pop, rock, rap, painting, dancing）和运动（hockey, kick, throw, catch）。你可以练习：She's a famous pop artist. / He kicked the ball and scored a goal! / I'm getting better at dancing. / The stadium was full. / Wow, that was fantastic!

不规则过去式：caught（catch）, threw（throw）, went（go）, kicked（kick，规则）。worse / worst 是 bad 的比较级和最高级。

famous for 表示"以……闻名"。get better at 表示"在……方面变得更好"。

## Tips

- **famous for** — 以……闻名。搭配：famous for its music, famous for painting, famous for hockey。例句：This city is famous for its rock music. / She's famous for her paintings. 注意 famous for + 原因。

- **get better at** — 在……方面进步。搭配：get better at dancing, get better at hockey。例句：I'm getting better at painting. / She got better at rap. 注意 at 后接名词或动名词。

- **caught / catch** — 接住了。catch → caught → caught。搭配：caught the ball, catch a cold, catch a bus。例句：He caught the ball! / I caught a cold. 注意 catch 的过去式是 caught（不是 ✗ catched）。

- **threw / throw** — 扔了。throw → threw → thrown。搭配：threw the ball, throw a party, throw away。例句：She threw the ball to me. / He threw a fantastic party. 注意三个形式都不同。

- **worse / worst** — 更差 / 最差。bad → worse → worst。搭配：worse than, the worst day, get worse。例句：The weather got worse. / That was the worst game ever. 注意 bad / worse / worst 是不规则变化。

- **pop / rock / rap** — 流行 / 摇滚 / 说唱。搭配：pop music, a rock band, rap artist。例句：She listens to pop. / He's a rap artist. / They play rock. 注意这些音乐类型通常不可数。

- **hockey / stadium** — 曲棍球/冰球 / 体育场。搭配：play hockey, a hockey game, at the stadium。例句：Let's go to the hockey game at the stadium. / The stadium was full.

- **kick / goal** — 踢 / 进球。搭配：kick the ball, score a goal, kicked into the goal。例句：He kicked the ball and scored a goal! / She kicked it into the net. 注意 score a goal 是固定搭配。

- **artist / dancer** — 艺术家 / 舞者。搭配：a pop artist, a famous dancer, a painting artist。构词：art + -ist, dance + -er。例句：She's a fantastic dancer. / He's a famous artist.

- **ready for** — 准备好迎接。搭配：ready for the game, ready for dancing, Are you ready for...? 例句：Are you ready for the concert? / I'm ready for hockey. 注意 ready for + 名词，ready to + 动词。

- **not bad / wow / cool** — 还不错 / 哇 / 酷。搭配：Not bad! / Wow, amazing! / That's cool! 例句：— How was the game? — Not bad! / Wow! That was fantastic! 注意这些是口语评价用语。

- **except** — 除了。搭配：except for, everyone except, except me。例句：Everyone went except me. / I like all music except rap. 注意 except 和 except for 的区别：except 后接同类事物，except for 后接不同类。

<quiz-sentence-builder>

1. **中文**：那位街头艺术家为路边的音乐人绘制了一副绝妙的肖像画。  
   **英文**：The street artist painted a stunning portrait of the musician.
   > **解析**：**artist** 作可数名词意为“艺术家、画家”。street artist 指“街头艺术家”，在句中作主语，描绘文化艺术休闲活动。

2. **中文**：我们喜欢在周五晚上跟着富有活力的流行音乐跳舞。  
   **英文**：We love to dance to energetic pop music on Friday nights.
   > **解析**：**dance** 作不及物动词意为“跳舞”。dance to music 意为“伴着音乐跳舞”，是描述舞蹈休闲爱好的固定搭配。

3. **中文**：这座海滨度假小镇以其热闹非凡的夜市而闻名。  
   **英文**：This coastal town is famous for its vibrant night markets.
   > **解析**：**famous for** 是固定形容词短语，意为“以……闻名的/因……而出名的”，后接名词短语作介词 for 的宾语。

4. **中文**：在最后一分钟踢进制胜一球让所有的球迷都陷入狂欢。  
   **英文**：Scoring the winning goal in the final minute excited all fans.
   > **解析**：**goal** 作可数名词在此意为“（足球等运动中的）进球、得分”。score a goal 意为“踢进一球/得分”。

5. **中文**：她是全场唯一获得起立鼓掌致意的表演者。  
   **英文**：She was the only performer who received a standing ovation.
   > **解析**：**only** 作形容词意为“唯一的、仅有的”。the only performer 指“唯一的演出者”，限定修饰名词。

6. **中文**：他喜欢在健身锻炼时听美国说唱音乐。  
   **英文**：He enjoys listening to American rap music while working out.
   > **解析**：**rap** 作不可数名词意为“说唱音乐、拉普”。rap music 作名词短语，描述流行音乐休闲偏好。

7. **中文**：投手投出了一个快速球，让击球手猝不及防。  
   **英文**：The pitcher threw a fast ball that caught the batter off guard.
   > **解析**：**threw** 是不规则动词 throw 的过去式，意为“投掷、扔出”。在句中作谓语动词，描述体育比赛中的动态投球动作。

8. **中文**：“哇，那位杂技演员的高空走钢丝绝技真是太不可思议了！”观众惊呼道。  
   **英文**：Wow, that acrobat's high-wire stunt was truly unbelievable.
   > **解析**：**wow** 作感叹词意为“哇、惊叹”，用于在观看演出、比赛或精彩休闲节目时表达强烈的赞叹与佩服之情。

9. **中文**：守门员在皮球飞越球门线前的一瞬间接住了球。  
   **英文**：The goalkeeper caught the flying ball just before it crossed.
   > **解析**：**caught** 是不规则动词 catch 的过去式，意为“接住、抓住”。在句中作谓语动词，描述球类运动中的精彩防守捕捉。

10. **中文**：除了约翰之外，我们组里的每个人都非常享受这场摇滚音乐会。  
   **英文**：Everyone in our group enjoyed the rock concert except John (famous for).
   > **解析**：**except** 作介词意为“除了……之外（不包括）”。在句中后接专有名词 John，表示排除个体。
