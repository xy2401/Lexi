## 单元讲解

本单元围绕"分享自驾游的经历"展开。核心是自驾相关表达：Follow the GPS.（跟着导航走。）、We need gas.（我们需要加油了。）、Take the highway.（走高速公路。）、Is there a parking space?（有停车位吗？）、The engine is fine.（引擎没问题。）。

词表里有驾驶词汇（driver's license, engine, gas, highway, tire, traffic light）和旅途词汇（road trip, rest stop, parking space, one-way）。你可以练习：I didn't know the road was one-way. / Do you think we can get there on time? / She told me to follow the sign. / Wait for me at the rest stop!

do you think 用于征求对方看法。on foot 表示"步行"。

## Tips

- **road trip** — 公路旅行。搭配：go on a road trip, a long road trip, road trip snacks。例句：We went on a road trip last summer. / Road trips are fun! 注意 road trip 是固定搭配。

- **GPS / follow** — 导航 / 跟随。搭配：follow the GPS, follow the sign, follow the road。例句：Follow the GPS—it knows the way. / Follow the signs to the highway. 注意 follow 后面接具体对象。

- **gas / engine** — 汽油 / 引擎。搭配：need gas, a gas station, the engine is running, start the engine。例句：We need gas—where's the nearest station? / The engine won't start! 注意 gas 在美式英语中指汽油（英式 petrol）。

- **highway / traffic light** — 高速公路 / 红绿灯。搭配：take the highway, on the highway, a red traffic light。例句：Take the highway—it's faster. / Stop at the traffic light. 注意 highway 比 road 更具体（高速路）。

- **driver's license** — 驾照。搭配：get a driver's license, have a license, show your license。例句：Do you have a driver's license? / I got my license last year. 注意美式 driver's license，英式 driving licence。

- **park / parking space** — 停车 / 停车位。搭配：park the car, a parking space, parking lot。例句：Can you park here? / There's no parking space. 注意 park 既是动词（停车）也是名词（公园）。

- **rest stop** — 休息站。搭配：at a rest stop, stop at the rest stop。例句：Let's stop at the rest stop. / We took a break at a rest stop. 注意 rest stop 通常在高速公路旁。

- **rent / tire** — 租 / 轮胎。搭配：rent a car, a rental car, a flat tire, change a tire。例句：We rented a car for the trip. / We had a flat tire! 注意 flat tire = 爆胎/漏气。

- **one-way / sign** — 单行道 / 标志。搭配：a one-way street, a road sign, follow the sign。例句：This is a one-way street! / The sign says "No Parking". 注意 one-way 的反义是 two-way。

- **get there / on foot** — 到达 / 步行。搭配：get there on time, get there by car, on foot。例句：How long to get there? / We had to go on foot. 注意 on foot 不加冠词（不说 ✗ on the foot）。

- **do you think / believe** — 你觉得 / 相信。搭配：Do you think...? / I can't believe it! 例句：Do you think we'll get there on time? / I can't believe the traffic! 注意 Do you think 后接从句。

- **said / told / didn't know** — 说了 / 告诉了 / 不知道。搭配：She said..., He told me..., I didn't know...。例句：She said to take the highway. / He told me about the road trip. / I didn't know it was so far!

<quiz-sentence-builder>

1. **中文**：我们应该跟着GPS导航走以保持在路线内。  
   **英文**：We should follow the GPS navigation to stay on route.
   > **解析**：GPS 表示“GPS全球定位系统/导航”；follow 意为“遵循/跟着”。

2. **中文**：我们需要在加油站停下来加点汽油。  
   **英文**：We need to stop at the station to get some gas.
   > **解析**：gas 表示“汽油（美式）”；get some gas 意为“加油”。

3. **中文**：走宽阔的高速公路比城市道路快得多。  
   **英文**：Taking the open highway is much faster than city roads.
   > **解析**：highway 表示“高速公路”。

4. **中文**：你有租车所需的有效驾照吗？  
   **英文**：Do you have a valid driver's license for renting a car?
   > **解析**：driver's license 表示“驾驶执照/驾照”；renting 意为“租”。

5. **中文**：在市中心很难找到停车位。  
   **英文**：It was very difficult to find a parking space downtown.
   > **解析**：parking space 表示“停车位”。

6. **中文**：汽车引擎开始发出怪异的响声。  
   **英文**：The car engine started making a strange noise.
   > **解析**：engine 表示“发动机/引擎”。

7. **中文**：告诉我我们能否在日落前到达那里。  
   **英文**：Let me know if we can get there before sunset.
   > **解析**：get there 表示“到达那里”。

8. **中文**：我们在高速公路休息站短暂地休息了一下。  
   **英文**：We took a relaxing break at the highway rest stop.
   > **解析**：rest stop 表示“（公路）休息站/休息处”。

9. **中文**：他们一起去进行了一场令人兴奋的夏季自驾游。  
   **英文**：They went on an exciting summer road trip together.
   > **解析**：road trip 表示“公路旅行/自驾游”。

10. **中文**：哦不！我们在去海滩的路上遇到了一个瘪胎。  
   **英文**：Oh no! We got a flat tire on our way to the beach.
   > **解析**：tire 表示“轮胎”；flat tire 意为“瘪胎/爆胎”。

11. **中文**：小心一点，因为这是一条狭窄的单行道。  
   **英文**：Be careful because this is a narrow one-way street.
   > **解析**：one-way 表示“单向的/单行道的”。

12. **中文**：当红绿灯变红时请停车。  
   **英文**：Stop the car when the traffic light turns red.
   > **解析**：traffic light 表示“交通信号灯/红绿灯”。

13. **中文**：我们决定租一辆敞篷车去旅行。  
   **英文**：We decided to rent a convertible car for the trip.
   > **解析**：rent 表示“租用/租赁”。

14. **中文**：遵循路牌指示寻找国家公园入口。  
   **英文**：Follow the road sign to find the national park entrance.
   > **解析**：sign 表示“路牌/标志/指示牌”。

15. **中文**：你认为我们能在天黑前到达酒店吗？  
   **英文**：Do you think we will reach our hotel before dark?
   > **解析**：Do you think... 表达“你认为……吗？”（询问对方看法）。

16. **中文**：我不知道那条路封闭修路了。  
   **英文**：I didn't know that the road was closed for repair.
   > **解析**：didn't know 表示“不知道”。

17. **中文**：因为没有公交车，我们不得不徒步继续前进。  
   **英文**：Since there was no bus, we had to continue on foot.
   > **解析**：on foot 表示“步行/走路”（介词短语）。

18. **中文**：请在休息站入口附近等我。  
   **英文**：Please wait for me near the rest stop entrance.
   > **解析**：wait for me 表示“等等我/等我”。

19. **中文**：我们买了一些薯片和柠檬水作为零食。  
   **英文**：We bought some potato chips and lemonade for snacks.
   > **解析**：chips 表示“薯片/炸薯条”。

20. **中文**：我不敢相信沿海高山的风景有多美！  
   **英文**：I can't believe how beautiful the coastal mountain scenery is!
   > **解析**：believe 表示“相信”；can't believe 意为“简直不敢相信”。
