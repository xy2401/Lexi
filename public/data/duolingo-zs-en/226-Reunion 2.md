## 单元讲解

本单元围绕"分享令人惊讶的近况"展开，场景是老友重逢或同学聚会。词表里有回忆与时间表达（ages, back then, look back on），有人际关系词汇（bond, influence, take after, related, neither of），也有"发现与结果"的表达（find out, turn out, stand out）。你可以用这些词聊重逢的惊喜：It's been ages! / Back then, neither of us was fluent in English. / I found out that she's a professor now—who would've thought? / It turns out we have more in common than I thought. / Looking back on our campus days, we really had a strong bond.

这里值得注意 turn out 和 find out 的区别：find out 强调"发现"（主动获取信息），turn out 强调"结果是"（事情的发展出乎意料）。另外，take after 是一个有趣的短语动词——它表示"长得像/性格像（长辈）"：She takes after her mother.

学完本单元，你可以用英语在重逢场景中分享近况、回忆过去、表达惊讶，并谈论人与人之间的相似和影响。

## Tips

- **turn out / find out** — turn out = 结果是（强调意外）：It turned out he was right. find out = 发现、查明（强调获取信息）：I found out the truth. 区别：turn out 主语常是事情，find out 主语常是人。

- **take after** — （外貌或性格）像（长辈）。搭配：take after sb, take after your mother/father。She takes after her dad—same smile. 注意 take after 只用于家庭成员间的相似，不用于朋友。

- **look back on** — 回顾、回想。搭配：look back on the past, look back on our school days。Looking back on campus life, I feel nostalgic. 比 remember 更有"回望过去"的画面感。

- **ages** — 很久（口语夸张说法）。搭配：It's been ages, for ages, ages ago。It's been ages since we met! / I haven't seen you in ages. 注意 ages 是复数，表"好多年"，实际可能只是几个月。

- **back then** — 那时候、当时。搭配：back then, back in those days。Back then, we were just students. 比 at that time 更口语化，带有怀旧感。常放在句首。

- **bond** — 纽带、情感联系。搭配：a strong bond, bond with sb, form a bond。We bonded over our love of music. 可作名词也可作动词。注意 bond 比 friendship 更强调深层的情感连接。

- **fluent** — 流利的（语言）。搭配：fluent in English, speak fluent French。She's fluent in three languages. 注意搭配介词 in：fluent in（不是 × fluent of）。名词是 fluency。

- **stand out** — 突出、显眼。搭配：stand out from the crowd, stand out in a group。She really stood out on campus. 注意 stand out 是不及物动词短语，不接宾语（× stand out others）。

- **make a fool** — 出丑、闹笑话。完整搭配：make a fool of yourself, make a fool of sb。I made a fool of myself at happy hour. 注意 make a fool of yourself = 让自己出丑。

- **neither of** — 两者都不。搭配：neither of us/them, neither of the two。Neither of us was ready. 注意 neither 后动词用单数（正式）或复数（口语均可）：Neither of them is/are here.

- **whose / whoever** — whose 是"谁的"（所有格关系代词），whoever 是"无论是谁"。搭配：whose idea was it, whoever said that。Whose turn is it? / Whoever wins, I'll be happy. 注意 whose 可引导定语从句：the person whose car was stolen。

- **influence / related** — influence 是影响（可作名词和动词），related 是相关的/有亲属关系的。搭配：have an influence on, be related to。She influenced my taste in music. / Are you two related? 注意 related 既可指"有关联"也可指"有亲戚关系"。

- **happy hour** — 欢乐时光（酒吧/餐厅的打折时段，通常下午5-7点）。搭配：go to happy hour, happy hour drinks。Let's meet at happy hour. 这是北美社交文化中的常见概念。
