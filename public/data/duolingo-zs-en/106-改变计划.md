## 单元讲解

本单元围绕"讨论修改计划"展开。核心是改变计划的表达：I wasn't happy to go sailing, so let's play badminton instead.（我本来就不想去帆船，我们改打羽毛球吧。）、What's wrong? We weren't going to cook—we were going to order.（怎么了？我们不是说好不做饭——叫外卖的吗？）、Can I borrow your racket?（我能借你的球拍吗？）。

词表里有运动词汇（badminton, chess, ping pong, sailing, skateboard）和日常词汇（borrow, lend, order, recipe, puzzle）。你可以练习：Let's play chess instead. / I'll lend you my skateboard. / That sounds good! / What else can we do?

instead 表示"改为、反而"。borrow（借入）和 lend（借出）方向相反。

## Tips

- **instead** — 改为、反而。搭配：do this instead, instead of that。例句：Let's play chess instead. / I'll have tea instead of coffee. 注意 instead 放句末或句首，instead of + 名词/动名词。

- **borrow / lend** — 借入 / 借出。搭配：borrow a book from, lend me your racket。例句：Can I borrow your racket? / I'll lend you my skateboard. 注意 borrow = 从别人那里借，lend = 借给别人。

- **sounds good** — 听起来不错。搭配：That sounds good! / Sounds good to me! 例句：— Let's play ping pong. — Sounds good! 注意 Sounds good 是口语中常用的同意表达。

- **wasn't / weren't** — 不是 / 不是（复数）。搭配：I wasn't, we weren't, it wasn't。例句：I wasn't ready. / We weren't going to cook. 注意 wasn't = was not, weren't = were not。

- **order / cook** — 点餐 / 做饭。搭配：order food, order pizza, cook dinner。例句：Let's order instead of cooking. / I'll cook if you order dessert. 注意 order 在餐饮中是"点餐/叫外卖"。

- **badminton / ping pong / chess** — 羽毛球 / 乒乓球 / 象棋。搭配：play badminton, play ping pong, play chess。例句：Let's play badminton. / Do you want to play chess? 注意球类运动前不加 the。

- **racket / skateboard / sailing** — 球拍 / 滑板 / 帆船。搭配：a badminton racket, ride a skateboard, go sailing。例句：I need a racket for badminton. / He rides a skateboard to school. 注意 go sailing = 去帆船运动。

- **what's wrong / else** — 怎么了 / 其他。搭配：What's wrong? / What else? / something else。例句：What's wrong with the plan? / What else can we do? 注意 What's wrong? 可表示关心或不满。

- **all sorts of / extra** — 各种各样的 / 额外的。搭配：all sorts of things, extra time, extra money。例句：We can do all sorts of things. / I have an extra racket. 注意 all sorts of = all kinds of。

- **recipe / bread / sweet** — 食谱 / 面包 / 甜点。搭配：a recipe for bread, a sweet tooth。例句：I found a recipe for bread. / Do you want something sweet? 注意 recipe 的发音：/ˈresɪpi/。

- **happy to / around** — 乐意 / 大约。搭配：happy to help, around five, look around。例句：I'm happy to lend you my racket. / It costs around ten dollars. 注意 around 可表示"大约"或"到处"。

- **fell / put / home** — 掉了 / 放 / 家。搭配：fell off, put away, go home, at home。例句：I fell off my skateboard! / Put the puzzle away. / Let's go home. 注意 fall → fell → fallen。
