## 单元讲解

本单元围绕"分享过去的回忆"展开，以年代为话题。核心是年代和回忆表达：I was born in the nineties—if you ask me, that was the best decade.（我出生在九十年代——要我说，那是最好的十年。）、In the fifties, people couldn't imagine the inventions we have nowadays.（在五十年代，人们无法想象我们现在的发明。）、To be honest, I don't remember the twentieth century, but my grandparents remind me of it.（说实话，我不记得二十世纪了，但我的祖父母会让我想起它。）、The population has grown so much since 1992—either you adapt or you don't.（自1992年以来人口增长了很多——要么适应，要么不适应。）。

词表里有年代词汇（eighties, fifties, nineties, nineteenth, twentieth）和回忆词汇（remember, remind, born, grown）。你可以练习：It was discovered in 1802. / The twenty-first century is different. / I couldn't do it. / That's my generation.

the + 年代（the eighties = 80年代）。could / couldn't 表过去能力。

## Tips

- **eighties / fifties / nineteen nineties** — 八十年代 / 五十年代 / 九十年代。搭配：in the eighties, in the fifties, in the nineteen nineties。例句：I grew up in the eighties. / Music in the fifties was great. / The nineties were fun. 注意年代前加 the，用复数形式。

- **born / generation / grown** — 出生 / 一代人 / 长大了。搭配：was born in, my generation, have grown up。例句：I was born in 1992. / My generation loves technology. / The kids have grown so fast. 注意 be born in + 年份/地点。

- **remember / remind / to be honest** — 记得 / 提醒 / 说实话。搭配：remember the past, remind me of, to be honest。例句：I remember the old days. / This reminds me of childhood. / To be honest, I miss it. 注意 remind sb of sth = 让某人想起。

- **could / couldn't / either** — 能 / 不能 / 也（不）。搭配：could do, couldn't understand, not... either。例句：We could play outside all day. / I couldn't use a phone. / I don't like it either. 注意 either 用于否定句（too 用于肯定句）。

- **invention / discovery / population** — 发明 / 发现 / 人口。搭配：a great invention, make a discovery, the population grew。例句：The internet was a huge invention. / It was an important discovery. / The population has doubled. 注意 invention = 创造新事物，discovery = 发现已有事物。

- **nowadays / if you ask me / nineteenth** — 如今 / 要我说 / 十九世纪。搭配：nowadays, if you ask me, the nineteenth century。例句：Nowadays everyone has a phone. / If you ask me, it's better now. / It was built in the nineteenth century. 注意 nowadays = these days。

- **twentieth / twenty-first / twenty-fifth** — 第二十 / 第二十一 / 第二十五。搭配：the twentieth century, the twenty-first century, the twenty-fifth anniversary。例句：The twentieth century had two world wars. / We live in the twenty-first century. / It's their twenty-fifth anniversary. 注意序数词前加 the。

- **1802 / 1992 / 2016** — 年份读法。搭配：in 1802, since 1992, in 2016。例句：It was founded in 1802. / I've lived here since 1992. / In 2016, I graduated. 注意年份读法：1802 = eighteen oh two。

- **generation / born / remember** — 一代 / 出生 / 记得。搭配：the younger generation, born in the 80s, remember when。例句：The younger generation is different. / She was born in the 80s. / Remember when we had no internet? 注意 generation gap = 代沟。

- **grown / population / discovery** — 成长 / 人口 / 发现。搭配：have grown, population growth, a scientific discovery。例句：The city has grown a lot. / The population is increasing. / It was a major discovery. 注意 grow → grew → grown。

- **to be honest / if you ask me / nowadays** — 说实话 / 依我看 / 如今。搭配：to be honest with you, if you ask me, life nowadays。例句：To be honest, I prefer the old days. / If you ask me, things are better now. / Life nowadays is fast. 注意这些是表达观点的口语短语。

- **couldn't / either / remind** — 不能 / 也不 / 提醒。搭配：couldn't imagine, not either, remind sb。例句：They couldn't predict the future. / I don't miss it either. / Photos remind me of the past. 注意 couldn't = could not（过去不能）。

<quiz-sentence-builder>

1. **中文**：他出生于九十年代的一个小城镇。
   **英文**：He was born in a small town during the nineteen nineties.
   > **解析**：born 表示“出生”；was born 意为“出生于”。

2. **中文**：科学家们在这一年代做出了重大科技发现。
   **英文**：Scientists made a major discovery in renewable energy.
   > **解析**：discovery 表示“发现/重大突破”。

3. **中文**：在八十年代，迪斯科和摇滚流行音乐风靡全球。
   **英文**：In the eighties, disco and pop music were popular worldwide.
   > **解析**：eighties 表示“八十年代（1980s）”。

4. **中文**：五十年代电视机开始进入千家万户。
   **英文**：Television sets entered ordinary households in the fifties.
   > **解析**：fifties 表示“五十年代（1950s）”。

5. **中文**：年轻一代人成长于互联网和智能手机时代。
   **英文**：The younger generation grew up with smartphones and internet.
   > **解析**：generation 表示“一代人/世代”。

6. **中文**：这些老树在这个古老的公园里已经生长了数百年。
   **英文**：These trees have grown here for more than a hundred years.
   > **解析**：grown 是 grow 的过去分词，表示“生长/成长”。

7. **中文**：依我看，那个年代的歌曲比现在的更好听。
   **英文**：If you ask me, songs from that decade sound much better.
   > **解析**：If you ask me 表示“依我看/在我看来”（表达观点）。

8. **中文**：智能手机的发明彻底改变了人类的沟通方式。
   **英文**：The invention of smartphones changed human communication.
   > **解析**：invention 表示“发明/创造”。

9. **中文**：九十年代见证了个人电脑的快速普及。
   **英文**：Computers became widespread during the nineteen nineties.
   > **解析**：nineteen nineties 表示“1990年代”。

10. **中文**：摇滚乐在十九六十年代在全球范围内兴起。
   **英文**：Rock music spread rapidly in the nineteen sixties.
   > **解析**：nineteen sixties 表示“1960年代”。

11. **中文**：这项伟大的技术突破发生于十九世纪末。
   **英文**：That historical event took place in the nineteenth century.
   > **解析**：nineteenth 表示“第十九/十九世纪的”。

12. **中文**：现如今，人们可以通过手机即时获取海量信息。
   **英文**：Nowadays, people access information instantly via mobile phones.
   > **解析**：Nowadays 表示“现今/如今”。

13. **中文**：这座城市的常住人口在过去十年间迅速增长。
   **英文**：The city's population grew dramatically over the past decade.
   > **解析**：population 表示“人口/居民数量”。

14. **中文**：你还记得我们第一次见面的那个阳光明媚的下午吗？
   **英文**：Do you remember the afternoon when we first met?
   > **解析**：remember 表示“记得/想起”。

15. **中文**：请提醒我在今天下班前回复这封电子邮件。
   **英文**：Please remind me to reply to the email before end of day.
   > **解析**：remind 表示“提醒/使想起”。

16. **中文**：老实说，我并不十分喜欢那种现代建筑风格。
   **英文**：To be honest, I don't really care for modern architecture.
   > **解析**：To be honest 表示“老实说/说实话”。

17. **中文**：人类在二十世纪见证了两次科技革命。
   **英文**：Space exploration advanced quickly in the twentieth century.
   > **解析**：twentieth 表示“第二十/二十世纪的”。

18. **中文**：我们生活在充满机遇与挑战的二十一世纪。
   **英文**：We live in the fast-changing twenty-first century today.
   > **解析**：twenty-first 表示“第二十一/二十一世纪”。

19. **中文**：我过去无法理解为什么人们那么喜欢老旧的胶片唱片。
   **英文**：I couldn't understand why people loved vintage vinyl records.
   > **解析**：couldn't 表示“不能/无法（could not）”。

20. **中文**：要么今天解决这个问题，要么推迟到下周。
   **英文**：Either fix the issue today or delay it until next week.
   > **解析**：Either...or... 表示“要么……要么……”。
