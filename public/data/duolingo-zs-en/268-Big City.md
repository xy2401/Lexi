## 单元讲解

本单元围绕"描述你的家乡"展开，但视角是从家乡看向大城市——一个年轻人离开 small but beautiful 的小镇，到 the big city 闯荡。词表里有城市生活的具体场景（deli, laundromat, broadway musical, Hollywood, show business），也有描述心态和价值观的词（uncertain, hardworking, values, artistic, conventional）。你可以练习这样的叙述：She left her hometown, far from her home, to make it in show business. / The big city is full of unique opportunities, but you have to take a risk and give it a chance. / It's hard to adjust when everything is up to your standards or not.

词表里有几组对比关系值得注意：conventional 和 artistic 代表两种生活态度；agriculture 和 show business 代表两种谋生方式；small but beautiful 和 the big city 代表两种环境。make the most 和 make it 都含 make，但意思不同——前者是"充分利用"，后者是"成功、出人头地"。looking to 表示"打算、寻求"，比 want to 多一层主动寻找的意味。

## Tips

- **make it** — 口语中极常用的短语，意为"成功、闯出名堂"：She finally made it in Hollywood. 也可以表示"赶得上、做得到"：Can you make it to the party? 注意和 make the most（充分利用）区分：Make the most of every opportunity.

- **deli** — delicatessen 的缩写，指卖熟食、三明治、奶酪等的小店，在纽约街头极其常见。口语中直接说 deli，几乎没人说全称。搭配：grab a sandwich at the deli / the deli on the corner。

- **laundromat** — 自助洗衣店。laundry（洗衣）+ -o- + mat（自动）。美国大城市里很多公寓没有洗衣机，居民去 laundromat 投币洗衣。可以这样联想：laundry + automatic = laundromat。

- **show business** — 演艺界、娱乐业，常缩写为 showbiz。包括电影、电视、戏剧、音乐等。搭配：go into show business / a career in show business。Hollywood 和 broadway musical 都是 show business 的具体场景。

- **conventional / artistic** — conventional 指传统的、循规蹈矩的：a conventional career / conventional values。artistic 指有艺术气质的、有创造力的。两者常形成对比：Her parents wanted a conventional life for her, but she was too artistic.

- **make the most** — 充分利用，后接 of：Make the most of your time in the city. 注意和 make it（成功）不同。类似表达：get the most out of / take full advantage of。

- **adjust** — 适应、调整。搭配：adjust to a new city / adjust to the surroundings。注意 adjust to 后接名词或动名词：It took her a year to adjust to living far from home. 同义词 adapt，但 adjust 更侧重微调，adapt 更侧重整体适应。

- **give it a chance** — 给它一个机会、试试看。口语中非常常用的鼓励表达：I know it seems scary, but give it a chance. 类似表达：give it a try / give it a shot。

- **looking to** — 打算、正在寻找。比 want to 更正式、更有目的性：She's looking to start a new career. / We're looking to move to the big city. 注意后接动词原形。

- **up to your standards** — 达到你的标准。up to 在这里表示"达到（某水平）"：The apartment wasn't up to her standards. 反义：below your standards。搭配：high standards / meet the standards。

- **take a risk** — 冒险。搭配：take a big risk / take a risk on something。例句：Moving to a new city is taking a risk, but it can pay off. 同义表达：take a chance / take a leap of faith。

- **values** — 价值观、价值观念（复数）。注意和 value（价值、价格）区分：family values / traditional values / personal values。词表里的 hardworking, conventional 都可以用来形容一个人的 values。

- **surroundings** — 环境、周围的事物（常用复数）。搭配：new surroundings / beautiful surroundings / adjust to your surroundings。注意拼写：两个 r，以 -ings 结尾，通常用复数形式。
