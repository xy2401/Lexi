## 单元讲解

本单元的主题是"分享音乐经历"，场景是聊音乐史与个人音乐品味。词表可以分成四组：音乐风格与元素（rock and roll, electronic, alternative, rhythm, soundtrack）、音乐人物与产业（songwriter, release, influence, influential, inspire）、时代与回忆（date back, in those days, hippie, pop culture, lifetime）、以及评论与口语（in my eyes, must have, it all, what, worldwide, and had）。拼起来就是一段乐迷的回忆：In those days, rock and roll influenced pop culture worldwide. That songwriter must have inspired a whole lifetime of listeners — in my eyes, it all started with one song.

语法主线有两个。一是 must have + 过去分词，表示对过去情况的肯定推测：She must have written it in one night.（她肯定是熬夜写的。）二是描述起源的表达：date back to...（追溯到……）与 influence/influential 的词族区分（动词影响 vs. 形容词有影响力的）。

学完这个单元，你能用英语聊音乐风格、评价艺术家的影响，还能讲述与音乐有关的个人回忆。

## Tips

- **rock and roll / electronic / alternative / rhythm** — rock and roll = 摇滚乐；electronic = 电子的：electronic music；alternative = 另类的：alternative rock（另类摇滚）；rhythm = 节奏：a strong rhythm。

- **soundtrack / release** — soundtrack = 电影原声带：the soundtrack of the film；release = 发行、发布：The band released a new album.

- **influence / influential / inspire** — influence 作动词"影响"、名词"影响"；influential = 有影响力的：an influential artist；inspire = 启发、激励：Her songs inspire me. 注意 ✗ influence somebody to do（少用）✓ influence somebody / inspire somebody to do。

- **date back / in those days / hippie** — date back (to) = 追溯到：This song dates back to 1968. in those days = 在那个年代；hippie = 嬉皮士，60 年代反文化的年轻人。

- **pop culture / worldwide / lifetime** — pop culture = 流行文化（popular culture 的缩略）；worldwide = 世界范围内：famous worldwide；lifetime = 一生：a once-in-a-lifetime concert。

- **in my eyes / must have / it all / what** — in my eyes = 在我眼里、在我看来；must have + 过去分词是对过去的肯定推测：They must have practiced for months. it all = 这一切：It all began in a garage. what 引导名词从句：I love what they do.

- **songwriter / and had** — songwriter = 歌曲创作者（song + writer 复合词）；and had 是并列过去完成时的片段：He signed the deal and had written twenty songs by then.

<quiz-sentence-builder>

1. **中文**：这首歌可以追溯到 1968 年。
   **英文**：This song dates back to 1968.
   > **解析**：date back to 是固定搭配"追溯到"；主语是单三，dates 加 s。

2. **中文**：在那个年代，人人都听摇滚乐。
   **英文**：In those days, everyone listened to rock and roll.
   > **解析**：In those days 是时间状语置于句首；listened 是过去式。

3. **中文**：乐队下周发行新专辑。
   **英文**：The band will release a new album next week.
   > **解析**：will 后接动词原形 release；next week 是将来的时间状语。

4. **中文**：她肯定练习了好几个月。
   **英文**：She must have practiced for months.
   > **解析**：must have + 过去分词表示对过去的肯定推测；for months 表时长。

5. **中文**：这位歌手对我的影响很大。
   **英文**：This singer influenced me a lot.
   > **解析**：influence 作动词"影响"；influenced 是过去式，me 作宾语。

6. **中文**：他是最有影响力的歌曲创作者之一。
   **英文**：He is one of the most influential songwriters.
   > **解析**：one of the + 最高级 + 复数名词；influential 修饰 songwriters。

7. **中文**：她的音乐启发了整整一代人。
   **英文**：Her music inspired a whole generation.
   > **解析**：inspire 是动词"启发"；inspired 是过去式。

8. **中文**：我喜欢电子音乐的节奏。
   **英文**：I love the rhythm of electronic music.
   > **解析**：the rhythm of... 用 of 表示所属；electronic 修饰 music。

9. **中文**：这部电影的原声带很棒。
   **英文**：The soundtrack of this movie is amazing.
   > **解析**：soundtrack 是"电影原声带"；is 与单数主语搭配。

10. **中文**：在我眼里，摇滚改变了流行文化。
   **英文**：In my eyes, rock and roll changed pop culture.
   > **解析**：In my eyes 是插入语表个人观点；changed 是过去式。

11. **中文**：这支乐队在全世界都很有名。
   **英文**：The band is famous worldwide.
   > **解析**：worldwide 作副词修饰 is famous，放在句末。

12. **中文**：我一生都爱着音乐。
   **英文**：I have loved music all my lifetime.
   > **解析**：现在完成时 have loved 表示持续至今的状态；all my lifetime 是时间状语。

13. **中文**：这一切都始于一家小酒吧。
   **英文**：It all started in a small bar.
   > **解析**：It all 表示"这一切"作主语；started 是过去式。

14. **中文**：我喜欢他们所做的一切。
   **英文**：I love what they do.
   > **解析**：what 引导名词从句作 love 的宾语。

15. **中文**：他签了约，并且已经写了二十首歌。
   **英文**：He signed the deal and had written twenty songs.
   > **解析**：and had 连接过去完成时，表示签约之前已完成的动作。

16. **中文**：我更喜欢另类摇滚。
   **英文**：I prefer alternative rock.
   > **解析**：alternative 修饰 rock 作定语；prefer 表偏好。

17. **中文**：那时候，嬉皮士们留着长发。
   **英文**：Hippies wore long hair in those days.
   > **解析**：hippie 的复数 hippies 作主语；wore 是 wear 的过去式。

18. **中文**：这张专辑是什么时候发行的？
   **英文**：When was this album released?
   > **解析**：一般过去时的被动疑问句：When was + 主语 + 过去分词。

19. **中文**：音乐必须打动人。
   **英文**：Music must move people.
   > **解析**：must 后接动词原形 move，表示"必须、一定"。

20. **中文**：那位作曲家启发我写出了自己的歌。
   **英文**：That songwriter inspired me to write my own songs.
   > **解析**：inspire somebody to do 是固定搭配；inspired 是过去式。

<quiz-cloze>

### 1

The tradition ____ to the 1950s.

- [x] dates back
- [ ] date back
- [ ] dated back to back

> **解析**：主语是单三，用 dates back；dated back to back 结构重复错误。

### 2

____, people wrote letters instead of emails.

- [x] In those days
- [ ] In these days ago
- [ ] On those days

> **解析**：In those days 是固定表达"在那个年代"；其余搭配不存在。

### 3

The singer ____ her first album in 2019.

- [x] released
- [ ] release
- [ ] releasing

> **解析**：in 2019 要求过去式 released；release 是原形，releasing 不能单独作谓语。

### 4

He ____ traveled the world with the band.

- [x] must have
- [ ] must to have
- [ ] must having

> **解析**：must have + 过去分词是对过去的推测；must 后直接接动词原形 have。

### 5

The teacher's words ____ my whole life.

- [x] influenced
- [ ] influential
- [ ] influences

> **解析**：需要过去式动词 influenced；influential 是形容词，influences 是现在时。

### 6
