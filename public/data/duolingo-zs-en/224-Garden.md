# Unit 224: Garden

> Garden: Ask about planting methods

## 单词

best bet, bush, could, cut down, gardener, green thumb, ground, have to be, hire, it seems like, lawn, looking, may, mow, muddy, myself, pond, pot, seed, should, shouldn't be, soil, spot, spray, stem, vegetable garden

## 单元讲解

本单元围绕"询问种植方法"展开，场景是园艺和庭院打理。词表里有植物与园艺词汇（seed, stem, bush, soil, pot, lawn, pond, vegetable garden），也有打理动作（mow, cut down, spray, plant 的意味），还有建议与判断表达（best bet, should, shouldn't be, could, may, it seems like）。你可以用这些词讨论园艺：What's the best bet for this spot? / You should plant the seeds in good soil. / It seems like the ground is too muddy. / I may hire a gardener to cut down that bush.

这里值得注意 green thumb 这个习语——它指"园艺天赋"，说 someone has a green thumb 就是说他们特别会种花种草。另外，best bet 是口语中"最佳选择"的意思：Your best bet is to plant in spring.

学完本单元，你可以用英语讨论种植方法、庭院维护，并给出或征求园艺建议。

## Tips

- **green thumb** — 园艺天赋、种花种草的本事。搭配：have a green thumb。She has a real green thumb—everything she plants grows. 注意这是美式说法，英式有时说 green fingers。这是一个习语，不能逐字翻译。

- **best bet** — 最佳选择、最靠谱的办法。搭配：your best bet is to..., the best bet for。Your best bet is to start with easy seeds. 口语中非常常用，比 best option 更随意。bet 原义是"赌注"，这里引申为"最有胜算的选择"。

- **soil / ground / muddy** — soil 是土壤（种植用的），ground 是地面（泛指），muddy 是泥泞的。搭配：rich soil, on the ground, muddy ground。The soil here is great for a vegetable garden, but after rain the ground gets muddy.

- **mow / cut down** — mow 是割草（mow the lawn），cut down 是砍倒（cut down a tree/bush）。搭配：mow the lawn, cut down a tree。注意 mow 只用于草，cut down 用于较大的植物。mow 的过去式是 mowed 或 mown。

- **seed / stem / bush** — seed 是种子，stem 是茎，bush 是灌木。搭配：plant seeds, a rose bush, the stem of a plant。生长顺序：seed → stem → plant/bush。注意 bush 比 tree 矮，通常没有明显主干。

- **lawn / vegetable garden / pond** — lawn 是草坪，vegetable garden 是菜园，pond 是池塘。搭配：mow the lawn, grow a vegetable garden, a garden pond。注意 lawn 需要 mow，garden 需要 plant/tend。

- **pot** — 花盆。搭配：a flower pot, plant in a pot, pot plant。If you don't have ground, you can grow herbs in pots. 注意 pot 也可指锅（cooking pot），要看语境。

- **hire / gardener** — hire 是雇用，gardener 是园丁。搭配：hire a gardener, hire someone to mow。I don't have a green thumb, so I hired a gardener. 构词：garden + -er = gardener（照看花园的人）。

- **it seems like** — 看起来好像、似乎。搭配：it seems like..., it seems like + 从句。It seems like the soil is too dry. 比 it seems that 更口语化。也可以说 it looks like。

- **have to be / shouldn't be** — have to be 表"必须是、一定是"（推断或要求），shouldn't be 表"不应该、按理不会"。The seeds have to be in sunlight. / It shouldn't be too hard to grow tomatoes. 注意 have to 强调客观必要，should 强调主观判断。

- **spray** — 喷洒。搭配：spray water, spray the plants, bug spray。You may need to spray the bush for insects. 可作动词也可作名词（a spray bottle）。

- **spot** — 地点、位置（口语）。搭配：a good spot, this spot, find a spot。This spot gets a lot of sun—perfect for seeds. 比 place 更口语，常指具体的一个小位置。

- **myself** — 我自己。搭配：do it myself, by myself, I'll mow it myself。I could hire someone, but I'd rather do it myself. 注意 myself 是反身代词，主语必须是 I：I did it myself.（× He did it myself.）
