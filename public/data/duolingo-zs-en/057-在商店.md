> 在商场购物时与人交流

## 单元讲解

本单元围绕"在商场购物时与人交流"展开。核心句型包括：How much is it?（多少钱？）、Would you like to pay with cash or credit card?（您想现金还是刷卡？）、How often do you go shopping?（你多久逛一次街？）。

词表里有支付方式（cash, credit card）和商店词汇（store, entrance, ad）。你可以练习：The store is open. / I saw an ad for this store. / I spent fifty dollars. / How do I get to the entrance?

got / saw / took / spent 都是不规则过去式：get → got, see → saw, take → took, spend → spent。too many 修饰可数名词：too many stores（不是 ✗ too much stores）。

## Tips

- **cash / credit card** — 现金 / 信用卡。搭配：pay with cash, pay by credit card, cash or credit card? 注意 pay with cash 和 pay by credit card 的介词不同。也可以说 pay in cash。

- **how much** — 多少钱、多少。搭配：How much is it? / How much does it cost? / How much did you spend? 注意 how much 问价格或不可数名词的量：How much water? 问可数名词用 how many。

- **how often** — 多久一次（频率）。搭配：How often do you shop? / How often do you go there? 回答：Every day. / Once a week. / Twice a month. 注意 how often 问频率，how long 问时间长度。

- **spend / spent** — 花费。spend 的过去式是 spent。搭配：spend money, spend time, spent fifty dollars。例句：I spent too much money. / She spent two hours shopping. 注意 spend 的主语是人（不是物）。

- **got / saw / took** — 不规则过去式。get → got, see → saw, take → took。搭配：got a taxi（打了出租）、saw an ad（看到一个广告）、took a taxi（坐了出租）。注意 get got got / see saw seen / take took taken。

- **kinds of** — 各种各样的。搭配：all kinds of, many kinds of, different kinds of。例句：They sell all kinds of things. 注意 kind of 也可以作副词表示"有点"：I'm kind of tired.（我有点累。）

- **entrance** — 入口。搭配：the entrance to the store, at the entrance, main entrance。反义词 exit（出口）。注意 entrance 的发音：/ˈentrəns/。来自 enter（进入）。

- **open / closed** — 开着的 / 关着的。搭配：The store is open. / The store is closed. 注意 open 和 closed 在这里是形容词。open 的动词意思也是"打开"：They opened the store at nine.

- **would you like to** — 您想要……吗？（礼貌邀请）。搭配：Would you like to try it? / Would you like to pay now? 注意比 Do you want to 更礼貌、更正式。回答：Yes, I'd like to. / No, thanks.

- **too many** — 太多（修饰可数名词）。搭配：too many stores, too many people, too many bags。注意 too many + 可数名词复数，too much + 不可数名词：too much money（不是 ✗ too many money）。

- **ad** — 广告（advertisement 的缩写）。搭配：see an ad, an ad for, online ad。注意 ad 是口语/非正式用法，正式写作中用 advertisement。复数是 ads。

- **cent** — 分（货币单位）。搭配：fifty cents, a few cents, 99 cents。注意 100 cents = 1 dollar。cent 来自拉丁语 centum（一百）。类似的：euro cent（欧分）。

<quiz-sentence-builder>

1. **中文**：我昨天在服装店买了一件新夹克。
   **英文**：I bought a new jacket at the clothing store yesterday.
   > **解析**：bought 是 buy 的过去式；store 表示“商店”。

2. **中文**：那个店员昨天把最后一套大衣卖掉了。
   **英文**：The clerk sold the last coat yesterday afternoon.
   > **解析**：sold 是 sell 的过去式；clerk 表示“店员”。

3. **中文**：我用信用卡支付了所有的衣服费用。
   **英文**：I paid for all the clothes using a credit card.
   > **解析**：paid 是 pay 的过去式；clothes 表示“衣服”。

4. **中文**：这双精致的皮鞋花了大约一百美元。
   **英文**：These leather shoes cost about one hundred dollars.
   > **解析**：cost 表示“花费/价值”；leather shoes 表示“皮鞋”。

5. **中文**：这件连衣裙的价格非常合理适中。
   **英文**：The price of this dress is very reasonable.
   > **解析**：price 表示“价格”。

6. **中文**：商场今天对所有冬装提供百分之二十的折扣。
   **英文**：The mall offers a twenty percent discount on winter clothes today.
   > **解析**：discount 表示“折扣/优惠”。

7. **中文**：请问这件蓝色衬衫有大号尺寸吗？
   **英文**：Do you have this blue shirt in a larger size?
   > **解析**：size 表示“尺寸/号数”。

8. **中文**：她把刚买的新衣服装进了大纸袋里。
   **英文**：She put her new clothes into a large paper bag.
   > **解析**：bag 表示“袋子/包”；clothes 表示“衣服”。

9. **中文**：收银员把找零和购物收据递给了我。
   **英文**：The cashier handed me the change and the receipt.
   > **解析**：cashier 表示“收银员”；receipt 表示“收据”。

10. **中文**：买完东西后请保留好你的购物收据。
   **英文**：Please keep your shopping receipt after buying items.
   > **解析**：receipt 表示“收据/发票”。

11. **中文**：我想在购买之前在试衣间试穿一下这件大衣。
   **英文**：I want to try on this coat in the fitting room.
   > **解析**：try on 表示“试穿”；fitting room 表示“试衣间”。

12. **中文**：试衣间就在商店的后方右侧。
   **英文**：The fitting room is right at the back of the store.
   > **解析**：fitting room 表示“试衣间”。

13. **中文**：这家百货商店正在举行年中大促销活动。
   **英文**：This department store is having a big summer sale.
   > **解析**：sale 表示“促销/特卖”。

14. **中文**：那位友善的店员热心地解答了我的问题。
   **英文**：The friendly clerk kindly answered all my questions.
   > **解析**：clerk 表示“店员/售货员”。

15. **中文**：这家小礼品店出售许多独具特色的手工艺品。
   **英文**：This small shop sells many unique handmade crafts.
   > **解析**：shop 表示“店铺/小店”。

16. **中文**：商店里有许多正在挑选服装的顾客。
   **英文**：There were many customers browsing clothes in the store.
   > **解析**：customers 表示“顾客”。

17. **中文**：这包名牌化妆品价格相当昂贵。
   **英文**：This designer makeup set is quite expensive.
   > **解析**：expensive 表示“昂贵的”。

18. **中文**：我们在街角买到了非常便宜又新鲜的水果。
   **英文**：We bought very cheap and fresh fruit at the corner.
   > **解析**：cheap 表示“便宜的”。

19. **中文**：请把所有购买的日常用品装进购物袋中。
   **英文**：Please place all purchased goods into the shopping bag with close.
   > **解析**：shopping bag 表示“购物袋”。

20. **中文**：这家大服装店每天吸引成百上千的购物者。
   **英文**：This big clothes store attracts hundreds of shoppers daily.
   > **解析**：clothes store 表示“服装店”。
