> 谈论节日

## 单元讲解

本单元围绕"谈论节日"展开。核心句型包括：We're planning a party.（我们在计划一个派对。）、I brought a gift for you.（我给你带了礼物。）、Let's build a snowman!（我们堆个雪人吧！）、The whole family is visiting.（全家人都要来。）。

词表里有节日相关词汇（gift, red envelope, dumpling, snack, snowman）和家庭词（grandchild, grandchildren）。你可以练习：I'm planning to visit my grandchildren. / She brought dumplings. / The children are building a snowman. / Come in and sit down!

plan to / planning 表示"计划"。brought 是 bring 的过去式。

## Tips

- **plan to / planning** — 计划。搭配：plan to visit, planning a party, make a plan。例句：We're planning to build a snowman. / I plan to visit my family. 注意 plan to do（计划做某事）。

- **brought / bring** — 带来了。bring → brought → brought。搭配：brought a gift, bring some snacks, bring to the party。例句：She brought dumplings to the party. 注意 bring（带来）和 take（带走）方向相反。

- **red envelope** — 红包。搭配：give a red envelope, receive a red envelope, red envelope with money。注意这是中国文化概念，英文直译 red envelope / red packet。春节时长辈给晚辈。

- **dumpling / snack** — 饺子 / 小吃。搭配：make dumplings, have a snack, Chinese dumplings。注意 dumpling 泛指有馅的面食（饺子、包子等）。snack 是零食/小吃。

- **snowman** — 雪人。构词：snow + man。搭配：build a snowman, make a snowman。例句：Let's build a snowman! / The children made a snowman. 注意 build / make a snowman 都可以。

- **grandchild / grandchildren** — 孙辈。搭配：my grandchild, all the grandchildren。注意 grandchild 的复数是 grandchildren（不规则）。grandchild 不分性别，具体说 grandson / granddaughter。

- **gift** — 礼物。搭配：a gift for you, give a gift, birthday gift。注意 gift 和 present 同义。gift 稍正式，present 更口语。gift 也可以指"天赋"：She has a gift for music.

- **come in / sit down** — 进来 / 坐下。搭配：Come in! / Sit down, please. / Come in and sit down. 注意这是招待客人的常用语。反义词：go out / stand up。

- **whole** — 整个的、全部的。搭配：the whole family, the whole day, the whole time。例句：The whole family is visiting. / I waited the whole day. 注意 whole 前通常加 the。

- **church** — 教堂。搭配：go to church, at church, a beautiful church。注意 go to church 表示"去做礼拜"（不加 the），go to the church 表示"去教堂那个建筑"。

- **such** — 如此的、这样的。搭配：such a good time, such as（例如）、such fun。例句：It was such a good party! / We had such fun. 注意 such + a + 形容词 + 名词：such a nice gift。

- **video** — 视频。搭配：take a video, watch a video, a video of the party。例句：I took a video of the snowman. / Let's watch the video. 注意 take a video = 拍视频。
