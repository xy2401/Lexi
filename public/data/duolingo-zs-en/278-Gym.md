# Unit 278: Gym

> Gym: Set fitness goals

## 单词

abs, cardio, cool down, effective, get, ground rules, have to, injury, left, make, need to, out of shape, pull-up, push-up, rather, resolution, session, sore, squats, stretch, trainer, treadmill, want to, warm-up, weights

## 单元讲解

本单元围绕"设定健身目标"展开，场景是健身房。词表里有具体的训练项目（cardio, squats, pull-up, push-up, weights, treadmill），有训练流程的词汇（warm-up, cool down, stretch, session），也有描述身体状态和目标的词（out of shape, sore, injury, resolution, effective）。你可以练习这样的叙述：I need to get back in shape—my resolution is to go to the gym three times a week. / I'd rather do cardio than lift weights, but I have to stretch first to avoid injury. / After the session, my abs were so sore I could barely move.

词表里有几组表达"必要性"的词值得注意：have to / need to / want to 从外部义务到内在愿望排列。ground rules 是"基本规则"，在健身语境中指训练前需要遵守的原则（比如先热身再训练）。resolution 常指新年决心（New Year's resolution），但也可以指任何坚定的决定。

## Tips

- **out of shape / in shape** — out of shape 指"身材走样、体能差"：I'm so out of shape—I can't even run a mile. in shape 指"身材好、体能好"：She's really in shape. 注意 shape 在这里不是"形状"，而是"身体状态"。搭配：get in shape / get back in shape / stay in shape。

- **cardio** — 有氧运动。cardiovascular（心血管的）的缩写。搭配：do cardio / cardio exercise / a cardio session。包括跑步、骑车、游泳等让心率升高的运动。和 strength training（力量训练）相对。口语中直接说 cardio，没人说全称。

- **warm-up / cool down** — 热身和放松。warm-up 是运动前的准备活动（名词，有连字符）；cool down 是运动后的缓和放松（动词短语）。搭配：do a warm-up / warm up before exercise / cool down after a run。注意 warm-up（名词）和 warm up（动词）的连字符区别。

- **pull-up / push-up** — 引体向上和俯卧撑。pull-up：向上拉（pull）身体，单杠动作；push-up：向下推（push）身体，地面动作。搭配：do pull-ups / do push-ups / I can't even do one pull-up。注意通常用复数：do ten push-ups。

- **squats** — 深蹲。通常用复数。搭配：do squats / a set of squats / squat down。动词形式 squat。例句：My legs were shaking after twenty squats. 健身房里最基础也最累的动作之一。

- **sore** — 酸痛的（运动后肌肉酸痛）。搭配：sore muscles / I'm so sore / my abs are sore。注意和 pain（疼痛，通常指受伤）区分：sore 是运动后的正常酸痛，pain 可能是受伤的信号。例句：I was sore for three days after that session.

- **treadmill** — 跑步机。tread（踩踏）+ mill（磨坊），原指人踩踏带动的装置，后来专指健身房里的跑步机。搭配：run on the treadmill / walk on the treadmill / get off the treadmill。例句：I ran for 30 minutes on the treadmill.

- **trainer** — 私人教练、训练师。搭配：a personal trainer / work with a trainer / hire a trainer。美式英语中更常说 personal trainer 或 coach；trainer 在英式英语中也可以指运动鞋（trainers = sneakers）。

- **resolution** — 决心、新年计划。最常见搭配是 New Year's resolution（新年决心）。搭配：make a resolution / keep a resolution / break a resolution。例句：My resolution is to work out every day. 注意 resolution 也有"分辨率"的意思（屏幕分辨率），看语境。

- **session** — 一次训练、一场。搭配：a gym session / a training session / a workout session。例句：That was an intense session—I'm exhausted. 比 workout 更正式一点，强调"一段时间的活动"。

- **injury** — 受伤、损伤。动词是 injure。搭配：suffer an injury / prevent injury / a knee injury。例句：You need to stretch to avoid injury. 注意 injury 是可数名词：two injuries。和 wound（外伤、伤口）不同，injury 更广泛。

- **effective** — 有效的。搭配：an effective workout / an effective routine / highly effective。例句：Squats are one of the most effective exercises for your legs. 注意和 efficient（高效的，强调省时省力）区分：effective 强调"有效果"，efficient 强调"效率高"。

- **abs** — 腹肌。abdominal muscles 的缩写。搭配：work on your abs / six-pack abs / ab exercises。口语中直接说 abs，没人说 abdominal muscles。例句：My abs are killing me after all those squats.

- **weights** — 哑铃、杠铃、器械（举重用的重物）。搭配：lift weights / pump weights / a weights session。例句：I'd rather do cardio than lift weights. 注意 weights 通常用复数。动词短语 lift weights 是"举重、做力量训练"。

- **ground rules** — 基本规则、底线。搭配：set some ground rules / establish ground rules / the ground rules are...。例句：Let's set some ground rules: always warm up, and never skip leg day. 原指棒球比赛中场地的基本规则，引申为"任何活动的基本准则"。
