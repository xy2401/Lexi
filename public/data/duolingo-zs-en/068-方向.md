# Unit 068: 方向

> 指路

## 单词

air, at, corner, country, crowded, desert, directions, downtown, dry, east, far away, from, hill, lost, map, mile, modern, noisy, north, of, quiet, sea, sky, south, straight, take, to, top, west, wide

## 单元讲解

本单元围绕"指路"展开。核心句型包括：Go straight.（直走。）、Turn left at the corner.（在拐角左转。）、It's far away.（很远。）、How do I get to downtown?（我怎么去市中心？）。

词表里有方位词（east, west, north, south）和描述环境的形容词（crowded, dry, modern, noisy, quiet, wide）。你可以练习：The desert is dry and quiet. / Downtown is noisy and crowded. / The hill is far away. / Take this road to the sea.

lost 表示"迷路的"：I'm lost! / We got lost. directions 是"方向/路线指引"。

## Tips

- **east / west / north / south** — 东 / 西 / 北 / 南。搭配：in the east, go north, south of the city。注意方位词前通常加 the：the north, the south。也可以作副词：Go north. / Drive east.

- **straight** — 直走、直的。搭配：go straight, straight ahead, a straight line。例句：Go straight for two blocks. / The road is straight. 注意指路时最常用：Go straight ahead.

- **corner** — 拐角、角落。搭配：at the corner, on the corner, turn at the corner。例句：The shop is on the corner. / Turn left at the next corner. 注意 at the corner 和 on the corner 都可以。

- **downtown** — 市中心（美式）。搭配：go downtown, in downtown, downtown area。例句：Let's go downtown. / Downtown is very noisy. 注意 downtown 前通常不加 the。英式用 city centre。

- **lost** — 迷路的。搭配：get lost, I'm lost, a lost child。例句：We got lost in the desert. / I'm lost! Where are we? 注意 get lost 是"迷路"（动作），be lost 是"迷路了"（状态）。

- **directions** — 方向、路线指引。搭配：ask for directions, give directions, follow the directions。例句：Can you give me directions? / I need directions to the hotel. 注意通常用复数。

- **crowded / noisy / quiet** — 拥挤的 / 嘈杂的 / 安静的。搭配：a crowded street, a noisy city, a quiet village。例句：Downtown is crowded and noisy. / The desert is quiet. 注意反义词对。

- **desert / sea / hill / sky** — 沙漠 / 海 / 山丘 / 天空。搭配：in the desert, by the sea, on top of the hill, in the sky。注意 desert（沙漠）和 dessert（甜点）拼写不同。

- **far away** — 很远。搭配：far away from, very far away。例句：The sea is far away. / How far is it? 注意 far away 通常不用于肯定句中修饰具体距离（✗ It is far away from here five miles → ✓ It is five miles away）。

- **mile** — 英里。搭配：two miles, a mile away, miles from。例句：The hotel is two miles from here. 注意 1 mile ≈ 1.6 km。mile 的复数是 miles。

- **wide** — 宽的。搭配：a wide road, a wide river, wide open。例句：This street is very wide. 反义词 narrow（窄的）。注意 wide 和 broad 同义，但 wide 更常用。

- **map** — 地图。搭配：a map of the city, look at the map, on the map。例句：Do you have a map? / It's not on the map. 注意 map 前通常加 a 或 the。
