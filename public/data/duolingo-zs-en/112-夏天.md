## 单元讲解

本单元围绕"讨论暑假计划"展开。核心是暑假活动表达：I plan on going hiking this summer vacation.（我打算这个暑假去徒步。）、We may build a sandcastle at the beach.（我们也许会在海滩堆沙堡。）、I enjoy photography—I love taking pictures of the sunset.（我喜欢摄影——我爱拍日落。）、You should wear sunscreen.（你应该涂防晒霜。）。

词表里有户外活动（hike, running, cricket, rugby）和海滩词汇（sandcastle, surfboard, wave, sunscreen）。你可以练习：I'm certain it'll be fun. / In my spare time, I practice the keyboard. / The fare for the electric scooter is cheap. / Pitch a tent and enjoy nature.

plan on + doing 表示"打算做"。may / may not 表示"也许/也许不"。

## Tips

- **plan on / summer vacation** — 打算 / 暑假。搭配：plan on doing, plan on going, summer vacation。例句：I plan on hiking this summer. / What are your plans for summer vacation? 注意 plan on + 动名词（不是 ✗ to do）。

- **may / may not** — 也许 / 也许不。搭配：may go, may not come, it may rain。例句：We may go surfing. / It may not be sunny. 注意 may 比 might 稍确定一些。

- **hike / running / cricket** — 徒步 / 跑步 / 板球。搭配：go hiking, go running, play cricket。例句：Let's go hiking in nature. / I go running every morning. / They play cricket in summer. 注意 go + 动名词（运动/户外活动）。

- **sandcastle / surfboard / wave** — 沙堡 / 冲浪板 / 浪。搭配：build a sandcastle, ride a surfboard, a big wave。例句：The kids built a sandcastle. / He rode the wave on his surfboard. 注意 sandcastle = sand + castle。

- **sunscreen / sunrise / sunset** — 防晒霜 / 日出 / 日落。搭配：wear sunscreen, watch the sunrise, at sunset。例句：Put on sunscreen before you go out. / We watched the sunset. 注意 sunrise/sunset 前加 the。

- **enjoy / spare time** — 享受 / 空闲时间。搭配：enjoy doing, in my spare time。例句：I enjoy photography. / In my spare time, I practice music. 注意 enjoy + 动名词（不是 ✗ to do）。

- **photography / musical instrument** — 摄影 / 乐器。搭配：do photography, a photography class, play a musical instrument。例句：Photography is my favorite hobby. / I play a musical instrument—the keyboard. 注意 photography 重音在第二音节。

- **tent / nature / certain** — 帐篷 / 自然 / 确定的。搭配：pitch a tent, in nature, I'm certain。例句：Let's pitch a tent here. / I love being in nature. / I'm certain you'll enjoy it. 注意 pitch a tent = 搭帐篷。

- **fare / electric scooter / motorcycle** — 费用 / 电动滑板车 / 摩托车。搭配：the bus fare, ride an electric scooter, a motorcycle。例句：The fare is only two dollars. / He rides an electric scooter. 注意 fare 是交通费用（不是 fee）。

- **teach / practice / favorite** — 教 / 练习 / 最喜欢的。搭配：teach someone, practice the piano, my favorite season。例句：Can you teach me to surf? / I practice every day. / Summer is my favorite season.

- **rugby / action / deck of cards** — 橄榄球 / 动作 / 一副牌。搭配：play rugby, an action movie, a deck of cards。例句：They play rugby at school. / Let's play cards—I have a deck of cards. 注意 rugby 不可数。

- **insect / future / season** — 昆虫 / 未来 / 季节。搭配：an insect bite, in the future, the summer season。例句：Watch out for insects! / In the future, I'll travel more. / Summer is the best season.
