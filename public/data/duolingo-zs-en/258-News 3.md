## 单元讲解

本单元的主题是"分享有趣的故事"，场景是播报新闻。词表可以分成四组：新闻结构与播报用语（update, coming up, next up, on the scene, will be, be next, know about）、事件与人物（incident, lifeguard, surfer, deer, medal, marathon, grand opening, promotion）、品质与褒奖（bravery, fascinating, look up to, be a part of, set a record）、以及动作与方位（come across, go away, pick, never, neighboring）。拼起来就是一条新闻：A lifeguard in our neighboring town set a record in the marathon. Next up, coming up: the grand opening of the new park.

语法主线是一般将来时的新闻播报用法：will be 描述即将发生的事（The winner will be announced tonight.），coming up / next up 预告下一条新闻。另一条主线是过去事件的叙述：come across（偶然发现）与 set a record（创纪录）常用于故事类报道。

学完这个单元，你能用英语播报新闻、预告节目内容，还能讲述感人的故事。

## Tips

- **will be / be next / coming up / next up** — 新闻预告用语。will be 是将来时骨架：The ceremony will be held Friday. be next = 下一个（是……）：You'll be next. coming up = 即将播出：Coming up: more weather. next up = 接下来：Next up, sports.

- **on the scene / update / know about** — on the scene = 在现场：Police were on the scene quickly. update = 最新消息、更新；know about = 了解、知道：Do you know about the incident?

- **incident / grand opening / promotion / marathon** — incident = 事件（常指事故）；grand opening = 盛大开业：the store's grand opening；promotion = 晋升或促销；marathon = 马拉松。

- **lifeguard / surfer / deer / medal** — lifeguard = 救生员；surfer = 冲浪者；deer = 鹿（单复数同形：two deer）；medal = 奖牌：win a gold medal。

- **bravery / look up to / set a record** — bravery = 勇敢：He received a medal for bravery. look up to = 敬佩、仰望：Kids look up to heroes. set a record = 创纪录：She set a new record.

- **come across / pick / go away / never** — come across = 偶然发现：I came across an old photo. pick = 挑选、捡起；go away = 走开、消失；never 加强否定：He never gives up.

- **be a part of / fascinating / neighboring** — be a part of = 成为……的一部分：I want to be a part of it. fascinating = 迷人的、极有趣的；neighboring = 邻近的：a neighboring city。

<quiz-sentence-builder>

1. **中文**：获胜者将于周五公布。
   **英文**：The winner will be announced on Friday.
   > **解析**：will be + 过去分词构成一般将来时的被动语态；on Friday 是时间状语。

2. **中文**：下一个出场的会是你。
   **英文**：You will be next.
   > **解析**：be next 表示"下一个是……"；will 后接 be 原形。

3. **中文**：接下来，请收看天气预报。
   **英文**：Next up, the weather will be coming up.
   > **解析**：Next up 是播报过渡语；will be coming up 用将来进行时预告节目。

4. **中文**：警方很快到达了现场。
   **英文**：Police were on the scene quickly.
   > **解析**：on the scene 是固定表达"在现场"；were 与复数主语 Police 搭配。

5. **中文**：我偶然发现了一个有趣的故事。
   **英文**：I came across a fascinating story.
   > **解析**：come across 是固定短语"偶然发现"；过去式为 came across。

6. **中文**：你听说过那起事故吗？
   **英文**：Do you know about the incident?
   > **解析**：know about 是固定搭配"了解……"；Do 引导一般疑问句。

7. **中文**：救生员因为勇敢而获得了奖牌。
   **英文**：The lifeguard received a medal for bravery.
   > **解析**：for bravery 表示原因；received 是过去式。

8. **中文**：孩子们在马拉松比赛中创了纪录。
   **英文**：She set a record in the marathon.
   > **解析**：set a record 是固定搭配"创纪录"；set 过去式同形。

9. **中文**：新图书馆的盛大开业就在明天。
   **英文**：The grand opening of the new library is tomorrow.
   > **解析**：grand opening 是固定表达"盛大开业"；is tomorrow 用一般现在时表日程。

10. **中文**：她得到了一次晋升。
   **英文**：She got a promotion at work.
   > **解析**：get a promotion 是固定搭配"获得晋升"；at work 表地点。

11. **中文**：我敬佩那位冲浪者。
   **英文**：I look up to that surfer.
   > **解析**：look up to 是固定短语"敬佩"；that surfer 作宾语。

12. **中文**：邻近的小镇举办了一场活动。
   **英文**：A neighboring town held an event.
   > **解析**：neighboring 作形容词修饰 town；held 是 hold 的过去式。

13. **中文**：一只鹿走进了花园。
   **英文**：A deer walked into the garden.
   > **解析**：deer 单复数同形，a deer 指一只；walked into 表示方向。

14. **中文**：我想成为团队的一员。
   **英文**：I want to be a part of the team.
   > **解析**：be a part of 是固定表达"成为……的一部分"；want to do 结构。

15. **中文**：最新消息马上就来。
   **英文**：The update will be here soon.
   > **解析**：update 作名词"最新消息"；will be 表将来。

16. **中文**：请挑一张你最喜欢的照片。
   **英文**：Please pick your favorite photo.
   > **解析**：pick 是动词"挑选"；祈使句以动词原形开头。

17. **中文**：疼痛终于消失了。
   **英文**：The pain finally went away.
   > **解析**：go away 是固定短语"走开、消失"；went 是过去式。

18. **中文**：他从不放弃。
   **英文**：He never gives up.
   > **解析**：never 放在实义动词之前；主语是单三，gives 加 s。

19. **中文**：这是一条迷人的新闻。
   **英文**：This is a fascinating piece of news.
   > **解析**：fascinating 作形容词修饰 news；piece of news 是新闻的量词表达。

20. **中文**：仪式即将开始。
   **英文**：The ceremony is coming up.
   > **解析**：现在进行时 is coming up 表近期将发生的事；come up = 即将到来。

<quiz-cloze>

### 1

The concert ____ held outdoors this weekend.

- [x] will be
- [ ] will
- [ ] be will

> **解析**：被动结构需要 will be held；will 单独无法接过去分词，be will 语序错误。

### 2

The fire chief was the first on the ____.

- [x] scene
- [ ] scenery
- [ ] scenes

> **解析**：on the scene 是固定表达"在现场"；scenery 指风景，复数不合搭配。

### 3

She ____ an old letter in the drawer.

- [x] came across
- [ ] came across to
- [ ] across came

> **解析**：come across 过去式为 came across，表示偶然发现；其余语序或搭配错误。

### 4

A ____ stood in the middle of the road.

- [x] deer
- [ ] deers
- [ ] deer's

> **解析**：deer 单复数同形；deers 是错误复数，deer's 是所有格不合语境。

### 5

The hero received a medal for his ____.

- [x] bravery
- [ ] brave
- [ ] bravely

> **解析**：for 后需要名词 bravery；brave 是形容词，bravely 是副词。

### 6

Next ____, we visit the city market.

- [x] up
- [ ] on
- [ ] out

> **解析**：Next up 是播报过渡语"接下来"；其余介词不构成该表达。

### 7

- [x] update
- [ ] updated
- [ ] updating

### 8

- [ ] recording

### 10

- [ ] lifeguards

### 14
