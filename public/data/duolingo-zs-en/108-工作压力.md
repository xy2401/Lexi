## 单元讲解

本单元围绕"表达工作压力"展开。核心是工作感受表达：My job is really stressful—I work long hours.（我的工作压力真大——工作时间很长。）、It is not relaxing at all.（一点都不轻松。）、I'm not happy with my occupation.（我对我的职业不满意。）、The boss is very strict.（老板很严格。）。

词表里有职业词汇（lawyer, mechanic, player, occupation）和感受词汇（stressful, relaxing, painful, awful）。你可以练习：How was work? / It was awful—especially the hours. / I wasn't able to finish on time. / Anyway, it's time to relax.

stressful 描述事物（工作有压力），stressed 描述人（感到有压力）。

## Tips

- **stressful / relaxing** — 有压力的 / 放松的。搭配：a stressful job, a stressful day, a relaxing evening。例句：My job is very stressful. / Yoga is relaxing. 注意 stressful 修饰事，stressed 修饰人（I'm stressed）。

- **occupation / lawyer / mechanic** — 职业 / 律师 / 机械师。搭配：What's your occupation? / work as a lawyer。例句：What's your occupation? / She's a lawyer. / He works as a mechanic. 注意 occupation 比 job 更正式。

- **hours / on time** — 工作时间 / 准时。搭配：long hours, work long hours, on time。例句：I work long hours. / I need to be on time. 注意 long hours = 长时间工作（不一定指"小时"）。

- **happy with / awful** — 对……满意 / 糟糕的。搭配：happy with my job, an awful day。例句：I'm not happy with my job. / Today was awful. 注意 happy with = satisfied with。

- **strict / necessary** — 严格的 / 必要的。搭配：a strict boss, strict rules, if necessary。例句：My boss is very strict. / Come early if necessary. 注意 strict 搭配 with（strict with sb）。

- **able to / possibly** — 能够 / 可能。搭配：be able to do, possibly, not possibly。例句：I wasn't able to finish on time. / Could you possibly help me? 注意 able to 和 can 意思相近，但能用于更多时态。

- **especially / even** — 尤其 / 甚至。搭配：especially the hours, even worse。例句：I hate Mondays, especially the meetings. / It's even more stressful now. 注意 especially 用于强调某一点。

- **how was / it is not** — 怎么样 / 不是。搭配：How was work? / It is not easy。例句：How was your day? / It is not relaxing at all. 注意 How was...? 用于询问过去的体验。

- **catch / ball / match** — 接住 / 球 / 比赛。搭配：catch the ball, a football match, watch a match。例句：Catch the ball! / Did you watch the match? 注意 catch 在这里是"接住"。

- **poor / painful / unusual** — 可怜的 / 痛苦的 / 不寻常的。搭配：poor thing, a painful experience, unusual behavior。例句：The poor player was injured. / Sitting all day is painful. / That's unusual.

- **anyway / time to / without** — 不管怎样 / 该……了 / 没有。搭配：Anyway, ..., It's time to relax, without a break。例句：Anyway, let's move on. / It's time to go home. / I can't work without a break.

- **really / so / small** — 真的 / 如此 / 小的。搭配：really stressful, so tired, a small problem。例句：It's really stressful. / I'm so tired. / It's just a small problem.

<quiz-sentence-builder>

1. **中文**：作为一名律师，他的工作非常繁重且充满压力。  
   **英文**：As a lawyer, his occupation is very busy and stressful.
   > **解析**：lawyer 表示“律师”；occupation 表示“职业”；stressful 表示“充满压力的”。

2. **中文**：这位汽修工设法按时修好了损坏的汽车。  
   **英文**：The mechanic was able to fix the broken car on time.
   > **解析**：mechanic 表示“汽修工”；be able to 表示“能够”；on time 表示“按时”。

3. **中文**：没有充足的休息，感到疲惫是不可避免的。  
   **英文**：Without enough rest, it is impossible to work well.
   > **解析**：without 表示“没有”；it is impossible 表示“是不可能的”。

4. **中文**：他在工厂里长时间工作，感到非常糟糕和头痛。  
   **英文**：He worked long hours and felt awful with a headache.
   > **解析**：long hours 表示“长时间”；awful 表示“糟糕的/极坏的”。

5. **中文**：老板对团队的工作表现感到相当满意。  
   **英文**：The boss is really happy with the team's performance.
   > **解析**：happy with... 表示“对……感到满意”；really 表示“确实/很”。

6. **中文**：在这家严格的公司里，按时交报告是必要的。  
   **英文**：It is necessary to submit reports on time in this strict company.
   > **解析**：necessary 表示“必要的”；strict 表示“严格的”；on time 表示“按时”。

7. **中文**：你的高压工作怎么样？感觉真的很痛苦吗？  
   **英文**：How was your stressful job? Is it really painful?
   > **解析**：how was... 询问“……怎么样？”；painful 表示“令人痛苦的/艰辛的”。

8. **中文**：下班后踢足球是一件非常令人放松的事情。  
   **英文**：Playing soccer after work is a very relaxing activity.
   > **解析**：relaxing 表示“令人放松的”。

9. **中文**：即便在周末，他也可能需要处理紧急工作。  
   **英文**：Even on weekends, he might possibly need to handle work.
   > **解析**：even 表示“甚至/即使”；possibly 表示“可能地”。

10. **中文**：这位优秀的球员在比赛中抓住了球。  
   **英文**：The player was able to catch the ball during the match.
   > **解析**：player 表示“球员/选手”；catch the ball 表示“接球”。

11. **中文**：对于一位汽修工来说，拥有良好的技术是必要的。  
   **英文**：For a mechanic, having good skills is necessary.
   > **解析**：mechanic 表示“汽修工”；necessary 表示“必要的”。

12. **中文**：这是一场非同寻常的比赛，充满了悬念。  
   **英文**：This was an unusual match full of excitement.
   > **解析**：unusual 表示“不寻常的/特别的”；match 表示“比赛”。

13. **中文**：不管怎样，我们今晚应该好好享受时光。  
   **英文**：Anyway, we should have a good time tonight.
   > **解析**：anyway 表示“不管怎样/总之”；have a good time 表示“玩得开心/过得愉快”。

14. **中文**：他的工作非常令人痛苦，但他依然坚持按时完成。  
   **英文**：His work was painful, but he finished it on time.
   > **解析**：painful 表示“痛苦的/艰难的”；on time 表示“按时”。

15. **中文**：你工作得怎么样？是不是特别累？  
   **英文**：How was your work day? Was it especially hard?
   > **解析**：especially 表示“特别/尤其”；how was... 表示“……怎么样”。

16. **中文**：没有老板的许可，任何人都不能提前下班离开。  
   **英文**：Without the boss's permission, no one can leave early.
   > **解析**：without 表示“没有/未经”。

17. **中文**：那家公司要求员工遵守严格的规章制度。  
   **英文**：That strict company sets high standards for every occupation.
   > **解析**：strict 表示“严格的”；occupation 表示“职业/工作”。

18. **中文**：是到了放松一下、摆脱压力的时候了！  
   **英文**：It is time to relax and get rid of work stress!
   > **解析**：it is time to do sth 表示“是时候做某事了”。

19. **中文**：尽管可怜的工人们很累，但他们依然尽力工作。  
   **英文**：Even though the poor workers were tired, they tried hard.
   > **解析**：poor 表示“可怜的/贫穷的”；even though 表示“即使/尽管”。

20. **中文**：现在是时候走出办公室，呼吸新鲜空气了。  
   **英文**：Now it is time to go out for some fresh air.
   > **解析**：go out 表示“出去/走出”。
