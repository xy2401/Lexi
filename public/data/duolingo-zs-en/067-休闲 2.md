## 单元讲解

本单元围绕"谈论自己的生活"展开。核心是 be good at / be bad at（擅长/不擅长）和 be afraid of（害怕）：I'm good at cooking. / She's bad at drawing. / He's afraid of hiking. / I hate camping.

词表里有大量户外活动（camping, fishing, hiking, rock climbing, barbecue）和 -ing 形式（baking, cooking, drawing）。你可以练习：I went hiking last weekend. / She won the race. / We had a barbecue. / Congratulations!

good for / bad for 表示"对……有益/有害"：Exercise is good for you. / Smoking is bad for your health.

## Tips

- **good at / bad at** — 擅长 / 不擅长。搭配：good at cooking, bad at drawing, good at sports。例句：I'm good at baking. / She's bad at fishing. 注意 at 后接名词或动名词：good at swimming。

- **afraid of** — 害怕。搭配：afraid of spiders, afraid of heights, afraid of rock climbing。例句：I'm afraid of hiking alone. 注意 afraid of + 名词/动名词：afraid of swimming。

- **good for / bad for** — 对……有益 / 有害。搭配：good for your health, bad for you, good for the environment。例句：Hiking is good for you. / Too much sugar is bad for your teeth.

- **went / win / won** — 去了 / 赢。go → went, win → won → won。搭配：went hiking, went camping, won the race, won the competition。例句：She won first place! / We went fishing.

- **have fun** — 玩得开心。搭配：have fun doing, have fun at, have fun with。例句：We had fun hiking. / Did you have fun? 注意 have fun 后接动名词：have fun camping（不说 ✗ have fun to camp）。

- **camping / hiking / fishing** — 露营 / 徒步 / 钓鱼。搭配：go camping, go hiking, go fishing。注意 go + 动名词 表示去做某项活动：go swimming, go skiing, go shopping。

- **rock climbing** — 攀岩。搭配：go rock climbing, indoor rock climbing。构词：rock（岩石）+ climbing（攀爬）。注意 rock climbing 是极限运动的一种。

- **barbecue** — 烧烤。搭配：have a barbecue, a barbecue party, barbecue sauce。缩写 BBQ / barbie（澳式口语）。注意 barbecue 既是名词（烧烤活动）也是动词（烧烤）。

- **congratulations** — 恭喜！搭配：Congratulations! / Congratulations on your win! 注意总是用复数形式。缩写 congrats（口语）。后接 on：Congratulations on winning the race!

- **hate** — 讨厌。搭配：hate doing, hate to do, I hate camping。例句：I hate fishing. / She hates cooking. 注意 hate 比 dislike 程度更强。hate doing 和 hate to do 意思基本相同。

- **spent / spend** — 花了（时间/钱）。spend → spent → spent。搭配：spent time, spent money, spend the weekend。例句：I spent the whole day hiking. 注意 spend 的主语是人。

- **wonderful** — 精彩的、美妙的。搭配：a wonderful time, wonderful weather, wonderful! 例句：We had a wonderful weekend. 注意 wonderful 比 good / nice 程度更强，接近 excellent。

<quiz-sentence-builder>

1. **中文**：我的小弟弟过去很害怕在深水区游泳。  
   **英文**：My little brother used to be afraid of swimming in deep water.
   > **解析**：**afraid of** 是固定形容词短语，意为“害怕……的”，后接名词或动名词（swimming）作宾语，描述心理畏惧状态。

2. **中文**：她喜欢在周日早晨烘焙新鲜的草莓派。  
   **英文**：She enjoys baking fresh strawberry pies on Sunday mornings.
   > **解析**：**baking** 是动词 bake 的动名词形式，意为“烘焙、烤制”。在句中作动词 enjoys 的宾语，指某种具体的日常休闲爱好。

3. **中文**：我们计划今年夏天去国家公园露营。  
   **英文**：We are planning a camping trip in the national park this summer.
   > **解析**：**camping** 作名词意为“野营、露营活动”。camping trip 为固定名词短语，意为“露营之旅”，作动词 planning 的宾语。

4. **中文**：他花了几个小时素描绘制郊外风景。  
   **英文**：He spent hours drawing detailed sketches of the countryside.
   > **解析**：**drawing** 是动词 draw 的动名词形式，在 spend time doing sth.（花费时间做某事）结构中作宾语补足语，表示画画娱乐。

5. **中文**：晨跑对你的心肺健康非常有好处。  
   **英文**：Morning jogging is exceptionally good for your health.
   > **解析**：**good for** 是固定形容词短语，意为“对……有益/有好处”，与 be 动词连用（be good for），后接名词或代词作宾语。

6. **中文**：为了在马拉松比赛中取胜，他每天都刻苦训练。  
   **英文**：He trained hard every day to win the marathon race.
   > **解析**：**race** 作可数名词意为“赛跑、速度比赛”，如 marathon race（马拉松赛）。作动词 win 的宾语，强调速度上的较量。

7. **中文**：上个周末，我们全家去海边小镇度假了。  
   **英文**：Last weekend, my family went to the coastal town.
   > **解析**：**went** 是不规则动词 go 的过去式，意为“去了”。与介词 to 连用（went to...），描述过去发生的出行活动。

8. **中文**：我很不擅长在聚会上记住别人的名字。  
   **英文**：I am really bad at remembering people's names at parties about bake.
   > **解析**：**bad at** 是固定形容词短语，意为“不擅长……的”，与 be 动词连用（be bad at），后接名词或动名词（remembering）作宾语。

9. **中文**：我们邀请了所有邻居来参加周日下午的烧烤聚会。  
   **英文**：We invited all our neighbors to a Sunday afternoon barbecue.
   > **解析**：**barbecue** 作名词意为“户外烧烤聚会”。在句中作介词 to 的宾语，描述与社交娱乐相关的休闲活动。

10. **中文**：她在全国钢琴比赛中获得了第一名。  
   **英文**：She won first prize in the national piano competition.
   > **解析**：**competition** 作名词意为“比赛、竞赛”（侧重技巧与综合角逐）。前有形容词 national 和 piano 饰限定，作介词 in 的宾语。
