## 单元讲解

本单元的主题是"探索一个城市"。词表可以分成四组：城市地标（city center, city hall, post office, gallery, landmark, temple, mosque, sculpture, railroad）、建造与历史（build, built, two thousand, model, ever）、方位与状态（across, between, into, for, busy, new, in town）以及文化与记录（documentary, film, writing, continue, which, ping, town, dc）。拼起来就是一次城市探索：The temple was built two thousand years ago. The gallery is across from the city hall. Let's continue into the city center.

语法主线有两个。一是 build 与 built：build 建造，不规则变化 build → built → built。描述历史建筑常用被动语态：The temple was built two thousand years ago. 二是方位介词的进阶用法：across（穿过、对面）、between（在两者之间）、into（进入，强调方向），对比 in（静态在内部）。另外 which 引导定语从句，修饰地点或事物：the gallery, which is new.

学完这些词，你能用英语探索并介绍一座城市——指路、描述地标、讲述建筑的历史。

## Tips

- **city center / city hall / post office** — 城市设施。city center 市中心；city hall 市政厅；post office 邮局。构词都是"功能 + 场所"复合词。

- **gallery / temple / mosque / sculpture** — 文化场所与艺术。gallery 美术馆、画廊；temple 寺庙、神庙；mosque 清真寺；sculpture 雕塑。注意场所与宗教的对应关系。

- **landmark** — 地标：a famous landmark. land + mark，字面"土地的标记"。

- **build / built** — build 建造，不规则动词 build → built → built。被动语态：was built in 1900. ✗ builded → ✓ built.

- **two thousand** — 两千：two thousand years ago. 注意 thousand 前加具体数字时不加 s：two thousand（✗ two thousands），但 thousands of（成千上万）要加 s。

- **ever** — 曾经，用于疑问句和完成时：Have you ever been to DC? 也可加强语气：the best ever.

- **dc / in town / town** — DC 指华盛顿特区（Washington, D.C.）；in town 在城里：Are you in town this week? town 城镇，go to town.

- **across / between / into** — 方位进阶。across 穿过、在对面：across the street；between 在两者之间；into 进入，强调方向：walk into the gallery. 对比 in 表静态位置。

- **busy / new / continue** — busy 繁忙的：a busy street；new 新的；continue 继续：continue into the city center. continue + to do / doing 均可。

- **documentary / film / writing** — documentary 纪录片；film 电影、拍摄；writing 写作、作品。-ary、-ing 后缀构词。

- **model / railroad / pray / for / which / ping** — model 模型、模特；railroad 铁路；pray 祈祷：People pray at the temple. for 表示目的或时长：for two thousand years；which 引导定语从句；ping 拟声词"叮"，也作动词发消息提示音。

<quiz-sentence-builder>

1. **中文**：这座寺庙建于两千年前。
   **英文**：The temple was built two thousand years ago.
   > **解析**：was built 是被动语态；two thousand years ago 是时间状语。

2. **中文**：美术馆在市政厅对面。
   **英文**：The gallery is across from the city hall.
   > **解析**：across from 表示"在……对面"；is 搭配单数主语。

3. **中文**：这条街很繁忙。
   **英文**：This street is very busy.
   > **解析**：busy 作表语；very 修饰形容词。

4. **中文**：你曾经去过 DC 吗？
   **英文**：Have you ever been to DC?
   > **解析**：Have you ever been 是现在完成时疑问结构，ever 表"曾经"。

5. **中文**：让我们继续走进市中心。
   **英文**：Let's continue into the city center.
   > **解析**：Let's 后接动词原形；into 表方向"进入"。

6. **中文**：邮局在银行和寺庙之间。
   **英文**：The post office is between the bank and the temple.
   > **解析**：between A and B 表示"在两者之间"。

7. **中文**：那是一座著名的地标。
   **英文**：That is a famous landmark.
   > **解析**：a famous landmark 作表语；famous 作定语。

8. **中文**：这座雕塑很美。
   **英文**：The sculpture is beautiful.
   > **解析**：The sculpture 是单数主语，用 is；beautiful 作表语。

9. **中文**：人们在这座清真寺祈祷。
   **英文**：People pray at the mosque.
   > **解析**：pray 用原形搭配复数主语 People；at the mosque 是地点状语。

10. **中文**：我看过一部关于铁路的纪录片。
    **英文**：I watched a documentary about the railroad.
    > **解析**：watched 是过去式；about the railroad 是后置定语。

11. **中文**：这部电影是在城里拍的。
    **英文**：The film was made in town.
    > **解析**：was made 是被动语态；in town 是地点状语。

12. **中文**：他们正在建造一座新模型。
    **英文**：They are building a new model.
    > **解析**：are building 是现在进行时；a new model 作宾语。

13. **中文**：她为这本书写作了两年。
    **英文**：She wrote the book for two years.
    > **解析**：wrote 是 write 的过去式；for two years 表持续时长。

14. **中文**：这是我一直想找的画廊。
    **英文**：This is the gallery which I wanted to find.
    > **解析**：which 引导定语从句修饰 the gallery。

15. **中文**：穿过那条街就是市政厅。
    **英文**：The city hall is across the street.
    > **解析**：across the street 表位置"在街对面"。

16. **中文**：镇上有一千座建筑。
    **英文**：There are one thousand buildings in the town.
    > **解析**：There are 搭配复数 buildings；one thousand 数字不加 s。

17. **中文**：你的手机刚刚叮了一声。
    **英文**：Your phone just pinged.
    > **解析**：pinged 是 ping 的过去式，表发出提示音。

18. **中文**：这座寺庙为游客而建。
    **英文**：The temple was built for visitors.
    > **解析**：was built 是被动语态；for visitors 表目的或对象。

19. **中文**：这条铁路继续延伸进山里。
    **英文**：The railroad continues into the mountains.
    > **解析**：continues 是三单形式；into the mountains 表方向。

20. **中文**：这是城里最新的建筑。
    **英文**：This is the newest building in town.
    > **解析**：newest 是 new 的最高级，前加 the；in town 是范围状语。

<quiz-cloze>

### 1

The temple was ____ two thousand years ago.

- [x] built
- [ ] busy
- [ ] new

> **解析**：被动语态 was built 表"被建造"；busy 和 new 是形容词，不构成被动。

### 2

The gallery is across ____ the city hall.

- [x] from
- [ ] into
- [ ] for

> **解析**：across from 表示"在对面"；into 和 for 不能构成此短语。

### 3

This street is very ____.

- [x] busy
- [ ] sculpture
- [ ] railroad

> **解析**：描述街道繁忙用 busy；sculpture 和 railroad 是名词，不能作表语形容词。

### 4

Have you ____ been to DC?

- [x] ever
- [ ] never
- [ ] ping

> **解析**：疑问句表"曾经"用 ever；never 与疑问句冲突，ping 语义不符。

### 5

Let's continue ____ the city center.

- [x] into
- [ ] between
- [ ] across

> **解析**：走进市中心表方向用 into；between 需要两个对象，across 表穿过而非进入。

### 6

The post office is ____ the bank and the temple.

- [x] between
- [ ] ever
- [ ] which

> **解析**：between A and B 是固定结构；ever 和 which 不能连接两个名词。

### 7
