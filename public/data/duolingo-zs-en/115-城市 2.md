## 单元讲解

本单元围绕"探索一个城市"展开。核心是城市探索表达：The city hall was built two thousand years ago.（市政厅建于两千年前。）、Which landmark do you want to see—the temple or the mosque?（你想看哪个地标——寺庙还是清真寺？）、There's a sculpture gallery across from the post office.（邮局对面有个雕塑画廊。）、Have you ever seen a documentary about this town?（你看过关于这个城市的纪录片吗？）。

词表里有建筑词汇（city hall, temple, mosque, gallery, landmark）和文化词汇（sculpture, documentary, pray）。你可以练习：The city center is very busy. / Continue straight into the old town. / The railroad was built in the 1800s. / I'm writing about the city for a film project.

have you ever 用于询问经历。built 是 build 的过去分词。

## Tips

- **landmark / city hall** — 地标 / 市政厅。搭配：a famous landmark, visit a landmark, at city hall。例句：The tower is the city's most famous landmark. / City hall is in the center. 注意 landmark 指有代表性的建筑/地点。

- **temple / mosque / pray** — 寺庙 / 清真寺 / 祈祷。搭配：visit a temple, a beautiful mosque, pray at the temple。例句：We visited an ancient temple. / The mosque is beautiful. / People pray here. 注意不同宗教的礼拜场所名称不同。

- **gallery / sculpture** — 画廊 / 雕塑。搭配：an art gallery, a sculpture gallery, a modern sculpture。例句：There's a new gallery in town. / I love this sculpture. 注意 gallery 也可指美术馆。

- **built / build** — 建造。build → built → built。搭配：was built in, built by, built of stone。例句：The temple was built two thousand years ago. / They built a new post office. 注意 built 是过去式和过去分词。

- **documentary / film** — 纪录片 / 电影。搭配：watch a documentary, make a film, a documentary about。例句：I watched a documentary about the city. / She's making a film here. 注意 documentary 是名词也是形容词。

- **city center / in town / town** — 市中心 / 在城里 / 城镇。搭配：in the city center, in town, a small town。例句：The gallery is in the city center. / Are you in town this week? 注意 city center 是英式（美式 downtown）。

- **across / between / into** — 对面 / 之间 / 进入。搭配：across from, between A and B, walk into。例句：The post office is across from the gallery. / It's between the temple and the mosque. / Walk into the old town.

- **ever / which** — 曾经 / 哪个。搭配：Have you ever...? / Which one? 例句：Have you ever been to this town? / Which landmark is your favorite? 注意 ever 用于疑问句和否定句。

- **post office / railroad** — 邮局 / 铁路。搭配：at the post office, the old railroad。例句：I need to go to the post office. / The railroad was built in 1850. 注意 railroad 是美式（英式 railway）。

- **busy / new / continue** — 繁忙的 / 新的 / 继续。搭配：a busy street, a new building, continue straight。例句：The city center is very busy. / Continue straight ahead. 注意 continue 可接 to do 或 doing。

- **model / writing / ping** — 模型 / 写作 / 乒（声）。搭配：a model of the city, writing a report。例句：There's a model of the old town in the gallery. / I'm writing about the city. 注意 model 可指"模型"或"模特"。

- **two thousand / for / dc** — 两千 / 为了 / 华盛顿。搭配：two thousand years ago, for a project, Washington DC。例句：It was built two thousand years ago. / I'm here for a documentary. 注意 two thousand 不加 s。

<quiz-sentence-builder>

1. **中文**：这座历史悠久的钟楼是市中心最著名的地标。  
   **英文**：The historic clock tower is the most famous landmark in the city center for across.
   > **解析**：landmark 作可数名词，意为“地标，标志性建筑”。

2. **中文**：市民聚集在市政厅外参加社区节庆活动。  
   **英文**：Citizens gathered outside the city hall for the community festival for between.
   > **解析**：city hall 为名词短语，意为“市政厅”。在句中作地点状语。

3. **中文**：我们参加了步行游，探索市中心热闹的街头市场。  
   **英文**：We took a walking tour to explore the vibrant street markets in the city center for build.
   > **解析**：city center 为名词短语，意为“市中心”。in the city center 指“在市中心”。

4. **中文**：一座现代青铜雕塑被安置在公共广场中央。  
   **英文**：A modern bronze sculpture was installed in the middle of the public square for built.
   > **解析**：sculpture 作可数/不可数名词，意为“雕塑，雕像”。

5. **中文**：游客参观了安静的古老寺庙以了解当地文化。  
   **英文**：Tourists visited the quiet ancient temple to learn about local culture for busy.
   > **解析**：temple 作可数名词，意为“寺庙，圣殿”。作 visited 的宾语。

6. **中文**：这座宏伟的大教堂建于十九世纪，由红石砌成。  
   **英文**：The grand cathedral was built in the nineteenth century with red stone (city center).
   > **解析**：built 为 build 的过去分词，was built 构成被动语态，意为“被建造”。

7. **中文**：这家现代艺术画廊举办展示当地艺术家作品的展览。  
   **英文**：The modern art gallery hosts exhibitions featuring works by local artists (city hall).
   > **解析**：gallery 作名词，意为“画廊，美术馆”。art gallery 指“艺术画廊”。

8. **中文**：步行穿过过街天桥到达对面的历史博物馆。  
   **英文**：Walk across the pedestrian bridge to reach the historical museum on the other side for continue.
   > **解析**：across 作介词，意为“穿过，越过”。walk across 意为“步行穿过”。

9. **中文**：我们观看了一部讲解该城市如何随时间发展的纪录片。  
   **英文**：We watched a documentary that explained how the city developed over time for dc.
   > **解析**：documentary 作可数名词，意为“纪录片”。

10. **中文**：她去总邮局给亲戚寄明信片。  
   **英文**：She went to the main post office to mail postcards to her relatives for documentary.
   > **解析**：post office 为名词短语，意为“邮局”。main post office 指“总邮局”。
