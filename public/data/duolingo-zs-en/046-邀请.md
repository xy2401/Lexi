# Unit 046: 邀请

> 对他人发出邀请

## 单元讲解

本单元围绕"对他人发出邀请"展开。核心句型是 Will you come with us? / Will you go out with me? / I'll invite them to the party. 加上 invite, ask, come with, go out 这些邀请相关动词，可以练习完整的邀请对话：Will you come to the dance party? I'd love to! / I'm sorry, I can't.

we'll, he'll, you'll 继续巩固 will 的缩写。the day after tomorrow（后天）提供了具体时间。theater, library, dance party 是邀请的目的地/活动。

willing 表示"愿意的"：Are you willing to come? as well = also（也）。boyfriend / girlfriend 和 marry 涉及恋爱话题。

## Tips

- **invite** — 邀请。搭配：invite someone to something：I'll invite them to the party. / She invited me to the dance party. 名词 invitation。注意 invite 的发音：/ɪnˈvaɪt/（动词），重音在第二音节。

- **come with / go out** — 一起来 / 出去（玩/约会）。搭配：Come with us! / go out with friends / go out for dinner。注意 go out 可以指"出去玩"也可以指"约会"：They're going out.（他们在交往。）

- **as well** — 也。放在句末：I'll come as well. / She's going as well. 比 also 更口语，位置更灵活。同义词：too, also。注意 as well 和 as well as 的区别：as well = too，as well as = 和、以及。

- **willing** — 愿意的。搭配：be willing to do：Are you willing to help? / I'm willing to try. 注意 willing 和 want 的区别：willing 强调"不反对、乐意配合"，want 强调"主动想要"。

- **the day after tomorrow** — 后天。搭配：I'll see you the day after tomorrow. 注意英语中"后天"没有单个词，必须用这个短语。对应的"前天"是 the day before yesterday。

- **theater** — 剧院（美式）。英式拼 theatre。搭配：go to the theater, movie theater（电影院）。注意 theater 在美式中也可以指电影院。

- **library** — 图书馆。搭配：go to the library, in the library, library book。注意发音：/ˈlaɪbreri/，不是"里布拉里"。来自拉丁语 liber（书）。

- **them / us** — 他们（宾格）/ 我们（宾格）。搭配：invite them, come with us, give them。注意主格和宾格：they → them, we → us。宾格放在动词或介词后。

- **you'll / we'll / he'll** — will 的缩写。you'll = you will, we'll = we will, he'll = he will。搭配：You'll love the party! / We'll go together. / He'll marry her.

- **marry** — 和……结婚。搭配：marry someone（直接接宾语，不加 with）：He married her. / Will you marry me? 注意不说 ✗ marry with someone。get married to 也可以。

- **give** — 给。三态：give - gave - given。搭配：give me a call, give them a gift, give it to me。注意 give 的双宾语结构：give someone something = give something to someone。

- **ask** — 问、请求。搭配：ask a question, ask someone to do：I asked her to come. / ask for help。注意 ask 和 question 的区别：ask 是动词，question 是名词。
