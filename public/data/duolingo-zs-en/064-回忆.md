## 单元讲解

本单元的主题是"回忆"，学习谈论过去的生活与经历。词表可以分成四组：过去式动词（came, cried, kept, knew, taught, wore, got back, got married）、时间与频率表达（ago, always, day, how often did, so much）、人物与生活（neighbor, life, living, alone, miss, belong to）、以及物品与回忆场景（apartment building, lake, necklace, ring, suit, special, perfect）。组合起来就是一段回忆：Ten years ago, my neighbor taught me to swim in the lake. I kept a special ring she gave me — it belonged to her and now it belongs to me. I miss those days so much.

语法主线有两个。第一是不规则动词过去式：come → came, know → knew, keep → kept, teach → taught, wear → wore，以及 cry → cried（辅音字母 + y 变 ied）。第二是过去时的特殊疑问句：Where did you live? Who did you play with? How often did you visit? 助动词 did 后动词恢复原形。was 是 am/is 的过去式。

学完这个单元，你能用英语回忆童年与往事、描述过去住过的地方和认识的人，还能用 did 引导的各种疑问句询问他人的过去。

## Tips

- **came / knew / kept / taught / wore** — 不规则动词过去式。come → came（来）；know → knew（认识、知道）；keep → kept（保存、保持）；teach → taught（教）；wear → wore（穿、戴）。这些没有 ed 规律，只能整词记忆。
- **cried / cry / crying** — cry 的三种形式。过去式 cried：y 前是辅音 r，y 变 i 加 ed；原形 cry；现在分词 crying：I was crying.（我当时在哭）。
- **got back / get back / got married** — get back 回来，过去式 got back：She got back home late. get married 结婚，过去式 got married：They got married in 2010. 注意 married 是形容词化的过去分词。
- **ago / always / day** — 时间词。ago 放时间段之后表"以前"：ten years ago；always 总是（频率副词，放 be 后实义动词前）；day 天：one day（有一天）。
- **how often did / where did / who did / who** — 过去时疑问结构。how often did 问过去频率：How often did you swim?；where did 问地点；who did 问对象：Who did you meet?；who 单独可作主语：Who taught you?
- **was** — am/is 的过去式：I was young. She was my neighbor. 复数用 were。
- **neighbor / belong to / miss** — neighbor 邻居；belong to 属于：The ring belongs to her.（注意第三人称单数 belongs to）；miss 想念、错过：I miss you so much.
- **life / living / alone** — life 生活、生命（复数 lives）；living 生计、生活：make a living（谋生）；alone 独自：live alone（独居）。注意 alone 表客观独自一人，lonely 表主观孤独。
- **apartment building / lake** — 地点。apartment building 公寓楼（美式；英式 block of flats）；lake 湖：by the lake（在湖边）。
- **necklace / ring / suit / special / perfect / so much** — necklace 项链；ring 戒指；suit 西装、套装；special 特别的；perfect 完美的；so much 如此多、非常：I miss her so much.

<quiz-sentence-builder>

1. **中文**：我小时候住在一栋公寓楼里。
   **英文**：I lived in an apartment building when I was a child.
   > **解析**：lived 是 live 的过去式；when I was a child 是过去时间从句。

2. **中文**：二十年前这里有一个湖。
   **英文**：Twenty years ago, there was a lake here.
   > **解析**：ago 放时间段后表"以前"；there was 是 there is 的过去式。

3. **中文**：我的邻居教我游泳。
   **英文**：My neighbor taught me to swim.
   > **解析**：taught 是 teach 的过去式；teach sb to do 表示"教某人做某事"。

4. **中文**：我们总是独自在湖边玩耍。
   **英文**：We always played by the lake alone.
   > **解析**：always 是频率副词放动词前；alone 作方式状语放句末。

5. **中文**：她去年结婚了。
   **英文**：She got married last year.
   > **解析**：got married 表示"结婚"，用过去式叙述往事；last year 是过去时间状语。

6. **中文**：我回到家时天已经黑了。
   **英文**：When I got back home, it was dark.
   > **解析**：got back 表示"回来"；was 是 is 的过去式。

7. **中文**：这枚戒指属于我奶奶。
   **英文**：This ring belongs to my grandmother.
   > **解析**：belong to 表示"属于"；主语单数用 belongs。

8. **中文**：这条项链很特别。
   **英文**：This necklace is very special.
   > **解析**：special 作表语；very 修饰形容词。

9. **中文**：我过去常穿那套西装。
   **英文**：I always wore that suit on special days.
   > **解析**：wore 是 wear 的过去式；on special days 表示"在特别的日子"。

10. **中文**：他当时还是个孩子时哭了很多。
   **英文**：He cried a lot when he was a little child.
   > **解析**：cried 是 cry 的过去式，y 变 i 加 ed；a lot 修饰哭的程度。

11. **中文**：你为什么那天在哭？
   **英文**：Why were you crying that day?
   > **解析**：were crying 是过去进行时，表示当时正在发生的动作。

12. **中文**：你过去住在哪里？
   **英文**：Where did you live before?
   > **解析**：where did 引导过去时疑问句；did 后动词用原形 live。

13. **中文**：谁教过你做饭？
   **英文**：Who taught you to cook?
   > **解析**：who 作主语直接加过去式 taught，不需要 did。

14. **中文**：你们多久见一次面？
   **英文**：How often did you meet your friends?
   > **解析**：how often did 询问过去频率；meet 用原形。

15. **中文**：我如此想念那段时光。
   **英文**：I miss those days so much.
   > **解析**：so much 加强 miss 的程度；those days 是复数。

16. **中文**：她过去以教书谋生。
   **英文**：She made a living by teaching.
   > **解析**：make a living 表示"谋生"；by teaching 表示方式。

17. **中文**：那是一生中最完美的一天。
   **英文**：It was the most perfect day of my life.
   > **解析**：was 是 is 的过去式；of my life 表示"我一生中"。

18. **中文**：我保留了所有旧照片。
   **英文**：I kept all the old photos.
   > **解析**：kept 是 keep 的过去式；all the old photos 作宾语。

19. **中文**：我认识他很多年了。
   **英文**：I knew him for many years.
   > **解析**：knew 是 know 的过去式；for many years 表示持续的时间段。

20. **中文**：有一天她突然回来了。
   **英文**：One day she came back suddenly.
   > **解析**：one day 表示"有一天"；came 是 come 的过去式。

<quiz-cloze>

### 1

I lived in an apartment ____ when I was a child.

- [x] building
- [ ] lake
- [ ] ring

> **解析**：apartment building 是固定搭配"公寓楼"；lake 和 ring 与 apartment 不搭配。

### 2

Twenty years ____, there was a lake here.

- [x] ago
- [ ] always
- [ ] alone

> **解析**：时间段 + ago 表示"……以前"；always 是频率副词，alone 表独自。

### 3

My neighbor ____ me to swim.

- [x] taught
- [ ] teach
- [ ] teaching

> **解析**：叙述过去用 taught（teach 的过去式）；teach 原形和 teaching 都不单独作过去谓语。

### 4

We always played by the lake ____.

- [x] alone
- [ ] ago
- [ ] special

> **解析**：alone 作状语表示"独自地"；ago 必须放时间段后，special 是形容词。

### 5

She got ____ last year.

- [x] married
- [ ] marry
- [ ] marriage

> **解析**：get married 是固定搭配"结婚"；marry 是动词原形，marriage 是名词，都不用于 got 后。

### 6

When I got back home, it ____ dark.

- [x] was
- [ ] is
- [ ] were

> **解析**：it 是单数且叙述过去，用 was；is 是现在时，were 配复数。

### 7

This ring ____ to my grandmother.

- [x] belongs
- [ ] belong
- [ ] belonging

> **解析**：主语 This ring 是单数，用 belongs；belong 原形不符，belonging 不能单独作谓语。

### 8

This necklace is very ____.

- [x] special
- [ ] so much
- [ ] ago

> **解析**：is 后接形容词 special；so much 是程度短语，ago 是时间副词，都不作表语。

### 9

I always ____ that suit on special days.

- [x] wore
- [ ] wear
- [ ] wearing

> **解析**：回忆过去用 wore（wear 的过去式）；wear 是现在时，wearing 不能单独作谓语。

### 10

He ____ a lot when he was a little child.

- [x] cried
- [ ] cry
- [ ] crying

> **解析**：was 表明过去语境，用 cried；cry 原形和 crying 都不能单独作过去谓语。

### 11

Why were you ____ that day?

- [x] crying
- [ ] cried
- [ ] cry

> **解析**：were + crying 构成过去进行时；cried 是过去式不能与 were 连用，cry 原形也不符。

### 12

Where ____ you live before?

- [x] did
- [ ] do
- [ ] was

> **解析**：过去时疑问句用 did；do 是现在时，was 后不接动词原形。

### 13

Who ____ you to cook?

- [x] taught
- [ ] did
- [ ] was

> **解析**：who 作主语直接接过去式 taught；用 did 需还原动词，was 语义不通。

### 14

How ____ did you meet your friends?

- [x] often
- [ ] many
- [ ] much

> **解析**：how often 询问频率；many 和 much 问数量，不表频率。

### 15

I miss those days so ____.

- [x] much
- [ ] many
- [ ] often

> **解析**：so much 是固定程度短语；many 修饰可数名词，often 是频率副词。

### 16

She made a ____ by teaching.

- [x] living
- [ ] life
- [ ] alone

> **解析**：make a living 是固定搭配"谋生"；life 不与 make a 构成此搭配，alone 是形容词。

### 17

It was the perfect day of my ____.

- [x] life
- [ ] living
- [ ] day

> **解析**：of my life 表示"我一生中"；living 指生计，day 与 the perfect day 语义重复。

### 18

I ____ all the old photos.

- [x] kept
- [ ] keep
- [ ] keeping

> **解析**：叙述过去用 kept（keep 的过去式）；keep 是现在时，keeping 不能单独作谓语。

### 19

I ____ him for many years.

- [x] knew
- [ ] know
- [ ] knewed

> **解析**：know 的过去式是不规则的 knew；know 是现在时，knewed 不存在。

### 20

One ____ she came back suddenly.

- [x] day
- [ ] ago
- [ ] year

> **解析**：one day 是固定表达"有一天"；ago 不用于 one 后，one year 需配合 ago 表"一年前"。
