## 单元讲解

本单元围绕"谈论户外旅行"展开。核心句型包括：Why don't we go hiking?（我们去徒步怎么样？）、Be careful of snakes!（小心蛇！）、The view is amazing.（景色太美了。）、I prefer hiking by myself.（我更喜欢独自徒步。）、What's the temperature?（气温多少？）。

词表里有自然词汇（forest, path, star, the moon, the sun）和动物（monkey, rabbit, snake）。你可以练习：Let's hike along the path. / There are wild monkeys in the forest. / The temperature is low today. / It's foggy—I can't see the view.

why don't 用于提建议。prefer 表示"更喜欢"。

## Tips

- **why don't** — ……怎么样？（提建议）。搭配：Why don't we go hiking? / Why don't you join us? 例句：Why don't we hike along the path? / Why don't you come with us? 注意 Why don't we...? = Let's...（更礼貌）。

- **be careful of** — 小心。搭配：Be careful of snakes! / be careful of the path, be careful。例句：Be careful of wild animals! / Be careful of the deep water. 注意 be careful of + 危险事物。

- **prefer** — 更喜欢。搭配：prefer hiking, prefer A to B, prefer to do。例句：I prefer hiking to cycling. / She prefers to go by herself. 注意 prefer A to B（比起B更喜欢A）。

- **by yourself / myself** — 独自。搭配：by yourself, by myself, by oneself。例句：Did you go hiking by yourself? / I prefer to hike by myself. 注意 by oneself = alone（独自）。

- **forest / path** — 森林 / 小路。搭配：in the forest, along the path, a forest path。例句：Let's walk along the path through the forest. / The forest is dark. 注意 forest 比 woods 更大更密。

- **monkey / rabbit / snake** — 猴子 / 兔子 / 蛇。搭配：a wild monkey, a cute rabbit, be careful of snakes。例句：I saw a wild monkey! / The rabbit is cute. / Be careful of snakes! 注意 wild 表示"野生的"。

- **temperature** — 温度、气温。搭配：the temperature is low / high, What's the temperature? 例句：The temperature is low today. / What's the temperature outside? 注意 temperature 的缩写是 temp（口语）。

- **foggy / deep / low** — 有雾的 / 深的 / 低的。搭配：foggy weather, a deep river, low temperature。例句：It's foggy—I can't see the view. / The river is deep. / The temperature is low.

- **the moon / the sun / star** — 月亮 / 太阳 / 星星。搭配：the moon is bright, the sun is setting, look at the stars。例句：The moon is beautiful tonight. / I can see the stars. 注意 moon 和 sun 前加 the。

- **view** — 景色、视野。搭配：a beautiful view, the view from the top, enjoy the view。例句：The view from the mountain is amazing! / What a view! 注意 view 和 scenery 的区别：view 是从某处看到的景色。

- **hike / join** — 徒步 / 加入。搭配：go hiking, hike along the path, join us, join the hike。例句：Let's go hiking! / Do you want to join us? / We hiked along the river.

- **sound** — 听起来、声音。搭配：That sounds great! / a strange sound, the sound of water。例句：Why don't we go hiking? That sounds great! / I heard a strange sound. 注意 sound 既是动词也是名词。

<quiz-sentence-builder>

1. **中文**：我们在茂密古老的原始森林里沿着小路漫步。
   **英文**：We walked down the path in the ancient forest.
   > **解析**：forest 表示“森林”；path 表示“小路”。

2. **中文**：沿着山间蜿蜒的小路前行，能看到清澈的溪流。
   **英文**：Follow the winding path along the mountain stream.
   > **解析**：path 表示“小路/路径”。

3. **中文**：我们计划在这个周末去高山郊外徒步远足。
   **英文**：We plan to hike in the mountains this weekend.
   > **解析**：hike 表示“徒步/远足”。

4. **中文**：清晨的山谷多雾且视野不佳，开慢点。
   **英文**：The valley was very foggy early in the morning.
   > **解析**：foggy 表示“多雾的/雾蒙蒙的”。

5. **中文**：山顶上的气温温度比山脚低了整整十度。
   **英文**：The temperature on mountain top is much lower.
   > **解析**：temperature 表示“温度/气温”。

6. **中文**：晴朗夜空里眨着眼的星星显得格外璀璨。
   **英文**：Twinkling stars shining brightly in dark night sky.
   > **解析**：stars 表示“星星”。

7. **中文**：金色的太阳从东方的平原慢慢升起。
   **英文**：The golden sun rose slowly in the eastern plain.
   > **解析**：sun 表示“太阳”。

8. **中文**：弯弯的月亮挂在皎洁的夜空之中。
   **英文**：The crescent moon hung in the quiet night sky.
   > **解析**：moon 表示“月亮”。

9. **中文**：小溪汇入深邃清澈的清潭水池之中。
   **英文**：The stream flows into a deep and clear pool.
   > **解析**：deep 表示“深邃的/深的”。

10. **中文**：夜里森林里的气温变得非常低，注意保暖。
   **英文**：The night temperature in forest dropped very low.
   > **解析**：low 表示“低的”。

11. **中文**：那只在树枝间跳跃的小松鼠非常可爱。
   **英文**：That small squirrel on the branch is very cute.
   > **解析**：cute 表示“可爱的”。

12. **中文**：我们在国家公园里保护着许多珍稀的野生动物。
   **英文**：We protect many wild animals in national parks.
   > **解析**：wild 表示“野生的”。

13. **中文**：调皮的猴子在树冠间灵活地荡来荡去。
   **英文**：The playful monkey swung through treetops.
   > **解析**：monkey 表示“猴子”。

14. **中文**：毛茸茸的小白兔子在草地上快活地蹦跳。
   **英文**：The fluffy white rabbit hopped on green grass.
   > **解析**：rabbit 表示“兔子”。

15. **中文**：草丛里藏着一条草绿色的野生蛇，请当心。
   **英文**：A wild green snake was hidden in the grass.
   > **解析**：snake 表示“蛇”。

16. **中文**：山谷深处传来一阵美妙悦耳的水流声音。
   **英文**：A sweet sound of flowing water came from valley.
   > **解析**：sound 表示“声音”。

17. **中文**：站在山顶上，你可以听见风吹过树林的声音。
   **英文**：You can hear the wind blowing through trees.
   > **解析**：hear 表示“听见/听到”。

18. **中文**：在森林里野外徒步时请务必当心毒蛇。
   **英文**：Please be careful of poisonous snakes in forest.
   > **解析**：be careful of 表示“当心/注意”。

19. **中文**：相比繁忙嘈杂的都市，我更喜欢大自然的宁静。
   **英文**：I prefer the quiet of nature to busy cities.
   > **解析**：prefer 表示“更喜欢/偏好”；nature 表示“大自然”。

20. **中文**：走进大自然能让人的身心得到彻底的放松。
   **英文**：Stepping into nature relaxes mind and body.
   > **解析**：nature 表示“大自然”。
