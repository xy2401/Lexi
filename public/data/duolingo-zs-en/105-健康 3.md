## 单元讲解

本单元围绕"和医生交流病情"展开。核心是看病表达：My ankle is broken—I'm in a lot of pain.（我的脚踝骨折了——非常疼。）、You need to lie down and rest.（你需要躺下休息。）、Take the medicine before bed.（睡前吃药。）、The nurse will take your blood pressure.（护士会量你的血压。）。

词表里有身体部位（ankle, brain, heart, stomach）和医疗词汇（x-ray, medicine, nurse, band-aid, vet）。你可以练习：My stomach hurts badly. / He hurt himself playing sports. / Do yoga and tai chi to stay healthy. / The doctor said to exercise more.

in pain 表示"处于疼痛中"。反身代词 himself / itself 表示"他自己/它自己"。

## Tips

- **in pain / hurt** — 疼痛 / 受伤。搭配：in a lot of pain, in pain, hurt my ankle, hurt himself。例句：I'm in a lot of pain. / She hurt her ankle. / He hurt himself. 注意 hurt 的过去式还是 hurt。

- **broken / ankle** — 骨折 / 脚踝。搭配：a broken ankle, broken bone, break a leg。例句：My ankle is broken. / He has a broken arm. 注意 broken 是形容词（骨折的），break 是动词。

- **lie down / lying** — 躺下 / 躺着。搭配：lie down, lying on the bed, lie down and rest。例句：Lie down and rest. / She's lying on the sofa. 注意 lie（躺）→ lay → lain，lying 是现在分词。

- **medicine / take** — 药 / 吃（药）。搭配：take medicine, take the medicine before meals。例句：Take the medicine three times a day. / Did you take your medicine? 注意"吃药"用 take（不用 ✗ eat）。

- **nurse / vet** — 护士 / 兽医。搭配：the nurse said, see a nurse, take the dog to the vet。例句：The nurse took my temperature. / Take the cat to the vet. 注意 vet = veterinarian（兽医）。

- **x-ray / blood** — X光 / 血。搭配：take an x-ray, an x-ray of my ankle, blood test, blood pressure。例句：You need an x-ray. / The nurse took a blood sample. 注意 x-ray 小写或大写都可以。

- **stomach / stomachache** — 胃 / 胃痛。搭配：my stomach hurts, have a stomachache。例句：My stomach hurts. / I have a terrible stomachache. 注意 -ache 表示"……痛"（headache, toothache, stomachache）。

- **exercise / fit / healthy** — 锻炼 / 健康 / 健康的。搭配：do exercise, keep fit, stay healthy。例句：Exercise to keep fit. / Eat well and stay healthy. 注意 fit 强调"体能好"，healthy 强调"没病"。

- **yoga / tai chi** — 瑜伽 / 太极。搭配：do yoga, practice tai chi。例句：She does yoga every morning. / Tai chi is good for your health. 注意 yoga 和 tai chi 都是不可数名词。

- **himself / itself** — 他自己 / 它自己。搭配：hurt himself, by itself, itself。例句：He hurt himself playing football. / The wound healed itself. 注意反身代词用于主语和宾语相同时。

- **band-aid / simple** — 创可贴 / 简单的。搭配：put a band-aid on, a simple fix。例句：Just put a band-aid on it. / It's a simple problem. 注意 Band-Aid 是品牌名，band-aid 是通用说法。

- **brain / heart / health** — 大脑 / 心脏 / 健康。搭配：the human brain, heart disease, good for your health。例句：Exercise is good for your brain and heart. / Health is more important than money.

<quiz-sentence-builder>

1. **中文**：患者在从楼梯上摔下后感到剧烈疼痛。  
   **英文**：The patient was in severe pain after falling down the stairs.
   > **解析**：in pain 为固定形容词短语，意为“疼痛的，处于痛苦中”，作表语。

2. **中文**：X光检查显示他左臂的骨头骨折了。  
   **英文**：The X-ray revealed that the bone in his left arm was broken.
   > **解析**：broken 作形容词，意为“骨折的，破裂的”。在句中作表语。

3. **中文**：她昨天在跑道上跑步时扭伤了右脚踝。  
   **英文**：She twisted her right ankle while running on the track yesterday.
   > **解析**：ankle 作名词，意为“脚踝，踝关节”。twist one's ankle 意为“扭伤脚踝”。

4. **中文**：请躺在床上，以便医生检查你的胸部。  
   **英文**：Please lie down on the bed so the doctor can examine your chest.
   > **解析**：lie down 为动词短语，意为“躺下”。祈使句形式。

5. **中文**：请在饭后服用这种开好的药物，一日三次。  
   **英文**：Take this prescribed medicine three times a day after meals.
   > **解析**：medicine 作不可数/可数名词，意为“药物，药”。take medicine 意为“服药”。

6. **中文**：细心的护士检查了我的体温并测量了脉搏。  
   **英文**：The caring nurse checked my body temperature and measured my pulse.
   > **解析**：nurse 作可数名词，意为“护士”。作句子的主语。

7. **中文**：快速喝冷水让他突然胃痛。  
   **英文**：Drinking cold water quickly gave him a sudden stomachache.
   > **解析**：stomachache 作名词，意为“胃痛，腹痛”。give someone a stomachache 表示“引发胃痛”。

8. **中文**：每天进行规律的运动有助于维持良好的心脏健康。  
   **英文**：Doing regular exercise every day helps maintain good heart health.
   > **解析**：exercise 作不可数名词，意为“体育锻炼”。do exercise 意为“进行锻炼”。

9. **中文**：在轻微的伤口上贴一张创可贴以防止感染。  
   **英文**：Put a band-aid on the minor cut to prevent infection.
   > **解析**：band-aid 作名词，意为“创可贴”。put a band-aid on... 表示“贴创可贴”。

10. **中文**：吃新鲜蔬菜和水果能保持你的免疫系统健康。  
   **英文**：Eating fresh vegetables and fruits keeps your immune system healthy.
   > **解析**：healthy 作形容词，意为“健康的”。在句中作补语修饰 system。
