## 单元讲解

本单元围绕"描述求职经历"展开。核心句型包括：I'm looking for a job.（我在找工作。）、Pleased to meet you.（很高兴认识你。）、Is it ok if I take a break?（我可以休息一下吗？）、How long have you worked here?（你在这里工作多久了？）、Good luck with your interview!（面试加油！）。

词表里有职场词汇（department, engineer, manager, receptionist, secretary, résumé）和求职用语（look for, fill out a form, advice）。你可以练习：I'd like to speak with the manager. / Please fill out this form. / She has been a secretary since last year. / I want to improve my skills.

since 搭配现在完成时。is it ok if / is it ok to 是礼貌请求。

## Tips

- **look for** — 寻找。搭配：look for a job, look for work, looking for。例句：I'm looking for a job as an engineer. / She's looking for a new position. 注意 look for 强调"寻找"的过程，find 强调"找到"的结果。

- **résumé** — 简历（美式）。搭配：send a résumé, write a résumé, my résumé。注意 résumé 来自法语，有重音符号。英式用 CV（curriculum vitae）。美式也可以写 resume（无重音）。

- **pleased to meet you** — 很高兴认识你。搭配：Pleased to meet you. / Nice to meet you. 注意这是初次见面的正式用语。回答：Pleased to meet you too. / The pleasure is mine.

- **is it ok if / is it ok to** — 可以……吗？（礼貌请求）。搭配：Is it ok if I leave early? / Is it ok to take a break? 例句：Is it ok if I call you back? / Is it ok to sit here? 注意比 Can I...? 更礼貌。

- **get along** — 相处。搭配：get along with, get along well, get along with colleagues。例句：I get along well with my manager. / Do you get along with your team? 注意 get along with + 人。

- **department / manager / secretary** — 部门 / 经理 / 秘书。搭配：the engineering department, the department manager, a secretary in the office。例句：She works in the engineering department. / The manager is in a meeting.

- **engineer / receptionist** — 工程师 / 前台接待。搭配：a software engineer, work as an engineer, the receptionist at the front desk。例句：I'm an engineer. / The receptionist can help you.

- **fill / form** — 填写 / 表格。搭配：fill out a form, fill in the form, an application form。例句：Please fill out this form. / I filled in the application form. 注意美式 fill out，英式 fill in。

- **since / how long** — 自从 / 多久。搭配：since last year, since 2020, How long have you...? 例句：I've worked here since last year. / How long have you been an engineer? 注意 since + 时间点，for + 时间段。

- **improve** — 提高、改善。搭配：improve my skills, improve my English, improve at。例句：I want to improve my programming skills. / She improved a lot. 注意 improve 既是及物也是不及物动词。

- **advice** — 建议。搭配：give advice, some advice, a piece of advice。例句：Can you give me some advice? / That's good advice. 注意 advice 不可数（不说 ✗ an advice / ✗ advices）。用 a piece of advice。

- **good luck / certainly** — 祝好运 / 当然。搭配：Good luck! / Good luck with... / Certainly! 例句：Good luck with your interview! / — Can I help you? — Certainly! 注意 certainly 比 yes 更正式、更热情。

<quiz-sentence-builder>

1. **中文**：在我调取您的求职申请文件时，请您稍等片刻好吗？  
   **英文**：Could you wait a minute while I retrieve your job file?
   > **解析**：**a minute** 作时间量词短语意为“一分钟、片刻”。wait a minute 是日常职场中礼貌请对方稍候的标准表达。

2. **中文**：员工有权在每天下午享受十五分钟的咖啡休息时间。  
   **英文**：Employees are entitled to a fifteen-minute coffee break.
   > **解析**：**break** 作可数名词意为“休息、小憩”。coffee break 表示“茶歇/咖啡休息时间”，作介词 to 的宾语。

3. **中文**：在面试前，请在表格上填写好您的个人背景信息。  
   **英文**：Please fill out this background form before your interview (be with).
   > **解析**：**fill** 作动词，fill out 是固定短语意为“填写（表格/问卷）”，在求职面试场景中极高频。

4. **中文**：人力资源经理已经仔细审阅了您的简历和求职信。  
   **英文**：The HR manager has reviewed your resume carefully.
   > **解析**：**has** 是助动词 accumulate 第三意形态。has reviewed 构成现在完成时，表示过去完成的动作对现在产生的影响。

5. **中文**：今天下午通过电子邮件发送我更新后的作品集可以吗？  
   **英文**：Is it ok to send my updated portfolio via email today.
   > **解析**：**is it ok to** 是常用客气询问句型，意为“做……可以吗/行不行？”，后接动词原形 send，向招聘方征询意见。

6. **中文**：我目前正在寻找一份市场营销方面的全职工作。  
   **英文**：I am currently looking for a full-time position in marketing.
   > **解析**：**look for** 是固定动词短语，意为“寻找、求职”。am looking for 构成现在进行时，表达正在求职的状态。

7. **中文**：前台接待员热情地问候了每一位前来应聘的候选人。  
   **英文**：The front desk receptionist greeted all job candidates warmly.
   > **解析**：**receptionist** 作可数名词意为“接待员、前台人员”，由 receive 衍生而来。前置名词 front desk 饰限定。

8. **中文**：申请人必须提交一份书面的个人陈述说明。  
   **英文**：Applicants are required to submit a written statement.
   > **解析**：**written** 是动词 write 的过去分词，作形容词意为“书面的、文字的”。written statement 指“书面声明”。

9. **中文**：面试官答应下周二给我打电话告知结果。  
   **英文**：The interviewer promised to call me next Tuesday with the result.
   > **解析**：**call** 作及物动词意为“打电话给（某人）”。promise to call sb. 描述招聘流程中的后续通知约定。

10. **中文**：请在这份录用协议表格的底部签上您的名字。  
   **英文**：Please sign your name at the bottom of the employment form.
   > **解析**：**form** 作可数名词意为“表格、申请单”。employment form（入职/雇佣表格）在求职环节中不可或缺。
