## 单元讲解

本单元的主题是"说明产品的问题"，场景是退货与客服交涉。词表可以分成四组：产品问题（defective, no good, useless, waterproof, nothing wrong with, state, sticker）、退款与保障（refund, full refund, guarantee, proof of purchase, policy, process）、客服场景（supervisor, service, satisfaction, speak politely to, unavailable, everybody）、以及语气与情态（must i, must we, carefully, in need of, their）。拼起来就是一段退货对话：This watch is defective. I'd like a full refund, and I have the proof of purchase right here.

语法主线有两个。一是 must 表"必须"的疑问与情绪用法：Must I wait longer?（我必须继续等吗？）must we... 常带抱怨语气。二是描述产品状态的表达：There's nothing wrong with it（它没毛病）与 It is defective（它有缺陷）构成买卖双方的典型对立。

学完这个单元，你能用英语说明产品缺陷、要求退款、引用保修政策，还能与客服有效沟通。

## Tips

- **refund / full refund / guarantee** — refund = 退款：get a refund；full refund = 全额退款；guarantee = 保修、保证：a two-year guarantee。money-back guarantee 是"退款保证"。

- **defective / no good / useless** — 三个差评词。defective = 有缺陷的：a defective part；no good = 没用、不行：The battery is no good. useless = 毫无用处的，语气最强。

- **nothing wrong with / state / sticker** — There's nothing wrong with the product.（产品没问题。）state 作动词"陈述、说明"，也可指"状态"：the state of the box；sticker = 贴纸、标签：the price sticker。

- **waterproof** — 防水的（water + proof），-proof 后缀表示"防……的"：fireproof, shockproof。手表广告常见。

- **proof of purchase / policy / process** — proof of purchase = 购买凭证（收据或发票）；policy = 政策、规定：return policy（退货政策）；process = 流程：the refund process。

- **supervisor / service / satisfaction** — supervisor = 主管；service = 服务：customer service（客服）；satisfaction = 满意度：satisfaction guaranteed（保证满意）。

- **speak politely to / must i / must we** — speak politely to = 礼貌地与……说话；must I / must we 是"我必须……吗"，常带不耐烦：Must we go through this again? 注意 must 表必要，mustn't 表禁止。

- **unavailable / carefully / in need of / everybody** — unavailable = 没空、不可用：The manager is unavailable. carefully = 仔细地；in need of = 需要：in need of repair；everybody 作单数主语。

<quiz-sentence-builder>

1. **中文**：这块表有缺陷。
   **英文**：This watch is defective.
   > **解析**：defective 是形容词作表语；单数主语搭配 is。

2. **中文**：我想要求全额退款。
   **英文**：I would like a full refund.
   > **解析**：would like + 名词表礼貌请求；full refund 是固定搭配"全额退款"。

3. **中文**：这是保修两年。
   **英文**：It has a two-year guarantee.
   > **解析**：guarantee 作名词"保修"；two-year 是带连字符的复合形容词。

4. **中文**：我有购买凭证。
   **英文**：I have the proof of purchase.
   > **解析**：proof of purchase 是固定搭配"购买凭证"；have 表拥有。

5. **中文**：这是我们的退货政策。
   **英文**：This is our return policy.
   > **解析**：return policy 是固定搭配"退货政策"；policy 作表语。

6. **中文**：退款流程需要三天。
   **英文**：The refund process takes three days.
   > **解析**：takes 表示"花费（时间）"；主语 process 是单三。

7. **中文**：电池没用了。
   **英文**：The battery is no good.
   > **解析**：no good 是固定表达"不行、没用"，作表语。

8. **中文**：没有说明书，这东西毫无用处。
   **英文**：Without the manual, it is useless.
   > **解析**：Without 短语作条件状语；useless 作表语。

9. **中文**：这个产品没有任何问题。
   **英文**：There is nothing wrong with the product.
   > **解析**：There is nothing wrong with... 是固定句型；wrong 修饰 nothing 后置。

10. **中文**：这块手表是防水的。
   **英文**：The watch is waterproof.
   > **解析**：waterproof 是形容词作表语；-proof 表示"防……"。

11. **中文**：我必须等更久吗？
   **英文**：Must I wait any longer?
   > **解析**：Must I + 动词原形表示"我必须……吗"，常带不耐烦语气。

12. **中文**：我们必须把整个过程再来一遍吗？
   **英文**：Must we go through the whole process again?
   > **解析**：Must we 疑问句表抱怨；go through = 经历。

13. **中文**：请礼貌地与客服人员交谈。
   **英文**：Please speak politely to the staff.
   > **解析**：speak politely to 中 politely 是副词修饰 speak；to the staff 说明对象。

14. **中文**：经理现在没空。
   **英文**：The manager is unavailable right now.
   > **解析**：unavailable 作表语"没空"；right now 是时间状语。

15. **中文**：请找主管来。
   **英文**：Please call the supervisor.
   > **解析**：call 这里表示"叫来"；the supervisor 作宾语。

16. **中文**：我们保证您的满意。
   **英文**：We guarantee your satisfaction.
   > **解析**：guarantee 作动词"保证"；your satisfaction 作宾语。

17. **中文**：请仔细检查盒子。
   **英文**：Please check the box carefully.
   > **解析**：carefully 是副词，修饰 check，放在宾语之后。

18. **中文**：这台机器需要修理。
   **英文**：The machine is in need of repair.
   > **解析**：in need of 是固定搭配"需要"；repair 作名词。

19. **中文**：价格标签还在上面。
   **英文**：The price sticker is still on it.
   > **解析**：sticker 是"贴纸、标签"；on it 表示位置。

20. **中文**：请说明一下情况。
   **英文**：Please state the problem clearly.
   > **解析**：state 是动词"陈述"；clearly 是副词修饰 state。

### 1

The speaker stopped working; it must be ____.

- [x] defective
- [ ] defect
- [ ] defectively

> **解析**：be 后需要形容词 defective；defect 是名词，defectively 不是常用词。

### 2

The shop promised me a full ____.

- [x] refund
- [ ] refunds
- [ ] refunded

> **解析**：a full 后接单数名词 refund；复数和过去式不合结构。

### 3

The watch comes with a five-year ____.

- [x] guarantee
- [ ] guaranteed
- [ ] guaranteeing

> **解析**：a five-year 后需要名词 guarantee；其余两项是动词形式。

### 4

Keep your receipt as proof of ____.

- [x] purchase
- [ ] purchasing's
- [ ] purchaser

> **解析**：proof of purchase 是固定搭配"购买凭证"；purchaser 是"购买者"。

### 5

- [ ] policing
