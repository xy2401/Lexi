## 单元讲解

本单元围绕"讲述英国的生活经历"展开。核心是更多不规则过去式：flew（fly 的过去式）, had（have 的过去式）, met（meet 的过去式）, took（take 的过去式）, went（go 的过去式）。你可以练习：I flew to London last year. / She lived in the UK for two years. / We had fish and chips. / I met a friend there.

how long 用于询问时间长度：How long did you live there? / How long did you study? 回答用 for + 时间段：For three years.

英国文化词汇：afternoon tea（下午茶）, fish and chips（炸鱼薯条）, black tea / green tea。注意英国人说 chips 指的是粗薯条（美式叫 french fries），而美式的 chips（薯片）在英国叫 crisps。

## Tips

- **flew / fly / flies** — 飞。fly 的过去式是 flew（不规则），第三人称单数是 flies。搭配：fly to London, flew home, flies every week。注意 fly → flew → flown（过去分词）。

- **had** — have 的过去式。搭配：had lunch, had a good time, had fish and chips。例句：We had afternoon tea. / She had a cookie. 注意 had 是 have/has 的过去式，所有人称通用。

- **met** — meet 的过去式。搭配：met a friend, met my wife, first met。例句：I met her at a concert. / Where did you meet? 注意 meet → met → met（过去式和过去分词相同）。

- **went** — go 的过去式。搭配：went to London, went home, went swimming。例句：She went to the pool. / They went to work. 注意 go → went → gone（三个形式都不同）。

- **how long** — 多久、多长。搭配：How long did you live there? / How long was the flight? 回答：For two years. / About ten hours. 注意 how long 问时间长度或物体长度。

- **afternoon tea** — 下午茶（英国传统）。搭配：have afternoon tea, at afternoon tea。注意英国下午茶通常在下午3-4点，包含茶、三明治和甜点。这是英国文化的重要组成部分。

- **fish and chips** — 炸鱼薯条（英国经典食物）。搭配：have fish and chips, a fish and chips shop。注意 chips 在英式英语中是粗薯条，在美式英语中是薯片（英式叫 crisps）。

- **black tea / green tea** — 红茶 / 绿茶。搭配：a cup of black tea, drink green tea。注意中文"红茶"在英文中是 black tea（因为茶叶颜色是黑的），不是 ✗ red tea。

- **arrived / lived / studied / worked** — 规则动词过去式。arrive → arrived, live → lived, study → studied, work → worked。注意 study → studied（变 y 为 i 加 -ed）。搭配：arrived in London, lived for a year, studied English, worked at a cafe。

- **pool** — 游泳池、水塘。搭配：swimming pool, go to the pool, at the pool。注意 pool 单独使用通常指游泳池。pool 也可以指台球（play pool）。

- **cookie** — 饼干（美式）。搭配：a cookie, chocolate chip cookie。注意美式 cookie = 英式 biscuit。英式的 cookie 通常指软曲奇，biscuit 指脆饼干。

- **wrong** — 错误的、不对劲的。搭配：What's wrong? / something wrong, the wrong answer。注意 wrong 和 false 的区别：wrong 更通用（错误的/不对的），false 侧重"虚假的"（false information）。
