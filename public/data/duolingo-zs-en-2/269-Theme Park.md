# Unit 269: Theme Park

> Theme Park: Suggest plans for a visit

## 单词

after next, all of a sudden, amusement park, cotton candy, ferris wheel, gigantic, go on, hail, head, hot dog, packed, requirement, ride, roller coaster, season pass, stuffed animal, temporarily, upside down, wait in line, was hoping to, was thinking, were supposed to, wondering where, wristband

## 单元讲解

本单元的场景是游乐园一日游：坐过山车（roller coaster）、摩天轮（ferris wheel），吃棉花糖（cotton candy）和热狗（hot dog），排队（wait in line）玩各种项目（ride）。你将学会和朋友商量游玩计划、应对突发变化。

语法重点是过去进行时的委婉表达：was hoping to、was thinking、were supposed to 用过去时态表达礼貌的提议或未实现的计划，比直接说 I want 更委婉。all of a sudden 和 temporarily 描述计划中的意外变化。

词汇按游乐园体验分组：游乐设施（roller coaster, ferris wheel, ride, amusement park）、食物与纪念品（cotton candy, hot dog, stuffed animal）、计划与变化（was hoping to, were supposed to, all of a sudden, temporarily, packed, after next）。学完本单元，你能用英语规划一次游乐园行程并灵活应对变化。

## Tips

- **roller coaster** — 过山车，字面义"翻滚的海岸"。引申义指"大起大落"：an emotional roller coaster。
- **ferris wheel** — 摩天轮，以发明者 George Ferris 命名。注意 Ferris 首字母大写，因为是人名。
- **were supposed to** — 表"本应该（但没做到）"或"按计划应该"。We were supposed to meet at 3, but... 比 should have 更中性。
- **packed** — 挤满人的，来自 pack（塞满）。The park was packed. 比 crowded 更口语、更形象。
- **cotton candy** — 棉花糖，美式说法；英式叫 candy floss。因口感像棉花（cotton）而得名。
- **season pass** — 季票/年卡，可在整个赛季无限次入园。比 single ticket 划算，是游乐园常见营销手段。
- **upside down** — 倒置的，过山车翻转时的状态。也可形容房间"乱七八糟"：The room is upside down.
- **wristband** — 腕带，游乐园用来标识门票类型或快速通道权限。wrist（手腕）+ band（带子）。
