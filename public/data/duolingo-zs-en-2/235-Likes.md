# Unit 235: Likes

> Likes: Compliment someone's home

## 单词

appealing, appropriately, by, can't get enough of, can't wait, catch up, don't, entertaining, homemade, housewarming, how, impressive, in style, isn't, lovely, miss out, obsessed with, play it by ear, show up, sounds, too hard on, unique, up to, virtual reality, won't

## 单元讲解

本单元的场景是参加朋友的乔迁派对（housewarming），参观新家并表达赞美，同时聊聊近况。话题轻松愉快，核心交际功能是"真诚地夸赞他人的品味和空间"。

语法重点在于表达喜好的多样句式：can't get enough of（怎么都看不够）、obsessed with（对……着迷）、lovely / impressive / appealing 等形容词的灵活运用。play it by ear 是习语，意为"到时候再说、见机行事"，体现口语中的随意态度。

词汇可分为三组：赞美与评价类（appealing, impressive, lovely, unique, in style, entertaining）、社交活动类（housewarming, catch up, show up, miss out, can't wait）、态度与方式类（play it by ear, too hard on, appropriately, up to, obsessed with）。virtual reality 和 homemade 则增添了具体话题细节。

学完本单元，你能在参观朋友新家时自然地表达欣赏，并用轻松的方式安排社交聚会。

## Tips

- **housewarming** — 由 house + warming 构成，指"乔迁派对"，即搬入新居后邀请朋友来暖房的聚会。
- **can't get enough of** — 意为"对……怎么也不嫌多、非常着迷"，语气比 like 强烈得多。I can't get enough of this view!
- **play it by ear** — 源自音乐术语（不看乐谱即兴演奏），引申为"随机应变、到时候再决定"。Let's play it by ear for dinner.
- **show up** — 意为"出现、到场"，比 arrive 更口语化，有时暗含"终于来了"或" unexpectedly 出现"的意味。
- **obsessed with** — 意为"对……极度痴迷"，语气比 interested in 强很多，口语中常带夸张色彩。She's obsessed with interior design.
- **in style** — 意为"有格调地、时髦地"，也可写作 in style。They decorated the whole place in style.
- **miss out** — 常接 on，意为"错过（好事）"。You don't want to miss out on the housewarming party.
- **too hard on** — 意为"对……太苛刻"，常用于劝慰。Don't be too hard on yourself about the mess.
