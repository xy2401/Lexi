# Unit 059: 在厨房

> 分享做饭经验

## 单词

also, any, bag, board, bottle, bottle of, butter, close to, come from, cooked, cracker, cup, dirty, dish, dishes, finish, for, fork, having, how much, knife, knives, locker, meal, much, put, ready, salt, spoon, wash, what about

## 单元讲解

本单元的场景是厨房和烹饪，你将学会描述做饭过程、厨房用具和食材。例如 "I cooked dinner and washed the dishes" 或 "How much salt do we need?"。

语法上涉及不可数名词与量词搭配（a bottle of, a cup of, how much），以及 knife → knives 这类 f/fe 变 ves 的复数规则。what about 用于提出建议或转换话题，come from 表示产地。

词汇分为三组：厨具与餐具（knife, knives, fork, spoon, board, bottle, cup）、食材与烹饪（butter, salt, cracker, cooked, meal, dish, dishes）、动作与状态（wash, put, finish, ready, dirty, close to）。学完本单元，你能用英语描述一次完整的做饭经历，从准备食材到清洗餐具。

## Tips

- **knives** — knife 的复数，f/fe 结尾变 ves 的规则还适用于 wife → wives, life → lives
- **dishes** — do the dishes 是"洗碗"的固定搭配；dish 单数可指"一道菜"，复数常指"餐具"
- **bottle of** — 量词结构，a bottle of water / two bottles of oil；bottle 本身可数
- **how much** — 修饰不可数名词（how much salt），how many 修饰可数名词（how many eggs）
- **come from** — 表示"来自、产自"，This butter comes from New Zealand
- **what about** — 用于提建议或追问，What about having soup? 等同于 How about
- **ready** — 形容词"准备好的"，be ready to do / get ready for 是常见搭配
