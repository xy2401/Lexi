# Unit 107: 打电话

> 打电话

## 单词

all the best, answer, ask, at the moment, break up, broke up, calling to, cool, customer service, give me a call, got a call, hang out, hang up, hear, how may i, hung up, i mean, it's, leave, leave a message, never mind, one moment, press, put, rang, ring, say, sorry, speaking, talk to you soon, text, thank you for, then, this is, who's calling

## 单元讲解

本单元完整呈现电话交流的全流程：从接听（answer, this is, speaking）、询问来意（who's calling, how may I）、转达与留言（leave a message, press）到挂断（hang up, hung up）和结束寒暄（all the best, talk to you soon）。

语法上，本单元涉及大量电话专用句式：This is...（自我介绍，不说 I am）、Who's calling?（询问对方身份）、I'm calling to...（说明来意）。同时 hang up / hung up / ring / rang 等不规则动词的过去式是叙事重点，如 The line broke up and she hung up.

词汇可分为：电话动作（answer, ring/rang, hang up/hung up, press, text）、电话套语（this is, speaking, who's calling, how may I, one moment, leave a message）、社交收尾（all the best, talk to you soon, thank you for, never mind）。

学完本单元，你能用英语完成一通完整的电话，包括自我介绍、说明来意、留言和礼貌结束通话。

## Tips

- **this is** — 电话中自我介绍用 This is... 而非 I am...；询问对方用 Who's calling? 而非 Who are you?
- **hang up / hung up** — hang up 指"挂断电话"；过去式为 hung up。注意 hang 作"悬挂"时也是 hung，但"绞死"用 hanged。
- **ring / rang** — ring 指电话"响"，过去式为 rang，过去分词为 rung；英式英语中 ring 也指"打电话"，如 I'll ring you later.
- **leave a message** — 固定搭配，指"留言"；对应表达为 take a message（帮别人记留言），注意 leave 与 take 的方向。
- **at the moment** — 意为"此刻、目前"，相当于 right now；常用于解释对方不便接听，如 She's in a meeting at the moment.
- **break up** — 在电话语境中指"信号断续、听不清"，The line is breaking up. 与"分手"义项靠语境区分。
- **never mind** — 用于表示"算了、没关系"，放弃刚才的请求；语气比 it doesn't matter 更随意、更口语化。
