# Unit 260: Business

> Business: Propose a new business idea

## 单词

accessible, amount, between, by the end of, carefully, data, decline, drop, entrepreneur, gradually, investor, level off, move in, over time, profit, propose, quickly, quietly, rise, slightly, steady, stock market, the right time, up and running, vacuum cleaner, vegan

## 单元讲解

本单元围绕创业和商业提案展开：描述市场趋势、向投资人展示计划、讨论公司运营。在英语商务环境中，用数据说话并清晰表达增长或下降趋势是核心技能。

语法重点是描述趋势的动词和副词搭配。rise / drop / decline 描述方向，gradually / slightly / quickly / quietly 修饰速度和幅度，level off 表示趋于平稳。by the end of 和 over time 提供时间框架，up and running 描述运营状态。

词汇分为三组：趋势动词（rise, drop, decline, level off, steady）；商业核心词（entrepreneur, investor, profit, stock market, propose, data）；程度与时间副词（gradually, slightly, quickly, carefully, by the end of, over time, the right time）。

学完本单元，你能用英语描述商业数据趋势，提出创业想法，并在投资人面前清晰地展示你的商业计划。

## Tips

- **entrepreneur** — 来自法语 entreprendre（承担），注意发音重音在末尾 /ˌɑːntrəprəˈnɜːr/，拼写中 -eur 是法语痕迹。
- **level off** — 描述数据"趋于平稳"，比 become stable 更形象。Sales leveled off after the initial surge.
- **up and running** — 固定搭配，意为"正常运转中"，强调从筹备到运营的完成。The business is up and running.
- **slightly** — 修饰小幅变化，与 dramatically / significantly（大幅）对立。A slight decline 比 a small decline 更正式。
- **propose** — 在商务语境中指"提议、提案"，比 suggest 更正式。propose a plan 是固定搭配，不说 propose to do a plan。
- **stock market** — 注意不说 stocks market。相关表达：stock price（股价）、stock exchange（证券交易所）。
- **by the end of** — 强调截止时间点，常与将来完成时搭配：By the end of this year, we will have doubled our profit.
