# Unit 057: 在商店

> 在商场购物时与人交流

## 单词

ad, cash, cent, close, closed, credit card, entrance, for, get to, got, here, how much, how often, kinds, kinds of, number, on, open, opened, saw, spend, spent, store, take, taxi, to, too many, took, why, with, would like to, would you like to

## 单元讲解

本单元设定在商场或商店购物的场景中，你将学会询问价格、付款方式、营业时间等实用对话。例如 "How much is this?" "I'd like to pay with a credit card" 或 "The store opened at nine"。

语法上混合了过去时（got, saw, spent, took, opened）和礼貌请求结构 would like to / would you like to。how much 问价格，how often 问频率，too many 修饰可数名词复数表示"太多"。

词汇分为：购物与支付（cash, cent, credit card, spend, spent, how much）、商店相关（store, entrance, open, closed, ad, kinds of）、交通与移动（get to, taxi, take, here, to）。学完本单元，你能在英语环境中完成一次完整的购物交流，从问价到付款。

## Tips

- **would like to** — 比 want 更礼貌的表达，I would like to buy... 适合对店员使用
- **credit card / cash** — 两种主要付款方式，pay by credit card 或 pay in cash，介词不同
- **how often** — 问频率"多久一次"，区别于 how much（价格/不可数量）和 how many（可数量）
- **too many** — 修饰可数名词复数（too many bags），too much 修饰不可数名词（too much money）
- **saw** — see 的过去式，see → saw → seen；saw 也可作名词"锯子"
- **entrance** — enter 的名词形式，反义词是 exit；注意重音在第一个音节 /ˈentrəns/
- **kinds of** — "各种各样的"，a kind of 单数，all kinds of 复数，口语中常替代 types of
