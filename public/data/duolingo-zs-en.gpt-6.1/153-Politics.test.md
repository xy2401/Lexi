# Politics练习

<practice unit="153" format="2">

标题：谈公共话题并保留不确定性
辅助词：writer, tomorrow, meeting, issues, teacher, shouting, said, wait

## 学习目标

- topics | 公共话题词 | 辨认信息、人物与议题。
- possibility | 可能与否定 | 表达可能有或可能没有。
- meaning | 解释与回应 | 使用意思、过去义务和强烈反应。

<exercise id="u153-possibility-08" type="choice" activity="word-choice" goal="possibility" stage="recognize">

目标词：there could be, there might be

### 题目

There could be a meeting. 与 There might be a meeting. 共同表达什么？

### 选项

- [a] 可能有会议
- [b] 确定没有会议
- [c] 必定有会议

### 答案

a

### 选项反馈

- [a] 两者都保留不确定性。
- [b] 没有否定，也没有确定判断。
- [c] 可能不等于必定。

### 提示

- 情态表达保留可能性。

### 讲解

could be / might be 不对应本课规定的固定概率。

</exercise>

<exercise id="u153-meaning-16" type="choice" activity="word-choice" goal="meaning" stage="recognize">

### 选项

- [a] We didn't have to wait.
- [b] We didn't had to wait.
- [c] We not had to wait.

### 答案

### 讲解

did not have to 表示过去不必。
