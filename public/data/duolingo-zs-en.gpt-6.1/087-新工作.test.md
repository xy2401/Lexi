# 新工作练习

<practice unit="87" format="2">

标题：求职交流、许可请求与工作经历
辅助词：please, in, this, I, use, my, the, will, you, now, I'm, looking, a, job, look, want, to, she, write, it, been, busy, day, worked, here, May, interview, does, year, some

## 学习目标

- request | 礼貌见面与请求许可 | 使用 pleased to meet you、is it OK if / to、certainly 与表格简历。
- experience | 讲述工作经历与时间 | 使用 have / has written、just、how long / since / before / last。
- workplace | 说明岗位与共事 | 认识部门职位，使用 look for、get along、advice 与祝福。

<exercise id="u087-request-01" type="matching" activity="matching" goal="request" stage="recognize">

目标词：pleased to meet you, is it ok if, is it ok to, certainly, form, fill

### 题目

把英文与对应的中文含义逐一配对。

### 配对

- [p1] pleased to meet you | 很高兴认识你
- [p2] is it OK if | 询问许可（后接完整句）
- [p3] is it OK to | 询问许可（后接原形动作）
- [p4] certainly | 当然／可以
- [p5] form | 表格
- [p6] fill | 填

### 提示

- 两种许可结构后面的内容不同。

### 讲解

fill in this form 是填写表格。

</exercise>

<exercise id="u087-experience-08" type="matching" activity="matching" goal="experience" stage="recognize">

目标词：have, has, written, just, it has

### 题目

把英文与对应的中文含义逐一配对。

### 配对

- [p1] have | 完成时助动词（I / you 等主语）
- [p2] has | 完成时助动词（she / it 等主语）
- [p3] written | write 的过去分词
- [p4] just | 刚刚（本题）
- [p5] it has | it 后接 has 的组合

### 提示

- 主语决定 have / has，后接过去分词。

### 讲解

I have written；She has written。

</exercise>

<exercise id="u087-experience-09" type="choice" activity="cloze" goal="experience" stage="build">

目标词：has, written

### 题目

She ____ written a résumé.

### 选项

- [a] has
- [b] have
- [c] is

### 答案

a

### 选项反馈

- [a] she 在本课配 has。
- [b] 这里不配 have。
- [c] 本题写好了使用完成时，不用 is written 代替。

### 提示

- 检查主语与完成时结构。

### 讲解

She has written a résumé.。

</exercise>
