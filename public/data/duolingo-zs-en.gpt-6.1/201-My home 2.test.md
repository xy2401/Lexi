# My home 2练习

<practice unit="201" format="2">

标题：说清尺寸，再回想当时的选择
辅助词：I, you, she, chosen, choose, chose, smaller, this, two, twelve, wide, size, had, known, equipment, hole, dug

## 学习目标

- past-choice | 回想过去的另一选择 | 使用 would have + 过去分词与缩写。
- measure | 描述尺寸是否合适 | 区分 long、tall、宽度单位与 fit。
- objects | 识别家居物品与材料 | 理解常用物品、metal、electrical 与 dig。

<exercise id="u201-past-choice-01" type="choice" activity="word-choice" goal="past-choice" stage="recognize">

目标词：would have, i would have

### 题目

I would have chosen a smaller frame. 在回想另一种过去选择时是什么意思？

### 选项

- [a] 当时条件不同，我本会选小一点的框
- [b] 已确认我实际买了更小的框

### 答案

a

### 选项反馈

- [a] 是假设过去的结果。
- [b] 句子没有确认实际购买。

### 提示

- 观察 would have + 过去分词。

### 讲解

不是把 would have 当成普通的已经。

</exercise>

<exercise id="u201-past-choice-02" type="choice" activity="cloze" goal="past-choice" stage="build">

目标词：would have

### 题目

I would have ___ a smaller frame.

### 选项

- [a] chosen
- [b] chose

### 答案

a

### 选项反馈

- [a] would have 后接过去分词。
- [b] chose 是一般过去式。

### 提示

- choose 的三种形式不同。

### 讲解

choose → chose → chosen。

</exercise>

<exercise id="u201-past-choice-03" type="compose" activity="compose" goal="past-choice" stage="build">

have | would | I | frame | smaller | a | chosen

<exercise id="u201-past-choice-05" type="choice" activity="word-choice" goal="past-choice" stage="recognize">

目标词：would you have

### 题目

哪句正确问“你当时本会选这个框吗”？

### 选项

- [a] Would you have chosen this frame?
- [b] Would you has chosen this frame?

### 答案

a

### 选项反馈

- [a] Would 在前，have 后用 chosen。
- [b] would 后不能把 have 变为 has。

### 提示

- would 后保留原形 have。

### 讲解

疑问开头是 Would you have。

</exercise>

<exercise id="u201-past-choice-07" type="input" activity="write" goal="past-choice" stage="recall">

目标词：would have, i would have

### 题目

写出“如果我当时知道尺寸，我本会选一个小一点的框。”

### 答案

- If I had known the size, I would have chosen a smaller frame.
- If I had known the size, I would've chosen a smaller frame.

<exercise id="u201-measure-08" type="matching" activity="matching" goal="measure" stage="recognize">

目标词：long, tall, feet wide, inches wide, fit, frame

### 题目

把英文与对应的中文含义逐一配对。

### 配对

- [p1] long | 长的
- [p2] tall | 高的
- [p3] feet wide | 以英尺表达宽度的结构
- [p4] inches wide | 以英寸表达宽度的结构
- [p5] fit | 合适或尺寸能装进
- [p6] frame | 框或框架

### 提示

- 高度与宽度分开。

### 讲解

数字放在 feet / inches 前，wide 放在后。
