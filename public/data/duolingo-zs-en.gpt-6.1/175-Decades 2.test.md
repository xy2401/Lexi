# Decades 2练习

<practice unit="175" format="2">

标题：先后有序地讲述经历
辅助词：I, we, she, you, arrived, left, finished, before, lunch, year, century, nineteen, eighty-four, two, thousand, and, five, twenty, project, finishing, voice, help

## 学习目标

- timeline | 分清两段过去 | 用 had 和时间连接语表达先后。
- dates | 读年份与年代 | 区分具体年份、年龄、世纪和前一年。
- milestone | 说成就与经历 | 使用 succeed in，并区分经验、预测和识别。

<exercise id="u175-timeline-01" type="choice" activity="word-choice" goal="timeline" stage="recognize">

目标词：had, by the time

### 题目

By the time we arrived, she had left. 哪件事更早？

### 选项

- [a] 她离开
- [b] 我们到达

### 答案

a

### 选项反馈

- [a] had left 表示在到达之前已经离开。
- [b] 到达是过去参照点；离开更早。

### 提示

- 找 had + 过去分词。

### 讲解

过去完成时把离开放在到达之前。

</exercise>

<exercise id="u175-timeline-02" type="choice" activity="cloze" goal="timeline" stage="build">

目标词：hadn't

### 题目

“我们到达时，他们还没完成”：By the time we arrived, they ___ finished.

### 选项

- [a] hadn't
- [b] don't

### 答案

a

### 选项反馈

- [a] hadn't finished 是在过去参照点之前尚未完成。
- [b] don't 后接原形，不能构成这个过去完成时。

### 提示

- 需要过去完成时的否定。

### 讲解

hadn't = had not。

</exercise>

<exercise id="u175-timeline-03" type="compose" activity="compose" goal="timeline" stage="build">

目标词：by the time, she'd

### 题目

组句：“我们到达时，她已经离开了。”

### 词块

time | the | By | left | she'd | arrived, | we

### 答案

- By the time we arrived, she'd left.

### 提示

- 先放到达这个参照点，再说更早的离开。

### 讲解

she'd left = she had left；词块中的逗号保留在 arrived 后。

</exercise>

<exercise id="u175-timeline-05" type="matching" activity="matching" goal="timeline" stage="recognize">

目标词：he'd, she'd, they'd, we'd, you'd

### 题目

把英文与对应的中文含义逐一配对。

### 配对

- [p1] he'd arrived | 他当时已经到了
- [p2] she'd arrived | 她当时已经到了
- [p3] they'd arrived | 他们当时已经到了
- [p4] we'd arrived | 我们当时已经到了
- [p5] you'd arrived | 你当时已经到了

### 提示

- arrived 是过去分词，这些缩写在这里都表示 had。

### 讲解

同一个 had 不因主语改变；区分各主语。

</exercise>

<exercise id="u175-timeline-06" type="choice" activity="word-choice" goal="timeline" stage="recognize">

目标词：she'd, had

### 题目

She'd finished. / She'd help. 两个 'd 依次是什么？

### 选项

- [a] had / would
- [b] would / had

### 答案

a

### 选项反馈

- [a] finished 是过去分词；help 是原形。
- [b] 这与后面的动词形式不匹配。

### 提示

- 看缩写后面的形式。

### 讲解

'd 可代表 had 或 would，要结合动词判断。

</exercise>

<exercise id="u175-dates-09" type="choice" activity="word-choice" goal="dates" stage="recognize">

in her seventies 在谈什么？

### 选项

- [a] 她七十多岁
- [b] 二十世纪七十年代

### 答案

a

### 选项反馈

<exercise id="u175-milestone-13" type="choice" activity="word-choice" goal="milestone" stage="recognize">

目标词：at last, succeed in

### 题目

哪句表达“我们终于成功完成了项目”？

### 选项

- [a] At last, we succeeded in finishing the project.
- [b] At last, we succeeded in to finish the project.

### 答案

a

### 选项反馈

- [a] succeed in 后接 -ing。
- [b] in 后不能这样接 to finish。

### 提示

### 讲解
