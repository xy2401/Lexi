# 事件练习

<practice unit="78" format="2">

标题：过去正在做什么：经过、地点与后续动作
辅助词：you, doing, I, walking, on, my, did, stop, the, be, he, she, we, they, read, reading, wait, waiting, work, working, seven, bag, try, teacher, this, road, of, didn't, sleep, what's, water

## 学习目标

- ongoing | 描述过去正在做的事 | 使用 was / were 与 what / where，分清时点和持续时段。
- sequence | 说明后续发生的动作 | 区分 leave / left、lose / lost、stopped 与 tried to。
- details | 补充位置与经过信息 | 使用 ask、follow、地点、side 与 at all 等表达。

<exercise id="u078-ongoing-01" type="matching" activity="matching" goal="ongoing" stage="recognize">

目标词：was, were, what, where, at, at that time

### 题目

把英文与对应的中文含义逐一配对。

### 配对

- [p1] was | be 的过去形式（I / he / she 等）
- [p2] were | be 的过去形式（you / we / they 等）
- [p3] what | 什么／做什么
- [p4] where | 哪里
- [p5] at | 在某个时点（本题）
- [p6] at that time | 在那时

### 提示

- be 的形式与询问信息分别判断。

### 讲解

What were you doing? 问活动，Where…? 问地点。

</exercise>

<exercise id="u078-ongoing-02" type="choice" activity="cloze" goal="ongoing" stage="build">

目标词：were, was

### 题目

They ____ waiting at that time.

### 选项

- [a] were
- [b] was
- [c] did

### 答案

a

### 选项反馈

- [a] they 在本课配 were。
- [b] 这里不是 I / he / she。
- [c] 不能用 did 直接接 waiting 作本句谓语。

### 提示

- 检查主语，再选择过去的 be。

### 讲解

They were waiting.。

</exercise>

- [c] They were waiting.

### 答案

### 讲解
