# 在美国练习

<practice unit="51" format="2">

标题：规则过去式、旅行经历与持续时间
辅助词：you, enjoy, the, yes, we, it, what, do, along, and, a, call, visit, stay, wait, walk, watch, play, paint, travel, rain, snow, I, museum, game, she, travelled, yesterday, in, an, two, long

## 学习目标

- events | 用规则过去式讲经历 | 识别旅行、观看、等待和天气动作的过去形式。
- grammar | 使用 did 提问与否定 | did / did not / didn’t 后恢复动作原形，区分 be 的过去式。
- time | 说明过去时间与持续长度 | 使用前天、去年和 for + 时长，区分 hour、year 与 years。

目标词：called, visited, stayed, waited, walked, watched

### 题目

把英文与对应的中文含义逐一配对。

### 配对

- [p1] called | 打了电话（call 的过去式）
- [p2] visited | 参观／看望了
- [p3] stayed | 停留／住了
- [p4] waited | 等待了
- [p5] walked | 走了
- [p6] watched | 观看了

### 提示

- 等待 waited 与观看 watched 注意中间字母。

### 讲解

这些过去式用于肯定陈述，不与 did 重复变化。

<exercise id="u051-grammar-05" type="choice" activity="cloze" goal="grammar" stage="build">

目标词：did, visited

### 题目

Did you ____ the museum?

### 选项

- [a] visit
- [b] visited
- [c] visiting

### 答案

a

### 选项反馈

- [a] did 后使用原形。
- [b] 过去信息已在 did 中，不重复加 -ed。
- [c] 本句不使用 -ing。

### 提示

- 过去助动词与动作不重复变化。

### 讲解

Did you visit…?。

<exercise id="u051-grammar-08" type="choice" activity="word-choice" goal="grammar" stage="recognize">

### 选项

- [a] It did not rain yesterday.
- [b] It did not rained yesterday.
- [c] It was rained yesterday.

### 答案
