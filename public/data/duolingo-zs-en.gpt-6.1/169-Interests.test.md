标题：运动习惯、持续时间与休闲爱好
辅助词：jogging, practicing, moved, Monday, before, poem, coins, reading

## 学习目标

- duration | 持续多久 | 使用 been、起点和持续时间问句。
- habits | 运动与频率 | 辨认活动并比较频率。
- leisure | 休闲与接话 | 介绍其他爱好并补充信息。

<exercise id="u169-duration-01" type="choice" activity="word-choice" goal="duration" stage="recognize">

目标词：have been, has been

### 题目

主语 She 的持续活动表达是哪句？

### 选项

- [a] She have been cycling.
- [b] She has been cycling.
- [c] She has been cycle.

### 答案

b

### 选项反馈

- [a] she 用 has。
- [b] has been 后接 cycling。
- [c] been 后的活动需要 -ing。

### 提示

- 先看主语，再看动作形式。

### 讲解

has been cycling。

</exercise>

<exercise id="u169-habits-09" type="choice" activity="word-choice" goal="habits" stage="recognize">

目标词：more often, less often

### 题目

比以前做得更频繁、比以前做得更少，分别用什么？

### 选项

- [a] more often；less often
- [b] less often；more often
- [c] how long；ever since

### 答案

a

### 选项反馈

- [a] more 增加频率，less 减少频率。
- [b] 顺序反了。
- [c] 这两个说时间长度和起点。

### 提示

- 次数频率与持续时间不是一个问题。

### 讲解

more often / less often。

</exercise>
