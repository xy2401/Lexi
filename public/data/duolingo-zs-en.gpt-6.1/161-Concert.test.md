# Concert练习

<practice unit="161" format="2">

标题：分享音乐偏好并回顾表演
辅助词：music, guitar, sang, song, concert, fee, book, tickets, theater, sold

## 学习目标

- preferences | 偏好与问法 | 区分现在、过去及否定助动词。
- performance | 表演与词形 | 描述音乐表现并使用 sung。
- venue | 入场与场地 | 辨认座位位置和订票表达。

<exercise id="u161-preferences-01" type="choice" activity="word-choice" goal="preferences" stage="recognize">

目标词：do, does

### 题目

询问“她弹吉他吗？”哪个现在问句正确？

### 选项

- [a] Do she play the guitar?
- [b] Does she play the guitar?
- [c] Does she plays the guitar?

### 答案

b

### 选项反馈

- [a] she 的现在一般问句用 does。
- [b] does 后是 play 原形。
- [c] does 后不再用 plays。

### 提示

- 先看主语，再检查动词原形。

### 讲解

Does she play the guitar?

</exercise>

<exercise id="u161-preferences-05" type="choice" activity="word-choice" goal="preferences" stage="recognize">

目标词：did, didn't, sing

### 题目

过去询问她有没有唱歌、回答没唱，哪组正确？

### 选项

- [a] Did she sang? She didn't sang.
- [b] Did she sing? She didn't sing.
- [c] Does she sang? She don't sing.

### 答案

b

### 选项反馈

- [a] did 与 didn’t 后都要原形。
- [b] 两处都用 sing 原形。
- [c] 时间和主语搭配都有错误。

### 提示

- 过去助动词承担时间变化。

### 讲解

Did she sing? / She didn't sing。

</exercise>

<exercise id="u161-venue-13" type="choice" activity="word-choice" goal="venue" stage="recognize">

目标词：in advance

### 题目

book tickets in advance 表示什么？

### 选项

- [a] 提前订票
- [b] 只在音乐会结束后买票
- [c] 在后排唱歌

### 答案

a

### 选项反馈

- [a] in advance 是提前。
- [b] 这与提前相反。
- [c] 不是订票时间的含义。

### 提示

- 注意动作是 book，词组说明时间。

### 讲解

in advance 不表示具体提前多少天。

</exercise>
