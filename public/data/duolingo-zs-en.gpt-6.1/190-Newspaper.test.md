# Newspaper练习

<practice unit="190" format="2">

标题：读清数量与变化，转述近期事件
辅助词：there, some, any, a, an, changes, reports, events, plan, two, towns, video, she, number, ten, twelve

## 学习目标

- been | 说发生了什么 | 按单复数使用 there has / have been 及否定疑问。
- change | 读懂变化和范围 | 区分 increase、decrease、improvement 与 most of。
- events | 识别事件词与语境 | 理解新闻动作，不从单词推断未确认事实。

<exercise id="u190-been-01" type="choice" activity="cloze" goal="been" stage="build">

目标词：there has been, damage

### 题目

There ___ been some damage.

### 选项

- [a] has
- [b] have

### 答案

a

### 选项反馈

- [a] damage 是不可数名词。
- [b] 这里没有复数名词主语。

### 提示

- 看 damage 的可数性。

### 讲解

There has been some damage. 是正确结构。

</exercise>

- [p1] there has been | 单数或不可数的肯定结构

- [p3] there hasn't been | 单数或不可数的否定缩写结构
