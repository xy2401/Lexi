# Mistakes练习

<practice unit="202" format="2">

标题：表达遗憾，读清过去假设
辅助词：I, you, her, known, left, earlier, more, time, promised, explain, map, tea, will, help, delay, feel

## 学习目标

- regret | 表达过去的另一可能 | 连接 if + had 与 would have / wouldn’t have。
- forms | 认出过去分词 | 区分 drawn、drunk 与 would have had 的结构。
- impact | 说明错误与影响 | 区分行为、情绪、警告和评价词。

<exercise id="u202-regret-01" type="choice" activity="word-choice" goal="regret" stage="recognize">

目标词：if, had, wouldn't have, promise

### 题目

If I had known, I wouldn't have promised. 在已说明当时不知情的情境中是什么意思？

### 选项

- [a] 如果当时知道，就不会作承诺
- [b] 已经知道今后绝不会再承诺

### 答案

a

### 选项反馈

- [a] 回想过去未实现的另一种情况。
- [b] 句子不保证未来永远不承诺。

### 提示

- 注意过去条件和结果。

### 讲解

would not have promised 指过去假设的结果。

</exercise>

<exercise id="u202-regret-02" type="compose" activity="compose" goal="regret" stage="build">

目标词：if, had, wouldn't have

### 题目

组句：“如果我当时知道，我就不会承诺了。”

### 词块

I | If | promised | have | wouldn't | I | known, | had

### 答案

- If I had known, I wouldn't have promised.

### 提示

- 条件句用 had known；结果句用 would not have。

### 讲解

known 与 promised 都是过去分词。

</exercise>

### 答案

### 答案

<exercise id="u202-forms-06" type="choice" activity="word-choice" goal="forms" stage="recognize">

目标词：would have had

### 题目

I would have had more time. 为什么末尾还有 had？

### 选项

- [a] 最后的 had 是 have 的过去分词，表示有
- [b] 这是重复词，必须删除

### 答案

a

### 选项反馈

- [a] 前面的 have 用于完成结构。
- [b] 删掉会改变过去假设的结构与意思。

### 提示

- 区分助动词 have 与主动词 have。

### 讲解

would + have + had 结构完整。

</exercise>

<exercise id="u202-forms-07" type="compose" activity="compose" goal="forms" stage="build">

目标词：had, would have had

### 题目

组句：“如果我当时早点出发，我本会有更多时间。”

### 词块

would | I | earlier, | left | had | I | If | time | more | had | have

### 答案

- If I had left earlier, I would have had more time.

### 提示

- left earlier 后保留逗号。

### 讲解

两个分句分别使用 had left 和 would have had。

</exercise>

<exercise id="u202-forms-08" type="input" activity="write" goal="forms" stage="recall">

目标词：would have had

### 题目

写出“我当时本会有更多时间。”

### 答案

- I would have had more time.
- I would've had more time.

### 提示

- 不要漏掉主动词 have 的过去分词。

### 讲解

最后的 had 表“有”。

</exercise>

<exercise id="u202-forms-09" type="matching" activity="matching" goal="forms" stage="recognize">

目标词：drawn, drunk, had, hadn't

### 题目

把英文与对应的中文含义逐一配对。

### 配对

- [p1] drawn | draw 的过去分词
- [p2] drunk | drink 的过去分词
- [p3] had drawn | 当时已经画好的结构
- [p4] hadn't drunk | 当时还没有喝的否定结构

### 提示

- drew / drank 是过去式，drawn / drunk 是过去分词。

### 讲解

drunk 在其他结构也可作形容词表示醉的。

</exercise>

<exercise id="u202-forms-10" type="choice" activity="cloze" goal="forms" stage="build">

### 答案

### 讲解

<exercise id="u202-forms-11" type="input" activity="write" goal="forms" stage="recall">

<exercise id="u202-impact-15" type="matching" activity="matching" goal="impact" stage="recognize">

目标词：guilty, mistaken, selfish, stupid, lucky, common sense

### 题目

把英文与对应的中文含义逐一配对。

### 配对

- [p1] guilty | 内疚的，或法律语境中的有罪的
- [p2] mistaken | 弄错或误解的
- [p3] selfish | 自私的
- [p4] stupid | 愚蠢的，可能带冒犯

### 讲解
