# Unit 092: 跳蚤市场

> 在二手市场购物

## 单词

a little, all kinds of, are, aren't, badly, buy one get one free, cap, collect, come in, deal, discount, discount on, flea market, furniture, gold, guess what, half-price, is, jewelry, leather, might, mug, one, poster, real, second-hand, silver, sneaker, sound, sunglasses, well, wool

## 单元讲解

本单元围绕"在二手市场购物"展开。核心句型包括：Guess what I found at the flea market!（猜猜我在跳蚤市场发现了什么！）、It's a great deal!（太划算了！）、Is this real gold?（这是真金吗？）、There's a discount on furniture.（家具有折扣。）、Buy one get one free!（买一送一！）。

词表里有材质词（gold, silver, leather, wool）和物品（cap, mug, poster, sneaker, sunglasses, jewelry, furniture）。你可以练习：I collect old posters. / These sneakers are second-hand. / All kinds of things are half-price. / That sounds like a good deal.

second-hand 表示"二手的"。might 表示"可能"。

## Tips

- **flea market** — 跳蚤市场。搭配：at the flea market, go to the flea market。例句：I found this at the flea market. / Let's go to the flea market! 注意 flea 是"跳蚤"，flea market 是卖二手货的露天市场。

- **second-hand** — 二手的。搭配：second-hand furniture, second-hand clothes, buy second-hand。例句：These sneakers are second-hand. / I bought a second-hand sofa. 注意 second-hand = used（用过的）。

- **deal / discount** — 交易/划算 / 折扣。搭配：a great deal, a good deal, a discount on, 50% discount。例句：It's a great deal! / There's a discount on jewelry. 注意 deal 在口语中表示"划算的交易"。

- **buy one get one free** — 买一送一。搭配：buy one get one free, BOGO（缩写）。例句：The mugs are buy one get one free! 注意这是促销用语，缩写 BOGO 在美式广告中常见。

- **half-price** — 半价。搭配：half-price, at half price, half-price sale。例句：Everything is half-price today! / I got it at half price. 注意 half-price 是形容词，at half price 是副词短语。

- **gold / silver / leather / wool** — 金 / 银 / 皮革 / 羊毛。搭配：real gold, silver jewelry, a leather cap, a wool sweater。例句：Is this real gold? / The bag is leather. 注意这些既是名词也是形容词。

- **real** — 真的。搭配：real gold, real leather, Is it real? 例句：Is this real silver? / It's not real gold. 注意 real 和 fake（假的）是反义词。really 是副词。

- **collect** — 收藏。搭配：collect posters, collect coins, I collect...。例句：I collect old mugs. / She collects vintage jewelry. 注意 collect 是动词，collection 是名词。

- **all kinds of** — 各种各样的。搭配：all kinds of things, all kinds of furniture。例句：They sell all kinds of things at the flea market. 注意 all kinds of = various = many different types of。

- **sneaker / sunglasses / cap** — 运动鞋 / 太阳镜 / 帽子。搭配：a pair of sneakers, wear sunglasses, a baseball cap。注意 sneaker 通常用复数（a pair of sneakers）。sunglasses 总是复数。

- **might / sound** — 可能 / 听起来。搭配：It might be real. / That sounds great! 例句：This might be real gold! / That sounds like a good deal. 注意 might 表示可能性（比 may 更不确定）。

- **guess what** — 猜猜看。搭配：Guess what! / Guess what I found! 例句：Guess what! I found a real leather bag! / Guess what I got at the flea market! 注意这是口语中引起注意的开头语。
