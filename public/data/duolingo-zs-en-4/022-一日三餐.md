# Unit 022: 一日三餐

> 讲述你想要的食物和饮料

## 单词

beef, beer, cake, cheese, chicken, dinner, fish, food, for, fruit, glasses of, have, hot pot, kind of, line, lunch, make, makes, meat, not, pork, race, thank you for, what would you like, wine, would like to, would you like, would you like to

## 单元讲解

本单元围绕"一日三餐"展开，词表覆盖了三种正餐（breakfast 没出现但有 lunch, dinner）和大量食物词（beef, cake, cheese, chicken, fish, fruit, meat, pork, hot pot）。核心句型是 What would you like? / I would like some chicken. / Would you like to have dinner with me?

would like to 和 would you like to 的出现标志着从"想要某物"升级到"想要做某事"：I would like to have lunch. Would you like to eat hot pot? 这是邀请和计划的常用结构。

make / makes 引入了"做饭"的表达：She makes dinner every day. hot pot 是火锅——一个直接来自中文饮食文化的词。thank you for 提供了感谢结构：Thank you for the food!

## Tips

- **would like to / would you like to** — 想要做某事 / 您想做某事吗？I'd like to have dinner. / Would you like to eat together? 这是比 want to 礼貌得多的表达，特别适合邀请和请求。缩写：I'd like to, She'd like to。

- **what would you like** — 询问对方想要什么（礼貌）：What would you like to eat? / What would you like to drink? 服务场景中的标准问法。回答：I'd like some fish, please.

- **lunch / dinner** — 午餐 / 晚餐（或正餐）。搭配：have lunch, have dinner, for lunch / for dinner。注意 dinner 在英式英语中有时指中午的正餐（尤其是英国北部），supper 才是晚饭。但在国际英语中 dinner = 晚饭 是最通用的理解。

- **food** — 食物（总称），不可数：some food, a lot of food。搭配：Chinese food, fast food, food and drink。注意 food 和 meal 的区别：food 是"食物"（材料），meal 是"一顿饭"（事件）。

- **beef / pork / chicken / fish / meat** — 肉类词汇。meat 是总称（不可数），其余是具体种类。注意 fish 既是"鱼"也是"鱼肉"，单复数同形（one fish, two fish）。搭配：a piece of meat, grilled fish, fried chicken。

- **hot pot** — 火锅。这是一个从中文饮食文化直接进入英语的词。在英语中通常写作 hot pot 或 hotpot。类似的还有 dim sum（点心）、tofu（豆腐）。

- **make / makes** — 做（饭）。搭配：make dinner, make a cake, make breakfast。注意 make 和 cook 的区别：make 更通用（make a sandwich 不一定用火），cook 强调加热烹饪。三单 makes：She makes lunch every day.

- **wine / beer** — 葡萄酒 / 啤酒。wine 通常不可数：a glass of wine, red wine, white wine。beer 也通常不可数：a bottle of beer, two beers（口语中可数化表份数）。搭配：drink wine, a wine glass。

- **kind of** — 种类。搭配：What kind of food do you like? / all kinds of meat。口语中 kind of 还可以表示"有点"（= sort of）：I'm kind of hungry.（我有点饿。）

- **thank you for** — 因为……而感谢：Thank you for the food! / Thank you for helping me. 注意 for 后面接名词或动名词：Thank you for coming.（不是 ✗ Thank you for come.）

- **cake** — 蛋糕。可数也可不可数：a cake（一个完整的蛋糕）、some cake（一些蛋糕）。搭配：birthday cake, a piece of cake。习语 a piece of cake = 小菜一碟（非常容易）。

- **have** — 在这里是"吃/喝"的意思：have lunch, have a beer, have some fruit。比 eat/drink 更通用、更口语化。搭配极广：have breakfast / dinner / a snack / a cup of tea。
