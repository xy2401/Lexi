# Unit 066: 故事

> 分享惊险的经历

## 单词

a long time ago, accident, afraid, anyone, bear, break, broke, careful, cloud, crazy, die, drove, finally, fire, first, go away, happen, happen to, hit, hurt, left, more, next, oh no, police, pool, rode, story, swam, tell, thunderstorm, told, went away

## 单元讲解

本单元围绕"分享惊险的经历"展开。词表里有大量不规则过去式：broke（break）, drove（drive）, rode（ride）, swam（swim）, told（tell）, hit（hit，过去式不变）, hurt（hurt，过去式不变）。你可以练习：A bear broke into our camp! / We drove away quickly. / I swam across the lake. / She told me a crazy story.

happen to 表示"发生在……身上"：What happened to you? / The accident happened to my friend. finally 表示"最终"。afraid 表示"害怕的"。

go away / went away 表示"离开、走开"。oh no 是惊叹语。

## Tips

- **broke / break** — 打破了、闯入了。break → broke → broken。搭配：broke a window, break into（闯入）、break down（坏了）。例句：The bear broke into our tent. 注意 break 的用法非常多。

- **drove / drive** — 开车了。drive → drove → driven。搭配：drove home, drove away, drive carefully。例句：We drove away as fast as we could. 注意 drive 的过去式是 drove（不是 ✗ drived）。

- **rode / ride** — 骑了。ride → rode → ridden。搭配：rode a horse, rode a bike, go for a ride。例句：She rode her bike to the lake. 注意 ride 的过去式是 rode（不是 ✗ rided）。

- **swam / swim** — 游泳了。swim → swam → swum。搭配：swam across the river, go swimming。例句：He swam in the pool. 注意三个形式都不同：swim, swam, swum。

- **told / tell** — 告诉了。tell → told → told。搭配：told me a story, told the police, tell the truth。例句：She told us a crazy story. 注意 tell 和 say 的区别：tell + 人，say + 内容。

- **hit / hurt** — 撞了 / 受伤了。过去式不变：hit → hit → hit, hurt → hurt → hurt。搭配：hit a tree, hurt my leg。例句：The car hit a bear. / I hurt my arm. 注意这类动词过去式与原形相同。

- **happen / happen to** — 发生。搭配：What happened? / happen to someone, happen to do。例句：What happened to you? / The accident happened yesterday. 注意 happen 没有进行时。

- **afraid** — 害怕的。搭配：be afraid of, I'm afraid so / I'm afraid not。例句：I'm afraid of bears. / She was afraid to move. 注意 afraid 只能作表语（不说 ✗ an afraid girl → ✓ a frightened girl）。

- **a long time ago** — 很久以前。搭配：A long time ago, there was... 例句：A long time ago, I lived near a forest. 注意这是讲故事的经典开头。ago 用于一般过去时。

- **finally** — 最终、终于。搭配：finally arrived, finally ended, and finally。例句：Finally, the police came. 注意 finally 通常放在句首或动词前：He finally stopped.

- **thunderstorm** — 雷暴。构词：thunder（雷）+ storm（暴风雨）。搭配：a thunderstorm, during the thunderstorm。注意 thunder 是雷声，lightning 是闪电。

- **careful / crazy** — 小心的 / 疯狂的。搭配：be careful! / a crazy story, go crazy。例句：Be careful of the bear! / That's a crazy story! 注意 careful 的反义词是 careless（粗心的）。
