# Unit 104: 新家

> 讨论买一个新房子

## 单词

armchair, away, bedroom, bill, block, bloom, built, center, eighty, electricity, enter, fence, fill, first of all, fixed, go in, look, move in, move out, neighborhood, nineteen, ninety, owner, painted, piece, playground, pound, rent, roof, shut, sold, space, stranger, wood, yard

## 单元讲解

本单元围绕"讨论买一个新房子"展开。核心是房屋相关表达：First of all, the house was built in 1990.（首先，这房子建于1990年。）、We need to move in before the electricity bill is due.（我们得在电费到期前搬进去。）、The owner sold the house for eighty thousand pounds.（房主以八万英镑卖掉了房子。）、There's a yard with a fence and a playground nearby.（有个带围栏的院子，附近有游乐场。）。

词表里有房屋词汇（roof, fence, yard, bedroom, space）和交易词汇（owner, sold, rent, bill, pound）。你可以练习：The roof was fixed last year. / A stranger painted the fence. / The neighborhood is nice. / Let's go in and look at the space.

first of all 表示"首先"。move in / move out 表示"搬入/搬出"。

## Tips

- **move in / move out** — 搬入 / 搬出。搭配：move in next week, move out of the apartment。例句：We move in on Saturday. / The old owner moved out. 注意 move in 后面不加 to（直接说 move in）。

- **owner / sold / rent** — 房主 / 卖了 / 租。搭配：the owner of the house, sold for..., rent a house。例句：The owner sold the house. / We rent a flat nearby. 注意 sell → sold → sold。

- **built / fixed / painted** — 建造 / 修理 / 粉刷。搭配：built in 1990, fixed the roof, painted the wall。例句：The house was built in 1990. / He fixed the roof. / We painted the bedroom. 注意这些都是过去分词（被动语态常用）。

- **roof / fence / yard** — 屋顶 / 围栏 / 院子。搭配：the roof leaks, a wooden fence, in the yard。例句：The roof needs fixing. / There's a fence around the yard. / The kids play in the yard.

- **electricity / bill** — 电 / 账单。搭配：the electricity bill, pay the bill, electricity costs。例句：The electricity bill is high. / We need to pay the bill. 注意 bill 可指各种账单（电费、水费、电话费）。

- **neighborhood / playground** — 社区 / 游乐场。搭配：a nice neighborhood, in the neighborhood, at the playground。例句：The neighborhood is quiet. / There's a playground for the kids. 注意 neighborhood 指"附近一带/社区"。

- **first of all / enter / go in** — 首先 / 进入。搭配：First of all, ..., enter the house, go in。例句：First of all, let's look at the kitchen. / Go in and have a look. 注意 enter 较正式，go in 更口语。

- **space / room / bedroom** — 空间 / 房间 / 卧室。搭配：enough space, a big space, two bedrooms。例句：There's a lot of space. / The bedroom is upstairs. 注意 space 不可数（不说 ✗ a space）。

- **eighty / nineteen / ninety** — 80 / 19 / 90。搭配：eighty thousand, nineteen years, ninety pounds。例句：It costs eighty thousand pounds. / The house is nineteen years old. 注意大数字：eighty thousand = 80,000。

- **wood / piece / block** — 木头 / 一块 / 街区。搭配：made of wood, a piece of furniture, two blocks away。例句：The fence is made of wood. / The shop is two blocks away. 注意 block 在美式英语中指"街区"。

- **stranger / shut** — 陌生人 / 关上。搭配：a stranger, shut the door, shut the window。例句：A stranger came to the door. / Shut the window—it's cold. 注意 shut = close（更口语）。

- **bloom / center / away** — 开花 / 中心 / 离开。搭配：flowers bloom, in the center, far away。例句：The flowers bloom in spring. / The house is in the center. / The station is far away.
