# Unit 032: 活动 2

> 讲述正在进行的活动

## 单词

about, boxes, came, cleaning, cook, cooking, doing, drinking, eating, food, him, japanese, light, needs, newspaper, playing, reading, right now, same, shower, talk about, talk to, talking, talking about, talking to, teach, thought, washing, watching, we're

## 单元讲解

本单元围绕"讲述正在进行的活动"展开，核心语法是现在进行时：be + 动词-ing。词表里有大量 -ing 形式：cleaning, cooking, doing, drinking, eating, playing, reading, talking, washing, watching。你可以练习：I'm reading. She's cooking. They're watching TV. We're eating dinner.

right now 强调"此刻"：What are you doing right now? I'm reading a newspaper. we're = we are，是现在进行时的主语 + be 缩写。

talk about / talk to / talking about / talking to 提供了"谈论"的两种搭配：talk to someone（和某人说话）、talk about something（谈论某事）。him 是 he 的宾格。

## Tips

- **现在进行时** — be (am/is/are) + 动词-ing，表示正在进行的动作：I'm reading. She's cooking. They're playing. 搭配时间词：right now, now, at the moment。注意不是所有动词都能用进行时——like, know, want 等状态动词通常不用：✗ I'm liking this → ✓ I like this。

- **we're** — we are 的缩写：We're eating. / We're watching TV. 现在进行时中非常常用。注意和 were（are 的过去式）的发音区别：we're /wɪr/, were /wɜːr/。

- **right now** — 现在、此刻。比 now 更强调"就在此刻"：What are you doing right now? 也可以表示"立刻"：I need it right now! 类似的：at the moment, at this moment。

- **cooking / cleaning / washing** — 家务/厨房活动的 -ing 形式。搭配：cook dinner → cooking dinner, clean the room → cleaning the room, wash the dishes → washing the dishes。注意 cook 的 -ing 直接加：cooking（不双写 k）。

- **reading / watching / playing** — 休闲活动的 -ing 形式。搭配：read a newspaper → reading a newspaper, watch TV → watching TV, play a game → playing a game。

- **talk to / talk about** — 和某人说话 / 谈论某事。搭配：I'm talking to my friend. / We're talking about food. 注意 talk to 和 talk with 都可以（to 更常见）。talk about + 话题。

- **him** — he 的宾格。放在动词或介词后：I like him. / I'm talking to him. 注意主格 he 和宾格 him 的区别：He likes me. / I like him. 不能混用。

- **newspaper** — 报纸。搭配：read a newspaper, in the newspaper。注意 news（新闻，不可数）和 newspaper（报纸，可数）的区别：I read the news in the newspaper。

- **same** — 相同的。搭配：the same（前面通常加 the）：We have the same book. / I think the same. 反义词 different。注意 same 前几乎总是有 the 或 this/that。

- **came** — come 的过去式：She came home. / He came to the party. 注意 come - came - come 的三态。搭配：come from, come here, come in。

- **thought** — think 的过去式，也可以作名词"想法"。动词：I thought it was good.（我原以为……）名词：a good thought, I had a thought. 注意 thought 和 though（虽然）的拼写区别。

- **shower** — 淋浴。搭配：take a shower（美式）/ have a shower（英式）。注意和 bath 的区别：shower 是站着冲水，bath 是泡在浴缸里。
