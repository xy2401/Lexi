# Unit 070: 旅行 3

> 制定交通出行计划

## 单词

airplane, arriving, backpack, bags, carry, coming, driving, elevator, far, flight, flying, from, gate, get lost, get off, get on, going, got lost, hall, how far, in, international, leaving, miss, next, on, pack, restroom, take, through, tour, way

## 单元讲解

本单元围绕"制定交通出行计划"展开。核心句型包括：How far is the airport?（机场多远？）、What time is your flight?（你的航班几点？）、Don't miss your flight!（别误了航班！）、Where is the gate?（登机口在哪？）。

词表里有旅行词汇（airplane, flight, gate, backpack, tour）和交通动作（get on, get off, carry, pack, driving, flying）。你可以练习：I'm flying to Paris. / We got lost on the way. / Get on the elevator. / I packed my bags.

get lost / got lost 表示"迷路"。how far 问距离。international 表示"国际的"。

## Tips

- **flight** — 航班。搭配：take a flight, a direct flight, an international flight, miss a flight。例句：My flight leaves at nine. / Don't miss your flight! 注意 flight 来自 fly。

- **gate** — 登机口、大门。搭配：Gate 12, at the gate, go to the gate。例句：Your gate is number five. / Go to Gate 12. 注意机场的 gate 是登机口，和一般的 gate（大门）不同。

- **get on / get off** — 上（交通工具）/ 下（交通工具）。搭配：get on the bus, get off the train, get on the elevator。注意 get on / off 用于大型交通工具（bus, train, plane）。小汽车用 get in / out of。

- **get lost / got lost** — 迷路。搭配：get lost, got lost on the way, don't get lost。例句：We got lost in the city. / I always get lost. 注意 get lost 是"迷路"（动作），be lost 是"迷路了"（状态）。

- **how far** — 多远（距离）。搭配：How far is it? / How far is the airport? 回答：Two miles. / About ten minutes by car. 注意 how far 问距离，how long 问时间长度。

- **pack** — 打包、收拾行李。搭配：pack my bags, pack a backpack, packed and ready。例句：I packed my bags last night. / Have you packed yet? 注意 pack 和 unpack（打开行李）对应。

- **airplane / flying** — 飞机 / 飞行。搭配：by airplane, take an airplane, flying to Paris。注意 airplane 是美式（英式用 aeroplane / plane）。by airplane = by plane = by air。

- **backpack / bags** — 背包 / 行李。搭配：carry a backpack, pack my bags, carry-on bags。注意 bags 在旅行语境中泛指行李（= luggage）。backpack 是双肩背包。

- **miss** — 错过、想念。搭配：miss the flight, miss the bus, miss you。例句：Don't miss your flight! / I missed the train. 注意 miss 的"错过"和"想念"两个意思都很常用。

- **international** — 国际的。搭配：an international flight, international airport, international travel。反义词 domestic（国内的）。注意 international 的发音：/ˌɪntərˈnæʃnl/。

- **restroom** — 洗手间（美式）。搭配：Where is the restroom? / a public restroom。注意美式 restroom = 英式 toilet / loo。在公共场合美式用 restroom 更礼貌。

- **through / way** — 穿过 / 路。搭配：go through, on the way, this way, the way to。例句：Go through the hall. / It's on the way. / Which way? 注意 on the way = 在路上/顺路。
