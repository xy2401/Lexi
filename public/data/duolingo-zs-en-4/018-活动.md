# Unit 018: 活动

> 谈论日常活动

## 单词

at school, baseball, basketball, cook, cooks, does, does not, doesn't, draw, evening, every, fine, lead, like to, likes to, morning, movie, music, noodle, play, read, sing, smart, sometimes, song, speaks, ten, tennis, time, tv, uses, usually, watch

## 单元讲解

本单元围绕"谈论日常活动"展开，词表里有大量动词：cook, draw, play, read, sing, watch，加上运动/娱乐名词（baseball, basketball, tennis, movie, music, tv, song），可以练习：I play basketball. She reads every evening. He likes to watch TV.

语法上，does / doesn't 的出现标志着三单疑问句和否定句：Does she cook? / He doesn't sing. 加上频率副词 usually / sometimes / every，可以描述日常习惯：I usually read in the evening. She sometimes plays tennis.

like to / likes to 引出了"喜欢做某事"的结构：I like to play basketball. She likes to cook. 注意 like to do 和 like doing 都对，意思基本相同。

## Tips

- **does / doesn't** — do 的三单形式，用于 he/she/it 的疑问句和否定句：Does he play tennis? / She doesn't cook. 注意：用了 does/doesn't 后，主动词恢复原形：✗ Does he plays? → ✓ Does he play? ✗ She doesn't cooks → ✓ She doesn't cook.

- **play** — 搭配球类运动和乐器：play basketball, play tennis, play the guitar（注意乐器前加 the）。也可以泛指"玩"：Let's play! 搭配：play a game, play with friends。

- **watch / read** — watch 用于屏幕类：watch TV, watch a movie。read 用于文字类：read a book, read a newspaper。注意不说 ✗ read TV 或 ✗ watch a book。

- **cook / cooks** — 做饭。三单 cooks。名词用法：a good cook（一个好厨师）——注意"厨师"是 cook 不是 ✗ cooker（cooker 是炊具）。搭配：cook dinner, cook for my family。

- **sing** — 唱歌。三态：sing - sang - sung。搭配：sing a song, sing in the shower。名词 singer（歌手）。注意和 song 的关系：sing 是动词，song 是名词（唱的歌）。

- **draw** — 画画。三态：draw - drew - drawn。搭配：draw a picture, draw a map。注意 draw 还有"拉、拖"的意思：draw a curtain（拉窗帘）。

- **usually / sometimes** — 频率副词。usually = 通常（约80%），sometimes = 有时（约30%）。位置：be 动词后，实义动词前：I usually get up at seven. / She is sometimes late.

- **like to / likes to** — 喜欢做某事。I like to read. / She likes to cook. 也可以用 like doing：I like reading. 两者意思几乎相同，但 like to do 偶尔暗示"觉得应该做"，like doing 暗示"享受过程"。

- **movie** — 电影（美式）。英式更常用 film。搭配：watch a movie, go to the movies（去看电影，美式）、movie theater（电影院）。

- **music / song** — music 是不可数的（some music, listen to music），song 是可数的（a song, many songs）。music 是"音乐"的总称，song 是"一首歌"。搭配：listen to music, sing a song, play music。

- **tv** — television 的缩写。搭配：watch TV, on TV（在电视上）。注意通常不加 the：✗ watch the TV → ✓ watch TV（除非特指那台电视机：Turn off the TV!）。

- **time** — 时间。搭配：What time is it? / have time（有时间）、free time（空闲时间）、time to go（该走了）。注意 time 也可以表示"次数"：three times（三次）。
