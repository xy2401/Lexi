# Unit 095: 欧洲

> 讲述在欧洲的旅行经历

## 单词

Berlin, Germany, adventure, all about, all over, anywhere, capital, center, climb, crazy about, delicious, ever, fan, flown, helicopter, high, huge, italy, liked, met, next time, pasta, photograph, pilot, sausage, seen, since then, spain, taken, tried, try

## 单元讲解

本单元围绕"讲述在欧洲的旅行经历"展开。核心是现在完成时的经历表达：Have you ever been to Italy?（你去过意大利吗？）、I've flown in a helicopter!（我坐过直升机！）、I've tried pasta in Spain.（我在西班牙尝过意面。）、I've seen it all over Europe.（我在欧洲到处都见过。）。

词表里有欧洲地名（Berlin, Germany, Italy, Spain）和食物（pasta, sausage）。你可以练习：Berlin is the capital of Germany. / I'm crazy about Italian pasta. / I've been all over Europe. / Next time, I'll try the sausage. / I met a pilot there.

ever 用于疑问句（曾经）。since then 表示"从那时起"。

## Tips

- **ever / have you ever** — 曾经（用于疑问句）。搭配：Have you ever been to...? / Have you ever tried...? 例句：Have you ever been to Germany? / Have you ever flown in a helicopter? 注意 ever 用于疑问句，肯定句用 never / already。

- **flown / fly** — 飞过。fly → flew → flown。搭配：have flown, flown to Berlin, flown in a helicopter。例句：I've flown to Italy three times. / She has never flown before. 注意 flown 是过去分词（用于完成时）。

- **seen / see** — 见过。see → saw → seen。搭配：have seen, seen it all, I've seen that。例句：I've seen beautiful places all over Europe. / Have you seen Berlin? 注意 seen 是过去分词。

- **taken / try / tried** — 拍过 / 尝试。take → taken, try → tried。搭配：taken a photograph, tried pasta, tried the sausage。例句：I've taken lots of photographs. / I've tried German sausage. 注意 try 的过去分词是 tried。

- **since then** — 从那时起。搭配：since then, ever since then。例句：I went to Italy in 2020, and since then I've been crazy about pasta. 注意 since then 搭配现在完成时。

- **crazy about** — 对……着迷。搭配：crazy about pasta, crazy about travel, go crazy about。例句：I'm crazy about Italian food! / She's crazy about photography. 注意 crazy about = love... very much（口语）。

- **capital / center** — 首都 / 中心。搭配：the capital of Germany, in the center, city center。例句：Berlin is the capital of Germany. / The hotel is in the center. 注意 capital 前加 the。

- **all over / anywhere** — 到处 / 任何地方。搭配：all over Europe, anywhere in the world, travel all over。例句：I've been all over Europe. / You can go anywhere. 注意 all over = everywhere。

- **pasta / sausage** — 意面 / 香肠。搭配：Italian pasta, German sausage, delicious pasta。例句：I've tried pasta in Italy. / The German sausage is delicious. 注意 pasta 不可数。

- **helicopter / pilot** — 直升机 / 飞行员。搭配：fly in a helicopter, a helicopter ride, the pilot。例句：I've flown in a helicopter! / The pilot was very skilled. 注意 helicopter 的发音：/ˈhelɪkɑːptər/。

- **adventure / fan** — 冒险 / 粉丝。搭配：an adventure, a big adventure, a fan of, a big fan。例句：Traveling is an adventure! / I'm a big fan of Italian food. 注意 fan 是 fanatic 的缩写。

- **next time** — 下次。搭配：next time, Next time, I'll... / See you next time! 例句：Next time, I'll try the sausage. / Next time, we'll go to Spain. 注意 next time 前不加介词。
