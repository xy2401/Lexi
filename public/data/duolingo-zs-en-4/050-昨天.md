# Unit 050: 昨天

> 使用动词的一般过去时

## 单词

cloudy, course, dark, fun, great, hard, last, last night, late, movie theater, night, out, rainy, there, warmer, was, was not, were, were not, where, who, why, yes, yesterday

## 单元讲解

本单元围绕"使用动词的一般过去时"展开，这是一个重大语法节点。核心是 was / were（be 动词的过去式）：I was at home. She was happy. They were at the movie theater. 搭配 yesterday 和 last night 这些过去时间词，可以练习：Where were you last night? I was at the movie theater. It was rainy yesterday.

was 用于 I / he / she / it，were 用于 you / we / they。否定：was not / were not。疑问：Was she happy? / Were you at home?

天气词（cloudy, rainy, dark, warmer）继续出现，但用过去时描述：It was cloudy yesterday. It was warmer last week. great 和 fun 描述过去的体验：It was great! It was fun!

## Tips

- **was / were** — be 动词的过去式。was 用于 I / he / she / it：I was tired. She was happy. were 用于 you / we / they：You were late. They were at home. 注意 be 动词过去时没有人称一致问题（was/were 两种形式覆盖所有人称）。

- **was not / were not** — 过去时的否定。缩写：wasn't / weren't。搭配：I wasn't at home. / They weren't happy. 注意疑问句中将 was/were 提前：Was she at the party? / Were you late?

- **yesterday** — 昨天。搭配：yesterday morning, yesterday afternoon, the day before yesterday（前天）。注意 yesterday 前不加介词：✗ on yesterday → ✓ yesterday。是一般过去时的标志词。

- **last night** — 昨晚。搭配：I was at home last night. / What did you do last night? 注意 last 也可以搭配其他时间：last week, last month, last year。last 前不加介词。

- **great** — 很棒的、伟大的。搭配：It was great! / a great movie, a great day。比 good 程度更强。注意 great 和 good 的比较级都是 better，最高级都是 best。

- **cloudy / rainy** — 多云的 / 下雨的。搭配：It was cloudy yesterday. / It was rainy last night. 构词：cloud + -y, rain + -y。类似的：sunny, windy, snowy, foggy。

- **dark** — 黑暗的、深色的。搭配：It was dark. / dark hair, dark blue。注意 dark 也可以形容颜色深：dark green（深绿色）。反义词 light（浅色的/明亮的）。

- **warmer** — 更暖和。warm 的比较级：It was warmer yesterday than today. 搭配：warmer than, get warmer（变暖）。注意 warm 的比较级直接加 -r。

- **movie theater** — 电影院（美式）。搭配：go to the movie theater, at the movie theater。英式用 cinema。注意 theater 也可以指剧院（看戏的地方），movie theater 特指电影院。

- **out** — 外面、出去。搭配：go out, eat out（出去吃）、out of（从……出来）。注意 out 通常不单独作表语：✗ I am out → ✓ I am outside / I am out of the house（但口语中 I'm out 也可以表示"我出去了/我不在"）。

- **course** — 当然（of course）、课程。搭配：of course!（当然！）、a course（一门课）。注意 of course 是一个固定短语，表示"当然"。course 单独使用通常指"课程"或"一道菜"。

- **who / where / why** — 疑问词。在过去时中：Who was there? / Where were you? / Why was she late? 结构：疑问词 + was/were + 主语。注意过去时的疑问句不需要 did（be 动词直接提前）。
