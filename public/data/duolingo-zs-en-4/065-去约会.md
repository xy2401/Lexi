# Unit 065: 去约会

> 安排与人见面约会

## 单词

another, answer, anything, bar, bill, calendar, chat, coming, end, excellent, free, full, fun, glad, go to the movies, half of, how, how about, ice, iced, midnight, noon, ok, present, present for, share, single, something, terrible, thank you for, that's all right

## 单元讲解

本单元围绕"安排与人见面约会"展开。核心句型包括：How about going to the movies?（去看电影怎么样？）、Are you free tonight?（你今晚有空吗？）、Thank you for the present!（谢谢你的礼物！）。

词表里有约会相关词汇（bar, bill, calendar, chat, present）和时间词（midnight, noon）。你可以练习：Let's go to the movies. / I'm free after noon. / The bar is full. / Let's share the bill. / That's all right.

how about 用于提建议：How about a drink? / How about going out? single 表示"单身的"。glad 表示"高兴的"。

## Tips

- **how about** — ……怎么样？（提建议）。搭配：How about dinner? / How about going to the movies? 注意 how about 后接名词或动名词：How about a chat? / How about meeting at noon? 同义：What about...?

- **free** — 有空的、免费的。搭配：Are you free tonight? / I'm free after noon / for free（免费）。注意 free 的两个意思都很常用。I'm free = 我有空。free of charge = 免费。

- **go to the movies** — 去看电影。搭配：go to the movies, at the movies。注意美式用 go to the movies，英式用 go to the cinema。movies 总是复数形式。

- **midnight / noon** — 午夜 / 中午。搭配：at midnight, at noon, before midnight。例句：Let's meet at noon. / The bar closes at midnight. 注意都用 at：at noon, at midnight。

- **bill** — 账单。搭配：the bill, pay the bill, split the bill（AA制）。例句：Can I have the bill, please? / Let's share the bill. 注意美式也用 check：Can I get the check?

- **present / present for** — 礼物。搭配：a present for you, give a present, birthday present。注意 present 和 gift 同义，但 present 更口语。present for + 人：a present for my girlfriend。

- **thank you for** — 谢谢你……。搭配：Thank you for the present! / Thank you for coming. / Thank you for your help. 注意 for 后接名词或动名词：Thank you for helping me.

- **that's all right** — 没关系、不客气。搭配：That's all right. / It's all right. 注意这是回答道歉或感谢的用语：— Sorry! — That's all right. / — Thank you! — That's all right.

- **single** — 单身的、单人的。搭配：Are you single? / a single room, a single ticket。注意 single 的反义词：in a relationship / married。single room = 单人间。

- **glad / excellent / terrible** — 高兴的 / 极好的 / 糟糕的。搭配：I'm glad! / excellent idea, terrible weather。注意 glad 比 happy 更短暂（通常对某事感到高兴）：I'm glad you're coming.

- **share** — 分享、分担。搭配：share the bill, share a table, share with。例句：Let's share the bill. / We shared a pizza. 注意 share 后接事物：share something (with someone)。

- **full** — 满的、饱的。搭配：The bar is full. / I'm full.（我吃饱了。）/ a full calendar。注意 full 的反义词是 empty（空的）。I'm full = 我吃饱了（口语）。
